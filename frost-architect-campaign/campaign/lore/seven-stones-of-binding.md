# The Seven Stones of Binding
## Ancient Artifacts of Containment

### Overview
The Seven Stones of Binding are powerful magical artifacts created through the combined efforts of the Yeti elders and dwarven craftsmen during the Time of Long Winter. These stones were specifically designed to imprison the Frost Architect, an ancient entity of primordial cold who threatened to plunge the world into eternal winter.

Each stone embodies a different aspect of the binding ritual and possesses unique properties. When brought together in the proper formation, they create a powerful magical prison capable of containing even the most powerful elemental entities.

### The Stones
The Seven Stones of Binding consist of:

#### 1. The Stone of Clarity
- **Appearance**: A multifaceted crystal that constantly shifts and changes, reflecting light in impossible ways
- **Location**: The Caverns of Echo
- **Powers**: 
  - Reveals truth amid illusion (advantage on saves against illusion magic)
  - Once per day: Cast See Invisibility spell
  - Reveals the true nature of the Frost Architect's servants
  - Provides visions of where other Stones might be found
- **Role in Binding**: Ensures the ritual targets the true essence of the entity being bound rather than a decoy or projection

#### 2. The Stone of Resilience
- **Appearance**: A dodecahedron of shifting metal that flows like quicksilver while maintaining its geometric form
- **Location**: Frosthold (abandoned dwarven fortress)
- **Powers**: 
  - Grants resistance to one damage type of bearer's choice (changeable after a long rest)
  - Once per day: Cast Stoneskin spell
  - When combined with the Stone of Clarity: Can create a protective barrier against cold energy
- **Role in Binding**: Strengthens the prison against the bound entity's attempts to break free

#### 3. The Stone of Harmony
- **Appearance**: A perfect sphere of crystal that hums with gentle musical tones when touched
- **Location**: The Glacier Temple
- **Powers**: 
  - Creates zones of balance where extremes (including temperature) are moderated
  - Once per day: Cast Zone of Truth spell
  - When combined with other Stones: Extends their effects to cover a wider area
- **Role in Binding**: Stabilizes the prison, preventing external forces from disrupting it

#### 4. The Stone of Power
- **Appearance**: A cubic stone with glowing runes that orbit around it like tiny satellites
- **Location**: The Forbidden Peak summit
- **Powers**: 
  - Enhances the magical abilities of its bearer (spell save DC +1)
  - Stores magical energy that can be released in times of need (3 charges, regain 1d3 daily)
  - When combined with other Stones: Amplifies their magical effects
- **Role in Binding**: Provides the magical energy needed to maintain the prison over centuries

#### 5. The Stone of Wisdom
- **Appearance**: An irregularly shaped crystal that seems to contain a swirling galaxy of tiny lights
- **Location**: Ancient Yeti burial grounds
- **Powers**: 
  - Grants insights into complex problems (advantage on Intelligence checks)
  - Once per day: Cast Divination spell
  - When combined with other Stones: Provides visions of how they should be used
- **Role in Binding**: Ensures the ritual adapts to countermeasures attempted by the bound entity

#### 6. The Stone of Balance
- **Appearance**: A flat, circular stone divided into light and dark halves that slowly rotate
- **Location**: The Frozen Waterfall
- **Powers**: 
  - Protects against extreme elements (resistance to fire and cold damage)
  - Once per day: Cast Protection from Energy spell
  - When combined with other Stones: Prevents natural disasters caused by magical imbalance
- **Role in Binding**: Manages the opposing forces used in the binding ritual, preventing backlash

#### 7. The Stone of Binding
- **Appearance**: A pyramidal crystal that occasionally shows glimpses of what appears to be a prison of ice
- **Location**: The Chamber of Binding (deep within the Forbidden Peaks)
- **Powers**: 
  - Can temporarily bind lesser creatures (as the Hold Monster spell, 3/day)
  - Creates barriers that even ethereal creatures cannot pass
  - When combined with all other Stones: Activates the complete binding ritual
- **Role in Binding**: The central focus that channels the powers of all other stones into the actual imprisonment

### The Binding Ritual
When all seven stones are gathered and placed in the proper formation (a seven-pointed star around a central circle), they can be used to renew the prison holding the Frost Architect. The ritual requires:

1. The stones to be placed in the correct orientation
2. A specific chant in an ancient language (known to Dreamspeaker)
3. Representatives of both Yeti and dwarven peoples to channel the magic
4. A significant amount of magical energy from the participants

The original binding cost many lives as participants sacrificed their life force to empower the ritual. The current generation hopes to find a less costly method of powering the renewed binding.

### DM Notes

- The stones should be recovered one by one throughout the campaign
- Each stone gives the party new abilities that will help them face increasingly difficult challenges
- As more stones are gathered, the Frost Architect becomes more aware of the party and sends stronger servants against them
- The binding ritual itself can be the climax of the campaign
- Consider creating moral dilemmas around the potential cost of the ritual and whether imprisonment is the only solution
