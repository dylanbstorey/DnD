### Background: Herbalist Turned Wild Mage
Madrigal once lived a peaceful life, studying and gathering the healing plants of the forest. Everything changed during a spring ritual to bless the year's harvest - as she completed the ceremonial blessing, unexpected wild magic surged through her body, infusing her with chaotic natural energy. Plants around her grew rapidly, withered, and transformed uncontrollably. Since that day, she has learned to harness this unpredictable power, turning her intimate knowledge of growing things toward more destructive ends when necessary.

#### Feature: Natural Channeler
Your connection to wild magic is particularly strong around natural flora. When casting spells in forests, gardens, or areas with significant plant life, you can reroll a single damage die, though you must accept the second result even if it's lower.

### Personality
Madrigal carries her former gentleness and whimsy, but it's now paired with bursts of fiery passion and unpredictability. Like a forest that both nurtures and destroys, she embodies the duality of nature - capable of delicate growth and devastating fury. She remains deeply connected to the natural world, but now understands its destructive cycles as intimately as its nurturing ones.

#### Personality Traits
- "I still speak to plants, but now they sometimes scream back warnings or encouragement."
- "I'm prone to emotional outbursts that cause minor magical effects like flowers suddenly blooming or wilting."

#### Ideals
- "Wild Balance: Nature is not just gentle growth, but also necessary destruction."
- "Uncontained Power: Magic, like nature, should not be overly controlled or it loses its true essence."

#### Bonds
- "I seek to understand the wild magic that flows through me and its connection to the ancient forests."
- "Those who destroy nature will face its unleashed fury through me."

#### Flaws
- "My unpredictable magic sometimes causes more destruction than I intended."
- "I struggle to control my emotions, which frequently trigger minor magical effects."

### Appearance
Madrigal remains a slender wood elf with copper skin, but her silver-streaked auburn hair now seems alive - subtly moving on its own, with tiny flowers that bloom and wilt with her changing emotions. Her once moss green eyes now flicker with arcane energy, sometimes shifting to vibrant purple during spellcasting. Small vines appear to be tattooed on her skin, but close observers notice they actually grow and move slightly.

Her robes blend traditional elven designs with chaos - patterns that shift and change, colors that subtly flow from green to copper and back. When she channels her wild magic, leaves and flower petals materialize around her, swirling in arcane winds before dissipating.

### Roleplaying Notes
- Madrigal struggles with her dual nature - the calm herbalist she once was and the chaos-touched sorcerer she's becoming
- She still has moments of distraction, but now they're accompanied by minor magical effects
- Despite her destructive capabilities, she uses violence as a last resort - though her definition of "last resort" has become more flexible
- She remains genuinely warm and caring, but now possesses a fierce protective streak
- Her cryptic plant metaphors have taken on new meaning, often hinting at deeper magical truths
