## CLASS FEATURES

### Sorcerer Core
- **Spellcasting:** Charisma-based spellcasting (Spell Save DC 13, Spell Attack +5)
- **Sorcerous Origin:** Wild Magic

### Wild Magic Features
- **Wild Magic Surge:** When you cast a sorcerer spell of 1st level or higher, the DM can have you roll a d20. On a 1, roll on the Wild Magic Surge table.
- **Tides of Chaos:** Gain advantage on one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw. Regain use after a long rest OR when DM has you roll on the Wild Magic Surge table.

### Font of Magic (Level 2)
- **Sorcery Points:** 3 (regain all on long rest)
- **Flexible Casting:** 
  - Create spell slot: 2 points = 1st level slot, 3 points = 2nd level slot
  - Convert slot to points: Slot level = points gained

### Metamagic (Level 3)
Choose 2 options:
- **Subtle Spell (1 point):** Cast without verbal or somatic components
- **Twinned Spell (spell level points):** Target a second creature with a single-target spell

## BACKGROUND: HERBALIST TURNED WILD MAGE

Madrigal once lived a peaceful life studying and gathering the healing plants of the forest. Everything changed during a spring ritual to bless the year's harvest—as she completed the ceremonial blessing, unexpected wild magic surged through her body, infusing her with chaotic natural energy. Plants around her grew rapidly, withered, and transformed uncontrollably.

Since that day, she has learned to harness this unpredictable power, turning her intimate knowledge of growing things toward more destructive ends when necessary.

### Feature: Natural Channeler
Your connection to wild magic is particularly strong around natural flora. When casting spells in forests, gardens, or areas with significant plant life, you can reroll a single damage die (must accept the second result).

## APPEARANCE

Madrigal is a slender wood elf with copper skin. Her silver-streaked auburn hair seems alive—subtly moving on its own, with tiny flowers that bloom and wilt with her changing emotions. Her once moss-green eyes now flicker with arcane energy, sometimes shifting to vibrant purple during spellcasting.

Small vines appear tattooed on her skin, but close observers notice they actually grow and move slightly. Her robes blend traditional elven designs with chaos—patterns that shift and change, colors that flow from green to copper and back.

When she channels wild magic, leaves and flower petals materialize around her, swirling in arcane winds before dissipating.

## ROLEPLAYING NOTES

### Character Arc
- Struggles with dual nature: the calm herbalist she was vs. the chaos-touched sorcerer she's becoming
- Moments of distraction now accompanied by minor magical effects
- Despite destructive capabilities, prefers violence as last resort (though that definition grows more flexible)
- Genuinely warm and caring, but now possesses a fierce protective streak

### Combat Style
- Opens with control (Entangle) or defense (Mirror Image)
- Uses Thorn Whip to reposition enemies
- Thunderwave for grouped enemies or emergencies
- Chaos Bolt when feeling lucky
- Tides of Chaos for crucial moments—embraces the risk of Wild Surge

### Relationships
- **Matrim:** Appreciates his steadfast nature; his oak shield reminds her of her forest home
- **Major:** Kindred spirits in their connection to nature; she often speaks to him as she would to plants
- **Cheese:** His chaos is external where hers is internal; finds his confidence both amusing and admirable

### The Wild Magic
Embrace the chaos. When Wild Magic Surge triggers:
- Describe how the magic feels different, uncontrolled
- Minor visual effects (flowers, leaves, color shifts) even when the surge is beneficial
- The Staff of Thorns' Wild Conduit reroll should be used dramatically
