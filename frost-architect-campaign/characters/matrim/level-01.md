### Background: Soldier
Matrim served as a guard for dwarven merchant caravans, where he learned to rely on his quick thinking and unconventional shield when they were ambushed far from the mountain holds. His use of wood rather than traditional dwarven metals earned him both mockery and grudging respect.

#### Feature: Military Rank
You have a military rank from your career as a soldier. Soldiers loyal to your former military organization still recognize your authority and influence, and they defer to you if they are of a lower rank.

### Personality
Matrim is a young dwarf finding his place in the world. Unlike many of his kin who scoff at using wood for protection, he has embraced his unusual fighting style. He's practical, determined, and slightly self-conscious about bucking dwarven traditions.

#### Personality Traits
- "I face problems head-on. A simple, direct solution is the best."
- "I'm always polishing and maintaining my oaken shield, treating it with the same respect others show metal."

#### Ideals
- "Resourcefulness: The best warrior uses what's at hand, not what tradition dictates."
- "Protection: I stand between danger and those who cannot defend themselves."

#### Bonds
- "I carry a small carving from the same oak that my shield was crafted from."
- "I seek to prove that innovation has a place in dwarven martial traditions."

#### Flaws
- "I sometimes feel I need to prove myself because of my unconventional methods."
- "I can be stubborn and slow to change my mind once it's made up."

### Appearance
Matrim stands 4'5" tall with a solid, muscular build typical of mountain dwarves. His dark brown hair is worn in simple braids, and his beard, while still relatively short by dwarven standards (befitting his youth), is neatly groomed with a few simple clasps.

His most distinctive feature is his shield—an actual cross-section of an oak trunk, sanded smooth on the inner side, with the outer bark preserved and reinforced with iron bands. The natural grain and rings of the wood are visible, and small runes have been burned into the edge for protection. The shield is attached to his arm with sturdy leather straps, allowing him to use it effectively in combat.

He typically wears chain mail over simple, hard-wearing clothes, with leather boots reinforced with metal plates. Around his neck hangs a small wooden pendant carved from the same oak as his shield.

### Roleplaying Notes
- Matrim is proud of his unusual shield and fighting style, but sensitive to mockery about it
- He respects tradition but believes in practical innovation
- He's still learning about the world outside dwarven society
- He has a dry sense of humor typical of dwarves
- Though young, he has a natural protective instinct toward others
- He tends to be quiet and observant until he has something important to say
