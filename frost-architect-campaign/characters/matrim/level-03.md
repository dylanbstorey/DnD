## CUSTOM ABILITIES
- **Frost Rage (2/long rest):** As a bonus action, enter a frost-fueled rage for 1 minute
  - Advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws
  - +2 bonus to damage rolls with Strength-based melee attacks
  - Resistance to cold damage
  - At the start of each turn while raging, roll on the Frost Surge table

### Frost Surge Table (d8)
1. **Frost Armor:** Ice crystals form over your skin, granting +2 AC until the start of your next turn.
2. **Chilling Strike:** Your next weapon attack deals an additional 1d6 cold damage.
3. **Winter's Breath:** You exhale a 15-foot cone of freezing air. Creatures in the area must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or take 2d6 cold damage.
4. **Numbing Aura:** Until the start of your next turn, enemies that start their turn within 5 feet of you have their speed reduced by 10 feet.
5. **Frozen Ground:** The ground in a 10-foot radius around you becomes difficult terrain for enemies until the start of your next turn.
6. **Icy Weapon:** Your weapon becomes encased in magical ice, extending its reach by 5 feet until the start of your next turn.
7. **Frost Shield:** Your shield emanates cold energy, pushing attackers away. The next creature that hits you with a melee attack is pushed 10 feet away.
8. **Winter's Heart:** You gain 1d10 temporary hit points.

## BACKGROUND: SOLDIER

Matrim served as a guard for dwarven merchant caravans, where he learned to rely on his quick thinking and unconventional shield when they were ambushed far from the mountain holds. His use of wood rather than traditional dwarven metals earned him both mockery and grudging respect.

### Feature: Military Rank
You have a military rank from your career as a soldier. Soldiers loyal to your former military organization still recognize your authority and influence, and they defer to you if they are of a lower rank.

## APPEARANCE & PERSONALITY

Matrim stands 4'5" tall with a solid, muscular build typical of mountain dwarves. His dark brown hair is worn in simple braids, and his beard, while still relatively short by dwarven standards (befitting his youth), is neatly groomed with a few simple clasps.

Since the encounter with the Frost Colossus, those who look closely might notice faint blue patterns that occasionally appear on his skin when he's angry or in danger, resembling frost patterns on a window. These patterns glow subtly when he calls upon his Frost Rage.

His most distinctive feature is his shield—an actual cross-section of an oak trunk, sanded smooth on the inner side, with the outer bark preserved and reinforced with iron bands. The natural grain and rings of the wood are visible, and small runes have been burned into the edge for protection. The shield is attached to his arm with sturdy leather straps, allowing him to use it effectively in combat.

He typically wears chain mail over simple, hard-wearing clothes, with leather boots reinforced with metal plates. Around his neck hangs a small wooden pendant carved from the same oak as his shield.
