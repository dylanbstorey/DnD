# Major the Mending Hound
## Loyal Healing Companion NPC

![Character Portrait](A brown and white hound dog with glowing blue eyes, wearing an enchanted collar with healing crystals and a magical sweater made of white yeti fur that shimmers with restorative energy)

### Basic Information
- **Species**: Brown and white magical hound dog
- **Role**: Healer, guide, and companion
- **Alignment**: Lawful Good
- **Personality**: Caring but pragmatic healer with a playful bedside manner
- **Special Ability**: Can speak to both Yetis and humans

- **Keen Senses**: Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell or hearing
- **Beast Speech**: Can communicate with both humans and Yetis through a special form of speech
- **Four-Legged**: Advantage on ability checks and saving throws against being knocked prone
- **Loyal Companion**: Naturally attunes to allies, strengthening healing magic directed at them

### Special Abilities

#### Truespeech
Major can communicate fluently with any intelligent creature that has a language. This is not true telepathy, but an unusual form of speech that somehow makes itself understood.

#### Healing Lick (3/day)
As an action, Major can lick a wounded creature, healing 2d8 + 4 hit points. When he uses this ability, his tongue glows with a soft blue light. The healing sensation is accompanied by a feeling of warmth and comfort, though some find the experience a bit... slobbery.

#### Pack Mending (1/day)
Major howls a soothing melody that resonates with healing energy. All allies within 30 feet regain 1d6 + 4 hit points and have one level of exhaustion removed. The howl creates visible ripples of blue-white energy that flow toward wounded creatures.

#### Scent Ailments
Major can smell diseases, poisons, and curses affecting creatures. By carefully sniffing a creature as an action, he can identify what ails them and gain advantage on any ability checks to treat the condition.

#### Warming Presence
Major's yeti fur sweater radiates gentle warmth. Allies within 5 feet of Major gain resistance to cold damage and advantage on saving throws against disease.

### Equipment
- **Magical Healing Collar**: A collar adorned with small healing crystals that amplify his natural abilities
  - Allows him to cast Lesser Restoration once per day
  - Grants +2 to Medicine checks
  - The crystals glow softly when near wounded creatures
- **Yeti Fur Sweater**: A carefully crafted sweater made from shed yeti fur, imbued with protective magic
  - Provides cold resistance
  - Adds +2 to AC
  - Has pockets containing medicinal herbs and bandages
- **Healing Herb Pouches**: Small pouches attached to his collar containing magical herbs
  - Can be used to create healing poultices
  - When mixed with his saliva, create enhanced healing effects
- **Frost Whisper Pendant**: A small crystal pendant shaped like a snowflake
  - Allows communication with Elder Snowheart who wears the twin pendant
  - Speak a message into it to be heard by the bearer of the matching pendant
  - Works at any distance

### Actions

#### Bite
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
*Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.

#### Therapeutic Nibble
Major gently nibbles on an ally's wound, stimulating natural healing. The target regains the use of one Hit Die without spending it and can immediately roll it to regain hit points.

#### Calming Presence
As an action, Major can emit a soothing aura that helps calm one panicked or frightened creature. The target can immediately make a new saving throw against any frightened condition with advantage.

#### Fetch Healing (1/day)
Major can magically locate and retrieve the nearest healing herb, potion, or medical supply within 100 feet, even if he doesn't know its location. This mysteriously works even in environments where such items shouldn't naturally exist.

### Healing Spells
Major can cast the following spells, using Wisdom as his spellcasting ability:

- **Cantrips (at will)**
  - Spare the Dying (delivered via gentle lick)
  - Guidance (delivered by nudging with his nose)
  - Mending (delivered by playfully pawing at the object)

- **1st Level (4 slots)**
  - Cure Wounds (delivered via healing lick)
  - Healing Word (delivered via sympathetic whine)
  - Purify Food and Drink (delivered by sniffing the item thoroughly)
  - Detect Poison and Disease (achieved through his enhanced sense of smell)

- **2nd Level (2 slots)**
  - Lesser Restoration (delivered via extended therapeutic licking)
  - Protection from Poison (achieved by extracting poison through gentle suction and spitting it out)

### Background
Major was born with an unusual connection to healing magic. The Yeti elders recognized his gift when he was just a pup and enhanced his natural abilities with special training and magical items. While many magical animals can communicate, Major's abilities go far beyond this, allowing him to channel healing energy through his unique canine methods.

His healing style is decidedly dog-like — enthusiastic licks, comforting presence, and occasionally bringing people exactly what they need without being asked. Despite the sometimes undignified delivery method, his healing magic is potent and has saved countless lives.

### Personality Traits
- **Nurturing**: Major can sense pain and instinctively wants to help, often checking on party members who try to hide injuries
- **Pragmatic**: Despite his caring nature, he understands that sometimes tough love is needed
- **Protective**: He positions himself near the most vulnerable members of the group
- **Playful Healer**: Maintains that healing should include joy, often incorporating play into his treatments
- **Occasionally Stubborn**: When he believes someone needs treatment, he can be insistent to the point of annoyance

### Roleplaying Tips
- Major's healing often involves physically connecting with patients - licking wounds, resting his head on injuries, or curling up beside the sick
- He sometimes brings people random objects he thinks will help them feel better (not always successfully)
- He gets visibly distressed when someone refuses necessary treatment
- When not actively healing, he maintains watchful vigilance, constantly sniffing the air for signs of danger or disease
- He has little concept of personal space and may administer "healing licks" without warning if he detects an injury
- Despite his sometimes silly methods, his medical knowledge is extensive and he should be taken seriously in health matters
