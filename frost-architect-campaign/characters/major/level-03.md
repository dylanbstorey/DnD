## RACIAL TRAITS

- **Keen Senses:** Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell or hearing
- **Truespeech:** Can communicate fluently with any intelligent creature that has a language
- **Four-Legged:** Advantage on ability checks and saving throws against being knocked prone

---

## CLASS FEATURES

- **Spellcasting:** Wisdom-based spellcasting (spell save DC 13, spell attack modifier +5)
- **Ritual Casting:** Can cast druid spells as rituals if they have the ritual tag
- **Wild Shape (2/Rest):** Transform into a beast of CR 1/4 or lower for up to 3 hours
- **Natural Recovery (Circle of the Land):** Recover spell slots during a short rest (once per day)
- **Circle Spells (Arctic):** Additional spells that don't count against prepared spells
  - 1st level: *hold person, spike growth*
  - 2nd level: *spike growth, sleet storm*

---

## SPECIAL ABILITIES

- **Pathfinder:** Advantage on Survival checks to navigate or track; can sense direction to nearest settlement
- **Loyal Companion:** Once per day, provide advantage to an ally within 5 feet on a saving throw or ability check
- **Warming Presence:** Allies within 5 feet gain resistance to cold damage
- **Pack Leader:** As a bonus action, direct an ally to use their reaction for an attack or movement

## COMBAT ACTIONS

### Standard Actions
- **Bite:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) cold damage with Frost Crystal Fang Caps
- **Help:** Give advantage to an ally's next ability check or attack roll
- **Dodge:** Gain advantage on Dexterity saving throws; attacks against you have disadvantage
- **Cast a Spell:** Cast any prepared spell following normal spellcasting rules
- **Wild Shape:** Transform into a beast with CR 1/4 or lower

### Bonus Actions
- **Healing Bark:** Cast Healing Word as a bonus action
- **Pack Leader:** Direct an ally to use their reaction for an attack or movement

### Reactions
- **Opportunity Attack:** Make a bite attack when an enemy leaves your reach

## ROLEPLAYING NOTES

- Major speaks directly and to the point, rarely wasting words
- He has a deep knowledge of mountain survival that he can share when asked
- While he can be sarcastic, he's never cruel and is genuinely concerned for the party's wellbeing
- He sometimes forgets human limitations and may suggest solutions that would be easy for a four-legged creature
- When facing danger, his playful side disappears completely, replaced by focused determination
- As a druid, he respects the natural order but understands the need to fight the unnatural cold brought by the Frost Architect
