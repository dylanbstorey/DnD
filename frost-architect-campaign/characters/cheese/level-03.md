## CLASS FEATURES

### Rogue Core
- **Sneak Attack (2d6):** Once per turn, deal extra damage to one creature you hit with an attack if you have advantage, OR if another enemy of the target is within 5 feet of it and you don't have disadvantage. Must use finesse or ranged weapon.
- **Thieves' Cant:** Secret language of rogues; hidden signs and code words.
- **Cunning Action:** Bonus action to Dash, Disengage, or Hide.
- **Expertise:** Double proficiency bonus for Acrobatics and Stealth.

### Swashbuckler (Level 3)
- **Fancy Footwork:** If you make a melee attack against a creature during your turn, that creature can't make opportunity attacks against you for the rest of that turn.
- **Rakish Audacity:** Add Charisma modifier (+2) to initiative rolls. You can also use Sneak Attack against a creature if you are within 5 feet of it, no other creature is within 5 feet of you, and you don't have disadvantage. (Enables dueling-style Sneak Attacks without needing advantage or allies nearby.)

## COMBAT ACTIONS

### Standard Actions
- **Attack:** Make a weapon attack (rapier, dagger, or claws)
- **Dash:** Double your movement for the turn
- **Disengage:** Your movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks
- **Dodge:** Attacks against you have disadvantage; advantage on DEX saves
- **Hide:** Make a Stealth check to become hidden
- **Help:** Give an ally advantage on their next check or attack

### Bonus Actions
- **Cunning Action:** Dash, Disengage, or Hide
- **Two-Weapon Fighting:** Attack with offhand dagger (no modifier to damage)

### Reactions
- **Opportunity Attack:** When an enemy leaves your reach (but remember Fancy Footwork protects you from theirs!)

## BACKGROUND: CRIMINAL (SPY VARIANT)

Cheese worked as a spy and infiltrator, though he tells people he was simply a thief. The truth is more complicated—and more dangerous.

### Criminal Contact
Cheese has a reliable contact in the criminal underworld who can provide information about local security, guard schedules, and potential entry points. This contact owes him nothing and expects payment, but the relationship is professional and trustworthy.

### The Real Story
Before joining the party, Cheese operated as a covert agent for the Yeti Council. His missions involved infiltrating human settlements to gather intelligence about the spreading cold, the movements of strange creatures, and any signs of the Frost Architect's influence.

His last mission went wrong. He was discovered—not as a spy, but as a smuggler. He made a choice in that moment: let them catch him for smuggling (imprisonment) rather than reveal his true purpose (execution). The Yeti Council, unable to extract him through official channels, sent Matrim and Major to break him out.

Cheese has never explained what he was actually doing, and he never will. The Council's secrets are not his to share.

## APPEARANCE & PERSONALITY

Cheese is a ginger and cream tabby Tabaxi, lean and wiry, standing about 5'6" when fully upright. His fur is well-groomed despite his circumstances—some habits die hard. His eyes are a striking amber-gold, and they're constantly moving, assessing exits, valuables, and threats in that order.

He wears his battered cavalier hat like a crown, the bedraggled feather somehow still jaunty. His clothing is practical but theatrical: a loose white shirt under a leather vest, soft boots, and his Warmthweave cloak draped dramatically over one shoulder. His rapier "Whisker" hangs at his hip in a worn scabbard.

His manner is all performance—the lazy confidence, the dramatic sighs, the theatrical bow before combat. But those who watch carefully notice that the act never slips, which suggests it's not entirely an act. The real Cheese exists somewhere underneath, but he's not sharing.

## ROLEPLAYING NOTES

### Voice & Mannerisms
- Speaks with exaggerated confidence and occasional dramatic pauses
- Refers to himself in third person when being particularly theatrical ("Cheese does not run. Cheese makes a tactical retreat.")
- Purrs when pleased; ears flatten when lying
- Tail movements betray emotions he's trying to hide

### Combat Style
- Opens with positioning—uses Feline Agility to get into perfect striking position
- Prefers one-on-one duels where Rakish Audacity shines
- Uses Fancy Footwork to strike and withdraw, never staying in one place
- Will absolutely monologue mid-fight if given the opportunity

### Relationships
- **Matrim:** Respects the dwarf's straightforward nature. Finds the oak shield endearing. Will never admit he was grateful for the rescue.
- **Major:** Professional respect between two Council operatives. They understand each other without needing to discuss it.
- **Madrigal:** Fascinated by her chaos magic—it's the opposite of his precision. Finds her unpredictability both alarming and refreshing.
- **The Yeti Council:** Absolute, unquestioning loyalty. If the Council says jump, Cheese is already in the air.

### What He Won't Talk About
- What his mission actually was
- How he came to work for the Yeti Council
- What the Council Token means
- Why he was really in prison
- Anything about his life before the mountains

### What He Will Talk About (Endlessly)
- His daring escapes (embellished)
- His skill with a blade (accurate)
- The inadequacy of prison security (detailed)
- Philosophy of locks ("A lock is just a puzzle that wants to be solved")
- The quality of local cheese (hence the name—or so he claims)
