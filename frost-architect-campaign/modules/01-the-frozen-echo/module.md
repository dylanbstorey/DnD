# The Frozen Echo: A Winter Adventure

## Introduction

*In the endless winter of the Frostpeak Mountains, where snow drifts pile higher than houses and the wind carries whispers of ancient magic, the Yeti clans have lived in harmony with the elements for countless generations. These gentle giants, misunderstood by outsiders as fearsome beasts, have built a society based on mutual support, communal living, and respect for the harsh but beautiful wilderness they call home.*

*Recently, however, something has disturbed this peaceful existence. The settlement of Snowhaven, a small but thriving Yeti community nestled in a protected valley, has fallen silent. No messengers have arrived with their monthly reports, and the mind-bond that connects all Yeti communities has grown faint. The Council of Elders has grown worried, and in their wisdom, they have called upon outsiders for help—adventurers whose smaller size and diverse skills might solve this mystery where Yeti emissaries might fail.*

## Scene 1: The Council Chamber

The adventure begins as our heroes find themselves standing in an enormous ice cave, their breath forming clouds in the frigid air. The ceiling arches high above, at least thirty feet tall, with crystalline ice formations catching the light of dozens of softly glowing blue lanterns. The walls of the cave are smooth, polished ice that reflects the lanterns' glow, creating an ethereal atmosphere of blue and white light.

At the center of this massive chamber sits a semicircular table carved from a single massive block of transparent ice. Behind this table sit three enormous figures—the Council of Yetis.

The Yetis are impressive figures, standing nearly twelve feet tall even when seated. Their thick white fur has subtle patterns—swirls and geometric shapes—that denote their clan and status. Despite their imposing size, their faces hold expressions of kindness and concern. Their eyes, dark and intelligent, study the adventurers carefully.

The central Yeti, whose fur has silvered with age, speaks first. His voice is surprisingly gentle for such a large creature:

"Welcome, small friends. I am Elder Frostmantle. These are my fellow council members—" he gestures to his left, "Elder Snowheart," then to his right, "and Elder Iceclaw. We have summoned you because we face a worry that keeps us from peaceful sleep."

Elder Snowheart, a female Yeti with intricate crystal beads woven into her fur, leans forward slightly. "The settlement of Snowhaven lies three days' journey to the north. It is home to seventeen of our kin, including five younglings. We have not heard from them in over a month."

Elder Iceclaw, whose right hand bears a natural formation of ice-like claws, adds, "Our kind can sense one another across distances, a bond of mind and spirit. But the bond to Snowhaven has grown... faint. Not broken, but muffled, like a voice calling through a snowstorm."

Elder Frostmantle speaks again, his brow furrowed with concern. "We would send our own, but the paths to Snowhaven are narrow and treacherous for ones of our size. You smaller folk could travel more swiftly, and perhaps approach without alerting whatever might be causing this silence."

## Scene 2: The Quest and Gifts

Elder Snowheart rises from her seat and approaches a small ice chest on a pedestal near the council table. "Before we proceed with gifts, let me introduce one more member who will join your party," she says.

She gestures to the side entrance, where Elder Iceclaw is standing. He steps aside, revealing a medium brown and white hound dog with warm brown eyes. The dog, Major, trots into the chamber, his tail wagging slightly as he takes in the assembled yetis.

The elders greet him warmly, and Elder Snowheart explains, "Major has been a valuable ally to our people for many years. He knows the paths of the mountains better than any map could show, and is a loyal friend to those who venture here."

Elder Iceclaw adds, "As a token of our appreciation, we have gifted him with this sweater," he says, gesturing to Major's attire. "It was woven from our own shed fur and imbued with protective magic. It shields him from the cold and offers some protection against the dangers of the mountains."

Major inclines his head in a respectful bow, his eyes fixed on the adventurers.

"Now, let us proceed with the gifts," Elder Snowheart says, opening the ice chest. "If you agree to help us, we offer these tokens to aid your journey."

From the chest, she withdraws several items: First, a set of crystal pendants shaped like snowflakes. "The Frost Whispers," she explains. "Speak a message into one, and that message will be carried to its twin." She hands one to Major and keeps another, which she wears around her neck.

Next, thick fur cloaks. "The Warmthweave Cloaks. They resist the deepest cold and will keep their wearers comfortable even in blizzard conditions." She presents one to each adventurer.

Then, small glass vials filled with what looks like liquid starlight. "The Essence of Healing. Break these vials in times of greatest need, and their magic will mend wounds and restore vigor." She gives one to each adventurer.

Finally, she produces small crystal flasks containing shimmering red oil. "Ember Oil, harvested from the eternal flames that burn deep within the mountains. Apply it to your weapons, and they will burn with magical fire, effective against the creatures of ice and cold." She offers one flask to each adventurer.

Elder Frostmantle rises to his full height, his eyes piercing the shadows. "Will you accept this quest, friends from the lowlands? Will you help us learn what has befallen our kin at Snowhaven?"

## Scene 3: Journey to Snowhaven

*Assuming the adventurers accept the quest, the journey begins the following morning.*

As the party leaves the massive ice cave system that houses the Yeti settlement, they emerge onto a high mountain plateau. The world stretches out before them—endless white peaks, valleys filled with snow-laden pine forests, and a clear blue sky overhead. The air is crisp and cold, but not unbearable, especially with the magical protection of the Warmthweave Cloak.

Major takes the lead, his brown and white coat contrasting sharply with the snow, while his magical yeti fur sweater seems to glow softly in the winter light. His paws find purchase on the snow with practiced ease. "The journey takes three days for your kind," he explains. "First through the Crystal Pine Forest, then across the Frozen Lake, and finally through Whisperwind Pass. Each has its challenges, but nothing we can't handle if we're careful."

As they travel, Major proves to be an excellent guide—pragmatic and focused, but with occasional moments of dry humor.

"Watch your step there," he might say as someone slips slightly on an icy patch. "I'd offer to carry you, but I left my saddle in my other fur."

Or, seeing the adventurers struggle with the cold despite their magical protection: "You know, there are advantages to having a built-in fur coat. Though the shedding in spring is a nightmare."

By nightfall, Major helps the party find shelter in a small cave. As they settle in for the night, the dog sits near the entrance, his magical sweater radiating a gentle warmth that helps take the edge off the bitter cold. The adventurers notice that when darkness falls completely, the yeti fur sweater emits a very faint glow, just enough to outline Major's silhouette as he keeps watch.

"The Yetis are good folk," he says quietly. "Most humans don't understand them. They see the size, the strength, the claws—and assume monster. Never bothering to notice the careful way they build their homes to preserve the trees, or how they shepherd the mountain goats rather than hunt them to extinction. They're peaceful by nature, which makes this silence from Snowhaven all the more concerning."

## Scene 4: Arrival at Snowhaven

After three days of travel, the party crests a ridge and looks down into a small valley. Nestled among a cluster of pine trees is Snowhaven—a collection of large, dome-shaped structures built from packed snow, ice, and timber. Smoke should be rising from central smoke holes, but the settlement appears still. Too still.

Major's ears perk up, and he growls softly. "Something's not right. I can sense... presence. But not Yeti. Not entirely."

As the party approaches cautiously, they see signs of struggle—disturbed snow, broken tools, and a partial collapse in one of the dome structures.

Suddenly, from behind one of the buildings, three creatures emerge. Humanoid figures wrapped in tattered, ice-crusted rags with skeletal limbs and glowing blue eyes. Frost forms in the air around them, and their bony fingers end in icy claws.

"Winter wights!" Major barks in alarm. "Spirits of the frozen dead, drawn to places of emptiness and cold. They must have been attracted by the absence of the Yetis' warmth!"

## Scene 5: The Confrontation

The winter wights attack, moving with unnatural jerky motions. Despite their fearsome appearance, they're not particularly strong opponents—more dangerous in their unpredictability than their combat prowess. They attack with ice-encrusted claws and seem to radiate cold that chills to the bone.

After the party defeats these frozen spirits, Major sniffs at the dissipating frost where they fell. "The wights are drawn to places of abandonment. But something must have driven the Yetis away first, leaving this place vulnerable to these spirits."

Further exploration of the settlement reveals most buildings empty, with signs of hasty departure. In the central dome, however, the party finds a single adult Yeti, unconscious and partially encased in unusual blue ice. Unlike the natural ice used in Yeti construction, this ice has strange runes glowing within it.

## Scene 6: The Puzzle

Near the imprisoned Yeti is a curious device—a circular platform with nine large ice tiles arranged in a 3x3 grid. Each tile has a different symbol etched into it: sun, moon, star, mountain, tree, cloud, river, crystal, and snowflake. Around the edge of the platform are the same symbols arranged in a specific order.

Major examines it closely. "This looks like a Yeti healing apparatus. They use these to focus their natural magic. But someone has disrupted the pattern. I think we need to arrange the tiles to match the sequence around the edge."

The puzzle is straightforward: players need to slide the tiles (only one can be moved at a time into an empty space) until they match the pattern shown on the rim. The solution requires just a handful of moves, simple enough for a young child to solve with a bit of patience.

When solved, the platform glows with gentle blue light, and the unnatural ice encasing the Yeti begins to melt. As it does, the Yeti stirs to consciousness.

## Scene 7: Revelations

The awakened Yeti introduces herself as Mistwalker, the healer of Snowhaven. Still weak, she explains what happened:

"A strange storm came... not natural. Within it, something gleaming and metal fell from the sky into the high peaks beyond our settlement. After that, the air grew colder—a different kind of cold that drove away warmth and life. The settlement became uninhabitable, and my people fled deeper into the mountains. I stayed to maintain our healing shrine, but was attacked by winter spirits and trapped in ice."

Major translates for any party members who don't understand the Yeti language. "A metal object from the sky? That's... unusual."

Mistwalker continues: "The unnatural cold is coming from whatever fell from the sky. It's driving away all warmth and life, leaving only emptiness that attracts the winter wights. If you could reach it and somehow neutralize it, the natural balance might be restored. Our people could return home."

## Conclusion

With this new information, the party has a clear next step—locate and deal with the mysterious object that crashed in the mountains. But that would be the next module in the adventure.

For now, they can use the Frost Whisper pendant to contact the Council of Elders and report what they've found. Elder Snowheart's voice comes through the pendant, relieved to hear that some of the Snowhaven Yetis survived but concerned about the frost-touch corruption.

"You've done well, friends," her voice echoes from the crystal. "But a greater mystery now lies before us. Return to us with Mistwalker and any others you can find. We must discuss this strange object from the sky and what it might mean for all who dwell in the mountains."

Major looks up at the high peaks where the object supposedly crashed. "Well," he says with a doggy grin, "I hope you all enjoyed the walk here, because the next leg of our journey looks to be considerably more... vertical. But first, let's get Mistwalker back to safety."

*End of Module One: The Frozen Echo*
