# Personalized Magical Rewards - Session 2

After defeating the Frost Colossus and returning to the Yeti Council, these specific magical items are presented to the heroes in recognition of their service and to aid them in their quest to find the Seven Stones of Binding.

## For Matrim Oakenfury: Frostbite Blade

### Description
*This longsword features crystalline fragments from the Frost Colossus embedded along its blade, forming patterns reminiscent of snowflakes. The hilt is wrapped in silvery leather with geometric Dwarven runes of protection. When wielded, frost forms along the blade's edge and the runes glow with a subtle blue light. The magical fragments seem to resonate with Matrim's natural fighting style, enhancing rather than hindering his movements.*

### Mechanics
- Magical longsword that adds +1d4 cold damage to attacks
- Once per day: Can create a 5-foot radius of difficult terrain (icy ground) around a struck target
- **Frost Wall (1/day)**: As an action, Matrim can plant the blade into the ground to summon a wall of impenetrable frost. The wall is 20 feet high, 40 feet wide, and 1 foot thick. It appears in any configuration Matrim chooses, as long as it makes contact with the ground and is created on a solid surface. The wall lasts for 1 hour or until Matrim dismisses it as a bonus action. The wall blocks line of sight and physical passage. It has AC 5, 150 hit points, and is vulnerable to fire damage.

## For Madrigal the Garden Witch: Bloom-in-Frost Focus

### Description
*This intricate focus takes the form of a small crystalline flower that never fully blooms nor wilts. The petals are crafted from transparent fragments of the Frost Colossus, while the stem is made of polished wood infused with Yeti magics. Despite being formed from ice, it feels warm to Madrigal's touch. When used to cast spells, frost patterns spread briefly across the target before dissipating into tiny, glimmering flower petals.*

### Mechanics
- Magic focus that adds +1d4 cold damage to damaging spells
- Spells cast using this focus ignore resistance (but not immunity) to cold damage
- Once per day: Can cast Plant Growth in snow or ice, creating temporary plant-like sculptures that provide difficult terrain for enemies but not allies
- While holding the focus, Madrigal can cause small flowers to bloom in snow or ice (non-combat effect, purely aesthetic)

## For Major: Gorget of the Northern Howl

### Description
*This elegant collar fits perfectly around Major's neck, made of supple leather lined with soft Yeti fur on the inside. Crystalline fragments from the Frost Colossus are set into the leather in a pattern resembling mountains and stars. The gorget adjusts magically to never impede Major's movement or speech. When activated, frost forms around Major's muzzle before he unleashes his powerful howl.*

### Mechanics
- **Bark of the North Winds**: Once per day, Major can unleash a breath weapon in a 15-foot cone. Targets must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or take 4d6 cold damage and be pushed back 10 feet. On a successful save, targets take half damage and aren't pushed.
- Provides resistance to cold damage while worn
- Enhances Major's already impressive voice, allowing him to be heard clearly even through howling blizzards or over great distances
- Small magical reservoir: Once per day, Major can tap into the gorget's power to reroll a failed saving throw against any effect

## For All Party Members: Ice-Sight Goggles

### Description
*These goggles are crafted from treated ice crystals set in frames of polished bone. When worn, they allow the wearer to peer through snow and ice as if it were slightly cloudy glass. The magic within them responds to the wearer's thoughts, allowing focus on specific depths and areas.*

### Mechanics
- Can see through snow and ice up to 1 foot thick
- Advantage on Perception checks in snowy or icy environments
- Ability to detect magical cold sources within 60 feet
- Protection against gaze attacks and bright light reflected off snow

## Presentation Ceremony

**Elder Snowheart:** *Carefully presents the Frostbite Blade to Matrim*
"This blade was forged with fragments of the very construct you defeated. Our smiths observed your unique fighting style—a dwarf honoring wood alongside metal—and crafted this to complement your strengths. May it serve you as faithfully as you serve our cause."

**Elder Frostmantle:** *Hands the crystal flower focus to Madrigal with surprising gentleness for his massive paws*
"For the one who speaks to growing things, we offer this bridge between your magic and our realm of ice and snow. Our elders say that even in the Time of Long Winter, some plants found ways to bloom. Perhaps your magic, enhanced by this focus, will help life persist against the coming cold."

**Dreamspeaker:** *Approaches Major and carefully secures the gorget around his neck*
"For our brother who bridges words between worlds, this collar holds the voice of winter itself. Use it wisely, loyal one. Sometimes a warning bark can turn aside danger before teeth must be bared."

**Elder Iceclaw:** *Distributes the Ice-Sight Goggles to each party member*
"These will allow you to see through the Frost Architect's deceptions. In the Caverns of Echo, where nothing is quite as it seems, such sight may prove as valuable as any weapon."

*The magical items shimmer briefly in unison when first held by their intended recipients, as if recognizing and bonding with their new owners.*
