# Session 2: The Frost Colossus

## Session Overview
This session picks up immediately after the players have explored the strange metallic sphere and triggered its security system. Having discovered the sphere's purpose as part of a network to gather cold energy for the Frost Architect, the players now face the sphere's ultimate defender: the Frost Colossus. After this encounter, they'll return to the Yeti Council where they'll learn about the Seven Stones of Binding and begin their greater quest.

## Background for DM
The players completed their mission to investigate the sphere near Snowhaven but were unable to shut it down before triggering its security systems. They've learned that the sphere is part of a larger network designed to gather cold energy and strengthen the Frost Architect, an ancient entity of primordial cold who once ruled the mountain region.

The Frost Colossus is not merely a mindless guardian but a direct extension of the Frost Architect's power, capable of speech and tactical thinking. This encounter serves both as a combat challenge and a storytelling opportunity to reveal more about the Frost Architect's nature and plans.

After the battle, the party will experience a vision that provides crucial information about the Frost Architect's network of spheres and overall plan. Their return to the Yeti Council will reveal the next phase of the campaign—the quest to find the Seven Stones of Binding that were used to imprison the Frost Architect generations ago.

## Part 1: Recap & Setup (10 minutes)

### Brief Recap
Begin by reminding players of:
- Their discovery of the sphere's purpose (network to gather cold energy)
- The spectral image of the Frost Architect they observed
- Their decision to retreat with information rather than attempt to disable the sphere
- The security system activating ice constructs as they left

### Current Situation
As the players attempt to leave the valley:

**Narrative Description:**
*As you make your way out of the sphere and back toward the valley's edge, the ground beneath your feet begins to tremble. At first, it's subtle—a vibration you might mistake for your own exhaustion. But within moments, it intensifies into violent shaking that makes maintaining balance difficult.*

*A thunderous crack echoes across the valley as a nearby glacier splits open. The sound is deafening, like the mountain itself is breaking apart. The already frigid air plummets to an impossible cold—so severe that even with your magical protection, your breath freezes instantly before your face, falling to the ground as tiny crystals of ice.*

*Major growls a warning, his fur standing on end. "Something big is coming," he says, his usual calm demeanor replaced by tense vigilance. "Something connected directly to the Frost Architect himself."*

**Environmental Effects:**
- The ground trembles beneath their feet (DC 12 Dexterity check to maintain balance while running)
- Temperature drops dramatically (characters without cold protection must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or gain one level of exhaustion)

## Part 2: The Frost Colossus Battle (30-45 minutes)

### Dramatic Entrance

**Narrative Description:**
*From the shattered glacier emerges a massive figure of crystalline ice. Standing fifteen feet tall, its humanoid body is composed of densely packed magical ice with glowing blue nodules embedded throughout its torso, shoulders, and head. Where a face should be, only two piercing blue eyes glow with malevolent intelligence, burning like cold stars in the featureless expanse of its head.*

*Each footstep it takes spreads frost in a wide circle, freezing the ground solid beneath and around it. The very air seems to crystallize in its presence, forming beautiful but deadly ice formations that grow and spread with each passing second.*

*It raises its massive arms toward you, ice cracking and reforming as it moves, and speaks in a voice like glaciers calving—deep, rumbling, and filled with the promise of an endless winter:*

*"The Architect awakens... the Long Winter approaches... resistance is futile... surrender to the cold embrace..."*

### Battlefield Setup
The encounter takes place in the bowl-shaped valley containing the sphere:

- **Unstable Ice Formations:** 
  - 3-4 large ice formations (10 feet tall) that can be toppled (Athletics DC 15)
  - Deals 2d6 bludgeoning damage to creatures in a 10-foot line
  
- **Deep Snow Areas:**
  - Counts as difficult terrain
  - About 40% of the battlefield is covered in deep snow
  
- **Frozen Pond:**
  - 20-foot diameter frozen pond
  - Can be broken with sufficient force (AC 10, 15 HP)
  - Creates 15-foot radius of difficult terrain; creatures on it when broken must make DC 13 Dexterity save or fall prone
  
- **Ridge Overlooking the Valley:**
  - 20 feet high, requires Athletics DC 14 to climb
  - Provides high ground advantage on ranged attacks
  - Partial cover (+2 AC) against attacks from below

### Frost Colossus Stats

**Frost Colossus**
*Huge elemental, chaotic evil*

- **Armor Class:** 16 (natural armor)
- **Hit Points:** 120 (adjust as needed for party size/level)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 22 (+6) | 10 (+0) | 20 (+5) | 8 (-1) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) |

- **Saving Throws:** Str +9, Con +8
- **Damage Immunities:** Cold, poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Exhaustion, poisoned, petrified
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** Fire
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11
- **Languages:** Primordial, Common
- **Challenge Rating:** 5 (1,800 XP)

#### Special Abilities

- **Frost Aura:** All creatures within 10 feet take 2d6 cold damage at the start of the Colossus's turn and must make a DC 14 Constitution save or have their speed reduced by half until the start of their next turn.

- **Siege Monster:** The Colossus deals double damage to objects and structures.

- **Ice Walking:** The Colossus can move across and climb icy surfaces without needing to make an ability check. Additionally, difficult terrain composed of ice or snow doesn't cost it extra movement.

#### Actions

- **Multiattack:** The Frost Colossus makes two slam attacks.

- **Slam:** Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3d8+6 bludgeoning damage plus 1d8 cold damage.

- **Ice Shards (Ranged Attack):** Ranged Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, range 60/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 2d8+4 piercing damage plus 1d8 cold damage.

- **Ground Freeze (Recharge 5-6):** As an action, the Colossus freezes the ground in a 20-foot radius. This area becomes difficult terrain, and creatures starting their turn there must make a DC 14 Dexterity save or fall prone.

- **Absorb Cold (Bonus Action):** The Colossus can absorb cold energy to heal 2d8+4 HP. If a cold spell targets the Colossus or includes it in its area of effect, the Colossus takes no damage and instead regains hit points equal to half the damage the spell would have dealt.

#### Legendary Actions
The Frost Colossus can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The Frost Colossus regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

- **Detect:** The Frost Colossus makes a Wisdom (Perception) check.
- **Ice Shard:** The Frost Colossus makes an Ice Shard attack.
- **Frost Step (Costs 2 Actions):** The Frost Colossus moves up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

#### Reactions
- **Ice Shield:** When the Frost Colossus is hit by an attack, it can create a shield of ice, adding +5 to its AC against that attack. If the attack still hits, the Frost Colossus takes only half damage from it.

### Crystalline Nodes
Players making Perception checks (DC 16) notice that crystalline nodes on the Colossus's body glow in the same pattern as runes on the sphere.

**If Players Succeed on the Perception Check:**
*You notice that the crystalline nodes embedded in the Colossus's body are pulsing with energy in a pattern identical to the runes you observed on the sphere. These nodes—located on its shoulders, chest, and head—seem to be power conduits of some kind, channeling the same cold energy that the sphere was gathering.*

- Four major nodes (shoulders, chest, and head)
- Each node has AC 18 and 25 HP
- Destroying a node disables one special ability
- Critical hits automatically strike a node if the player declares they're aiming for one
- Any attack dealing 20+ damage can target a node with a successful DC 16 Dexterity save by the Colossus

**Node Effects:**
- **Destroying Left Shoulder Node:** Disables Ice Shards ability
- **Destroying Right Shoulder Node:** Disables Ground Freeze ability
- **Destroying Chest Node:** Disables Frost Aura
- **Destroying Head Node:** Disables Absorb Cold ability and weakens Legendary Actions (reduce to 1)

### Shatter Form Phase
When reduced to half health (60 HP), the Frost Colossus enters its second phase:

**Narrative Description:**
*As your weapons strike true and the Colossus staggers back, its massive form begins to crack. Fissures spread across its crystalline body like a spider's web. For a moment, it stands frozen in place—then, with a sound like a thousand icicles shattering at once, it explodes into fragments that scatter across the battlefield.*

*Before you can celebrate, the fragments begin to move with purpose. The largest pieces pull together, forming three smaller but still dangerous constructs of ice. Each stands about eight feet tall, more agile than their massive predecessor but glowing with the same cold power.*

*The battle has entered a new phase.*

The Colossus shatters into 3 Medium-sized Ice Elementals:

**Ice Elemental**
*Medium elemental, neutral evil*

- **Armor Class:** 14
- **Hit Points:** 40 each
- **Speed:** 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 16 (+3) | 16 (+3) | 16 (+3) | 6 (-2) | 10 (+0) | 8 (-1) |

- **Damage Immunities:** Cold, poison
- **Condition Immunities:** Poisoned
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** Fire
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages:** Primordial
- **Challenge Rating:** 2 (450 XP)

#### Special Abilities
- **Ice Walk:** The elemental can move across and climb icy surfaces without needing to make an ability check.
- **Frost Form:** A creature that touches the elemental or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 1d6 cold damage.

#### Actions
- **Multiattack:** The elemental makes two slam attacks.
- **Slam:** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 2d6+3 bludgeoning damage plus 1d6 cold damage.
- **Frost Breath (Recharge 5-6):** The elemental exhales a 15-foot cone of freezing wind. Each creature in that area must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw, taking 3d6 cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Tactical Notes
- The Colossus targets spellcasters first with ranged attacks
- Uses Ground Freeze when multiple enemies are grouped together
- Absorbs cold whenever below 75% health
- When shattered, elementals surround and focus on a single target
- The Colossus occasionally speaks phrases in Common like "Submit to the inevitable cold" or "The Architect's reign begins anew"
- If players try to run, the Colossus pursues for 2-3 rounds before returning to guard the sphere

### Victory Conditions
The battle ends when:
- The Colossus and all ice elementals are defeated
- The party retreats beyond the valley (difficult, as the Colossus will pursue)

## Part 3: Aftermath and Vision (15 minutes)

### After the Battle

**Narrative Description:**
*As you land the final blow, the Frost Colossus begins to crack, fractures spreading across its massive form like lightning through ice. It raises one arm toward the sky, as if reaching for something unseen, and utters final words in that strange language of ice and wind.*

*Then, with a sound like a thousand frozen lakes cracking at once, it shatters completely. Shards of magical ice explode outward, then dissolve into mist that swirls briefly before dissipating into the cold mountain air.*

*As the mist clears, you see what remains—crystalline fragments littering the snow, each glowing with residual magical energy. These fragments might hold properties useful for cold-based magic or crafting.*

*But before you can fully process your victory, a final pulse of energy erupts from the sphere. This pulse is different—not aggressive, but revelatory. It washes over you like a wave of insight, and for a brief moment, all of you share the same vision...*

### The Vision

**Narrative Description:**
*Your consciousness expands beyond your physical form, and a series of images floods your mind:*

*First, you see multiple spheres identical to the one you've discovered, arranged in a precise pattern across the mountain region. Lines of cold energy connect them, forming a ritual circle of tremendous power.*

*Next, you witness these connected spheres channeling elemental cold into a central location deep within a forbidding mountain range—the Forbidden Peaks that the Yeti elders mentioned. The energy flows into what appears to be an ancient prison of magical ice and stone.*

*Within this prison, a figure of ice and shadow grows stronger with each pulse of energy—the same figure you glimpsed in the sphere's projection. Cracks form in the magical barriers containing it as it absorbs more power.*

*Finally, you see a landscape transformed by endless winter—mountains and valleys buried under impossible depths of snow, forests crystallized into formations of deadly ice, and armies of constructs similar to the Colossus marching outward from the Forbidden Peaks, led by the fully-restored figure of the Frost Architect.*

*The vision fades, leaving you with the certain knowledge that this sphere was merely one component of a much larger and more terrible plan.*

**Vision Sequence:**
1. A network of spheres forming a ritual circle across the mountain region
2. The circles channeling elemental cold into a central location in the Forbidden Peaks
3. A prison of ancient magic beginning to crack
4. A figure of ice and shadow growing stronger
5. A landscape transformed into endless winter with armies of ice constructs marching outward

### Physical Evidence
After defeating the Colossus, the players can collect:

- **Crystalline Fragments (2d4+2):** 
  - Each fragment has different magical properties
  - Can be used as components for cold-based magic items
  - Arcana check (DC 14) to identify potential uses
 
**Potential Fragment Uses:**
- Component for weapons with cold damage bonus
- Material for crafting amulets of cold resistance
- Ingredient for potions of frost protection
- Catalyst for enhancing spells with cold effects
- Core element for creating freezing traps

## Part 4: Return to the Council (20 minutes)

### Journey Back

**Narrative Description:**
*The journey back to the Yeti settlement is easier than your outward trek. As you put distance between yourselves and the sphere, the unnatural cold begins to recede, and the bizarre ice formations become less frequent.*

*Normal wildlife gradually returns—mountain goats picking their way across rocky slopes, eagles soaring overhead. It's as if nature itself is reclaiming the space that had been corrupted by the sphere's influence.*

*Major seems more thoughtful than usual during the return journey. "I can sense it now," he says during one of your rest stops. "The presence of the Frost Architect feels stronger than before. It's as if your discovery of the sphere has accelerated something that was already in motion."*

### Skill Check Opportunities During Return
- **Survival (DC 13):** Find a safer, faster route back
- **Nature (DC 14):** Identify unusual changes in the ecosystem caused by the sphere's influence
- **Perception (DC 15):** Notice signs of other spheres being deployed in distant locations
- **Arcana (DC 16):** Recognize patterns in the cold energy's dissipation that might reveal more about how the network functions

### Council Chamber

**Narrative Description:**
*When you return to the Yeti settlement, you find the council chamber even more crowded than before. Representatives from distant Yeti settlements have arrived—distinctive in their varied fur patterns and ornaments—adding their voices to the concerned murmurs that fill the ice cavern.*

*The ancient Yeti called Dreamspeaker now sits with the elders at the council table, her age-worn wisdom clearly valued in this time of crisis. Maps are spread across the ice table, showing the mountain regions and marked with locations that correspond to the sphere network you saw in your vision.*

*The conversations quiet as you enter, all eyes turning to you with a mixture of hope and dread, eager to hear what you've discovered.*

### Council Reaction

**Elder Frostmantle:** *Rising to greet you* "You return intact—this alone brings joy. But your faces tell me your journey revealed troubling things."

*After the party shares their discoveries about the sphere, the Colossus, and their vision...*

**Elder Snowheart:** "Your report confirms our worst fears. The Frost Architect has indeed returned, though still weakened from his imprisonment. These spheres are his attempt to regain power and transform our lands once more."

**Elder Iceclaw:** *Marking the sphere locations on a map* "We must warn all settlements, both Yeti and human. These spheres threaten all who live in these mountains."

**Dreamspeaker:** *Her ancient voice crackling like ice* "The visions you experienced mirror those that have troubled my sleep. The network of spheres, the prison weakening... time grows short."

### The Ancient Binding

**Dreamspeaker:** "The ancient binding is failing. When our ancestors faced the Frost Architect generations ago, they used powerful magic focused through sacred stones—the Stones of Binding."

*She traces a pattern on the table with one clawed finger, creating a formation of seven points surrounding a center.*

"Originally, seven stones were crafted. Our ancestors, together with dwarf allies from the mountain kingdoms, gave their lives to empower these stones. The ritual succeeded, imprisoning the Frost Architect in a place where no warmth ever reaches, deep within the Forbidden Peaks."

*Her expression grows somber.*

"But time has not been kind. Three of the stones—Harmony, Wisdom, and Balance—were lost to calamity over the centuries. Earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, tomb robbers who disturbed what should have remained sealed." *She shakes her head slowly.* "The binding held because the four cardinal stones remained intact: Clarity, Resilience, Power, and Binding itself."

"These four must be found and brought to the Chamber of Binding. They are enough to renew the prison—but only if we act before the Frost Architect breaks free entirely."

*She looks directly at the party, her ancient eyes holding a spark of urgency.*

"With each sphere he activates, his power grows. Soon, no ritual will be strong enough to contain him."

### Player Knowledge Check Opportunities
- **History (DC 15):** Recall legends about ancient binding rituals and their requirements
- **Arcana (DC 16):** Understand the magical principles behind the binding and why the four cardinal stones are essential
- **Religion (DC 14):** Recognize ritual patterns similar to those used in divine sealing ceremonies

### The Next Quest

**Elder Frostmantle:** "You have done a great service by uncovering this threat. But a greater task now lies before us all. The spheres must be disabled before they can form a complete network. And the Four Stones of Binding must be recovered if we are to renew the prison that holds the Frost Architect."

**Major:** *Sitting beside the party* "I believe our paths continue together, friends. The mountains have grown more dangerous, and your skills will be needed if we're to face this ancient evil."

**Dreamspeaker:** "The first stone is said to lie in the Caverns of Echo, three days' journey to the east. It is guarded by ancient magic and creatures born of frost and shadow."

**Elder Snowheart:** *Presenting a crystal map* "This will guide you to the Caverns. But be warned—if the Frost Architect knows we seek to renew his prison, he will send his servants to stop you. The journey will be perilous."

## Part 5: Rewards and Conclusion (10 minutes)

### Rewards
The Council offers thanks and rewards:

**Frost Crystal Weapons:** Weapons infused with fragments from the Colossus (deal +1d4 cold damage)

**Description:** *These weapons have been infused with crystalline fragments from the Frost Colossus. The fragments have been carefully integrated into the weapons' structure by Yeti craftspeople. When wielded, a faint blue glow emanates from the crystal components, and frost forms along the weapon's edge or striking surface.*

**Yeti Fur Cloaks:** Advantage on saves against cold effects

**Description:** *Made from the shed fur of elder Yetis and treated with ancient protective magic, these thick cloaks provide exceptional warmth and magical protection against cold. The fur seems to shift color slightly with changing light, sometimes appearing pure white, sometimes with hints of blue or silver. When cold dangers are present, the fur bristles slightly of its own accord.*

**Ice-Sight Goggles:** Can see through snow and ice up to 1 foot thick

**Description:** *These goggles are crafted from treated ice crystals set in frames of polished bone. When worn, they allow the wearer to peer through snow and ice as if it were slightly cloudy glass. The magic within them responds to the wearer's thoughts, allowing focus on specific depths and areas.*

**Experience points:** For defeating the Colossus and completing the mission (enough to ensure level advancement if the party started at level 1)

### Session Conclusion

**Narrative Description:**
*As you accept these gifts from the grateful Council, the weight of responsibility settles upon your shoulders. You've uncovered an ancient evil awakening, one that threatens not just the Yeti settlements but potentially the entire region.*

*The crystalline map that Elder Snowheart has given you glimmers faintly in the blue light of the council chamber, the route to the Caverns of Echo clearly marked. The first of four Stones of Binding awaits there—the first step in a desperate race against time.*

*Major stands beside you, his steady presence a reminder that you won't face these challenges alone. "Tomorrow we begin preparations," he says quietly. "The path to the Caverns is not easy, and what guards the Stone may be unlike anything we've faced so far."*

*Outside the council chamber, you can hear the sounds of the Yeti settlement preparing for harsher times—supplies being gathered, defenses strengthened, younglings being taught survival skills. The race to find the Four Stones of Binding before the Frost Architect completes his network of spheres has begun, and much depends on your success.*

*The ancient evil stirs in its weakening prison. The spheres gather their cold energy. And somewhere in the Caverns of Echo, the first key to salvation waits to be found.*

## Adjusting Difficulty
- If the Frost Colossus proves too challenging, reduce its HP (to 90-100) or remove one of its special abilities
- For more experienced parties, enhance the Colossus with additional legendary actions
- The node weaknesses provide a tactical dimension—emphasize or de-emphasize based on party composition
- Major can offer tactical advice or even assist in the battle if the party is struggling

## Character Integration Notes
- If any characters have fire-based abilities, this is their time to shine, as the Colossus is vulnerable to fire
- Characters with ranged attacks can use the ridge for advantage
- Those with cold resistance will be particularly effective against the Frost Aura
- Players with high Strength can attempt to topple ice formations onto the Colossus

## Foreshadowing Elements
Several elements foreshadow future sessions:
- The vision of the sphere network establishes the campaign's ticking clock
- The mention of the Caverns of Echo introduces the next adventure location
- The revelation of the dwarven connection sets up later exploration of an abandoned dwarven fortress
- The Frost Architect's partial appearance hints at the campaign's final boss
