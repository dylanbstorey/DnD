# Session 3: The Caverns of Echo

## Session Overview
This session takes the players on their quest to find the first of the Seven Stones of Binding in the mysterious Caverns of Echo, facing new servants of the Frost Architect and solving ancient puzzles.

## Part 1: Preparations and Journey

### Departure from Yeti Settlement
- The party prepares for the three-day journey to the Caverns of Echo
- Elder Snowheart provides a crystal map showing the caverns' location
- Dreamspeaker gives additional information: "The Stone of Clarity was hidden in the deepest chamber, where echoes never fade. It reveals truth amidst illusion."
- Major confirms he will continue to guide the party

### Journey Overview
The three-day journey takes the party through:
1. **Day 1:** Mountain passes and alpine meadows
2. **Day 2:** Dense pine forests increasingly affected by cold magic
3. **Day 3:** Rocky badlands leading to the cavern entrance

> **DM Note:** One quick encounter during travel keeps the momentum moving but doesn't bog down the session. Choose or roll from the table below.

### Journey Encounter Table (d4)
1. **Frost-Touched Wildlife** (1 Frost-Infected Bear plus 2d4 Frost Wolves)
   - These normal animals have been infected by cold energy
   - Blue ice crystals grow from their fur
   - Aggressive and unnaturally coordinated
   - Bear: AC 14, HP 42, Multiattack (2 claws), Bite attack adds 1d6 cold damage
   - Wolves: AC 13, HP 18 each, Pack tactics

2. **Blizzard Ambush** (Environmental challenge + combat)
   - Sudden magical blizzard reduces visibility to 10 feet
   - Disadvantage on Perception checks and ranged attacks
   - 1d4+1 Ice Scouts use the blizzard as cover to ambush the party
   - DC 15 Perception or Survival check spots them before they attack

3. **Mountain Travelers** (3-4 human merchants)
   - Frightened travelers fleeing from "strange cold storms"
   - Can provide rumors of other spheres being sighted
   - Willing to trade supplies but very anxious to continue southward
   - One merchant has frostbite with unusual blue patterns - early stage of frost infection

4. **Unstable Pass** (Environmental challenge)
   - Narrow mountain pass destabilized by cold magic
   - Several small avalanches threaten the path
   - Each character must make 2 Dexterity saves (DC 14)
   - Failure results in 2d8 bludgeoning damage and being partially buried
   - Alternative route adds half a day to journey

## Part 2: The Cavern Entrance

### Arrival
"After three days of travel, you arrive at a stark rocky outcropping. A wide, dark opening yawns in the mountainside—the entrance to the Caverns of Echo. Crystalline formations surround the entrance, growing like teeth from both above and below. The air here is unnaturally still, and sounds seem to travel strangely, bouncing back with distorted timing. Major's ears twitch with discomfort."

### Guardian Encounter
The entrance is guarded by:
- 2 Frost Sentinels (improved Ice Guardians)
  - AC 17, HP 65 each
  - **Multiattack:** Two ice blade attacks
  - **Ice Blade:** +8 to hit, 2d6+5 slashing plus 1d8 cold damage
  - **Ice Spear (Ranged):** +6 to hit, range 60/120 ft., 1d10+3 piercing plus 1d8 cold damage
  - **Frost Barrier (1/day):** Creates a wall of ice 15 feet long, 10 feet high, 1 foot thick (AC 12, 30 HP per 10-foot section)

### The Entrance Chamber
After defeating or bypassing the guards:
- Large chamber with unusual acoustics - whispers return as thunderous echoes
- Floor covered with a thin sheet of ice inscribed with ancient symbols
- Archways lead to three different tunnels
- Frozen waterfall along one wall contains partially visible humanoid shapes

> **DM Guidance:** Arcana check (DC 15) reveals the shapes in the ice are not dead bodies but magical constructs, currently dormant.

## Part 3: The Cavern Exploration

### Echo Warrens
The Caverns feature three main areas that must be explored:
1. The Whispering Galleries
2. The Frozen Lake
3. The Chamber of Resonance

### Navigation Challenges
- Normal mapping doesn't work due to acoustic distortion
- Sound-based navigation is required (Perception checks)
- Ice formations shift and change when not observed directly
- Some passages appear as solid walls until sound reveals them

### The Whispering Galleries

"These winding tunnels amplify and distort sound in strange ways. Whispers from hundreds of yards away sound as if spoken directly in your ear, while shouts disappear into nothing. The walls are smooth ice polished to mirror-like perfection, reflecting light and sound in disorienting patterns."

**Features:**
- Maze-like tunnels with perfect acoustics
- Whispers of ancient voices provide clues and misleading information
- Ice mirrors show reflections that move independently
- Central chamber with frozen fountain that produces musical tones when touched

**Puzzle:** Players must reproduce a specific sequence of tones to open a hidden passage. The correct sequence can be heard as whispers throughout the galleries, but must be distinguished from false sounds.

**Encounter:** Echo Mimics (2-3)
- Creatures that perfectly copy the appearance and sounds of intruders
- AC 14, HP 36 each
- **Mimic Voice:** Can perfectly imitate any sound or voice it has heard
- **False Appearance:** Appears identical to a party member
- **Touch of Dissonance:** +6 to hit, 1d8+3 cold damage plus 1d8 thunder damage
- **Disorienting Echo:** Target must make DC 14 Wisdom save or be confused until end of next turn (disadvantage on attack rolls, can't use reactions)

### The Frozen Lake

"The tunnel opens into an enormous cavern dominated by a perfectly still, frozen lake. The ice is so clear it appears to be liquid until touched. Beneath the surface, strange shapes move in the depths. Stone columns rise from the ice at irregular intervals, each carved with runes that glow with a soft blue light. The ceiling is lost in darkness above."

**Features:**
- Lake surface can be walked upon (slippery, DC 12 Acrobatics to run or fight effectively)
- Touching the ice reveals visions of the past
- Columns contain clues about the Stone of Binding
- Far side of lake has a sealed door requiring activation of all columns

**Puzzle:** Players must activate all columns in the correct sequence by touching them and speaking the word revealed in their vision. Incorrect sequence causes the ice to crack beneath them.

**Encounter:** Depth Hunter (1)
- Massive predator that swims beneath the ice
- Visible as a shadowy shape following the party
- Can attack through the ice, creating temporary holes
- AC 16, HP 85
- **Multiattack:** Two claw attacks
- **Ice Breach:** +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., 2d10+6 slashing damage, target must make DC 15 Strength save or be pulled into the water
- **Frost Breath (Recharge 5-6):** 30-foot cone, DC 16 Dexterity save, 6d6 cold damage

### The Chamber of Resonance

"This perfectly circular chamber rises into a natural dome. The walls are covered in crystalline formations that vibrate in harmonic patterns when sound reaches them. At the center stands a circular dais with seven depressions arranged in a pattern resembling a constellation. One depression glows with inner light. The air here feels charged with potential energy, as if the entire chamber is waiting for something."

**Features:**
- Every sound creates visible ripples of light along the walls
- Center dais controls a powerful magical lock
- Ancient runes circle the room, telling the story of the binding ritual
- Harmonics can be used to reveal hidden features

**Puzzle:** Players must create the correct harmonic pattern using sound (instruments, voice, or creative use of objects) to unlock the Stone's container. The pattern is hinted at by the constellation shape of the depressions.

**Final Challenge:** As they solve the puzzle, the chamber activates its final defense:

**Guardian of Resonance** (1)
- Crystalline humanoid figure composed of sound and ice
- Emerges from the wall as the Stone's container begins to open
- Tests the worthiness of those seeking the Stone
- AC 18, HP 110
- **Multiattack:** Two resonant strike attacks
- **Resonant Strike:** +8 to hit, 2d8+5 force damage plus 1d8 thunder damage
- **Sonic Pulse:** 20-foot radius, DC 15 Constitution save, 4d8 thunder damage and pushed 10 feet away on failed save, half damage on success
- **Harmonic Absorption:** When hit by thunder or force damage, heals for half the damage instead
- **Frequency Shift:** As a bonus action, changes its resonant frequency. The next creature to hit it with a melee attack must make a DC 15 Constitution save or take 3d6 thunder damage

## Part 4: The Stone of Clarity

### Recovering the Stone
After defeating the Guardian or passing its test:
- The center depression on the dais opens
- A crystal stone rises from within, glowing with inner light
- "The Stone of Clarity floats before you, a multifaceted crystal about the size of a fist. Its surfaces constantly shift and change, reflecting light in impossible ways. Looking into it, you briefly see visions of other places—other spheres being deployed across the mountains."

### Stone Powers
The Stone of Clarity grants:
- Ability to see through illusions (advantage on saves against illusion magic)
- Once per day: Cast See Invisibility spell
- Reveals the true nature of the Frost Architect's servants
- Provides a vision of where the next Stone might be found

### Escape Challenge
As soon as the Stone is taken:
- The caverns begin to destabilize
- Ice formations start collapsing
- Temperature drops dramatically
- Players must escape before being trapped

### Pursuit
During the escape:
- More Frost Sentinels appear at the entrance
- The dormant constructs in the frozen waterfall break free
- Major helps find the fastest route out

## Part 5: Return and Next Steps

### Vision from the Stone
Once safely away from the caverns, the Stone activates:
- Shows a vision of an abandoned dwarven fortress overrun with winter wights
- Reveals this is the location of the second Stone (the Stone of Resilience)
- Hints that the stones must be used in a specific formation

### Major's Insight
"This Stone connects to the others. As we find more, their combined power will grow. The Frost Architect will sense that we have this one now. His servants will hunt us more aggressively."

### Session Conclusion
- The party has recovered the first of seven Stones
- They now have a destination for their next quest
- The Frost Architect's forces are now aware of their interference
- Time is running short as more spheres are being deployed
