# Vision of the Dwarven Fortress
*As revealed by the Stone of Clarity*

## The Vision

*As you hold the newly acquired Stone of Clarity outside the Caverns of Echo, its facets begin to glow with an inner light. The world around you seems to fade as images form within the Stone's crystalline depths, becoming more vivid until they surround you completely...*

You find yourselves standing before a massive mountainside fortress, its entrance formed of intricately carved stone doors set deep into the mountain face. Dwarven runes line the archway above, though many are obscured by frost. The once-proud entrance now bears the unmistakable signs of abandonment—cracked stonework, rusted metal fixtures, and an eerie silence that hangs in the air.

As your vision draws closer, you notice the fortress doors stand partially open, with strange crystalline ice formations growing outward from the gap like reaching fingers. Unnatural cold emanates from the entrance, visible as a pale mist that clings to the ground.

The scene shifts to interior glimpses:

- Great halls with soaring pillars, now draped in ice and shadow
- Forges once hot with dwarven fire, now cold and silent
- Workshops filled with abandoned tools and half-finished projects
- Living quarters with personal belongings still in place, as if vacated suddenly

Throughout these spaces, you catch glimpses of shifting forms—winter wights drifting through corridors, ice constructs standing guard at key junctions, and thin layers of frost covering everything like a shroud.

Deep within the mountain, in what appears to be a ceremonial chamber, you see a pedestal surrounded by dwarven statues standing in a circle. Upon the pedestal, partially encased in unnaturally perfect ice, sits a stone similar to the one you now hold, though with different patterns swirling within its depths.

The final image shows a towering figure of ice and shadow stalking the halls—taller than the others, with a crown-like formation upon its head and armor of crystalline ice. It moves with purpose, directing the lesser servants as they transform the dwarven halls to better serve their master.

*As the vision fades, you feel a subtle pull from the Stone, drawing you in the direction of the fortress you just witnessed...*

## Interpreting the Vision

### The Fortress
The vision reveals Frosthold, once the greatest dwarven stronghold in the northern mountains. According to historical records held by the Yeti Council, it was abandoned during the aftermath of the original binding ritual generations ago. The dwarves sealed it and considered it a tomb for those who sacrificed themselves in the binding.

### Key Information

1. **The Stone of Resilience**: The second of the Seven Stones of Binding is located within Frosthold, in what appears to be a ceremonial chamber. It is partially encased in magical ice.

2. **Fortress Occupation**: The fortress is now occupied by servants of the Frost Architect, including:
   - Winter wights
   - Ice constructs
   - A new, powerful lieutenant - the Frost General

3. **Defenses**: Unlike the Caverns of Echo, which were largely empty until recently, Frosthold appears to be actively guarded and patrolled.

4. **Points of Interest**:
   - The main entrance (partially accessible)
   - The great halls (ceremonial spaces)
   - The forges (potential source of fire/heat)
   - Workshops (possible equipment/resources)
   - Living quarters (historical records/clues)
   - Ceremonial chamber (location of the Stone)

### Yeti Council Knowledge

The Yeti Council can provide limited additional information about Frosthold:

- It was built approximately 300 years ago as a dwarven trading post and mining colony
- During the Time of Long Winter, it became a sanctuary for those fleeing the Frost Architect
- Dwarven craftsmen from Frosthold worked with Yeti elders to create the Seven Stones of Binding
- After the binding ritual, the surviving dwarves sealed the fortress and abandoned the region
- No one has entered Frosthold in living memory

### Journey Details

- The fortress lies three days' journey to the east, deeper in the mountain range
- The path involves crossing a high mountain pass that may be treacherous in the current conditions
- The Stone of Clarity will guide the way, glowing more brightly as you approach Frosthold

### Preparations Needed

Before embarking on this journey, the party should consider:

1. **Fire Sources**: Given the prevalence of ice creatures, fire-based weapons or spells may be particularly effective.

2. **Cold Protection**: The magical cold within Frosthold will be more intense than in the Caverns of Echo.

3. **Stealth Equipment**: The occupied nature of the fortress suggests that stealth may be a viable approach.

4. **Dwarven Language**: Any party members who can read Dwarvish will be valuable for deciphering runes and texts within.

5. **Stone of Clarity**: Understanding how to use the stone's powers of illusion detection will be crucial in a place where the Frost Architect's deceptions may be present.

## The Frost General

The imposing figure glimpsed in the vision is a new and significant threat—the Frost General, a powerful lieutenant created by the Frost Architect to command its lesser servants. Unlike the mindless winter wights or the simple-minded ice constructs, the Frost General possesses tactical intelligence and purpose.

The vision suggests that the Frost General is actively overseeing the transformation of Frosthold into a stronghold for the Frost Architect's forces. Its presence indicates that the Frost Architect considers the Stone of Resilience important enough to warrant special protection.

This will be the party's first encounter with an entity capable of complex strategy and potentially communication, offering both increased danger and opportunity for the party to learn more about their enemy's plans.

## Connection to the Binding Ritual

The Stone of Resilience represents the strength and endurance required to withstand the Frost Architect's power. Its location within a dwarven fortress is symbolic—dwarves were chosen to craft and bear this stone during the original binding due to their legendary resilience and steadfastness.

The next phase of the quest will reveal more about how the dwarves contributed to the original binding and what sacrifices were made.
