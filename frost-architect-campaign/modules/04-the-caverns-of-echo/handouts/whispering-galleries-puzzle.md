# The Musical Fountain Puzzle
*A Challenge of Sound and Harmony*

## Overview
The central chamber of the Whispering Galleries contains an ancient fountain carved from crystalline ice. This magical construct once flowed with enchanted water that enabled communication across vast distances, but now it stands frozen, its magic dormant. Unlocking its power—and the hidden passage it conceals—requires playing the correct sequence of tones.

## Physical Description
The frozen fountain sits in the center of a perfectly circular chamber. Eight crystalline formations extend from the fountain in a starburst pattern, each capable of producing a distinctive musical tone when struck or touched. The formations vary in size and shape, creating a natural octave of notes from low to high.

The chamber's unusual acoustics cause sound to behave in unexpected ways:
- Whispers from the far side of the chamber may sound as if spoken directly into one's ear
- Loud noises might vanish completely, absorbed by the crystalline structures
- Certain tones create visible ripples of blue light that travel along the walls

## The Puzzle Challenge
To activate the fountain and reveal the hidden passage to the Frozen Lake, players must:

1. Discover the correct sequence of eight tones
2. Play them in the proper order
3. Maintain the correct timing between tones

## Clues for Players

### The Whispering Voices
Throughout the galleries, players can hear faint whispers if they stand in the right locations and remain quiet. These whispers provide clues to the correct sequence:

- Near the first mirror: *"Begin with what's closest to the heart..."*
- Near the second mirror: *"End with what's farthest from the beginning..."*
- Near the third mirror: *"The seventh follows the sixth, as night follows day..."*
- Near the fourth mirror: *"What stands opposite must be played in opposition..."*

### Visual Clues
The crystalline formations around the fountain have subtle markings that suggest their proper order. When viewed with a light source held at the correct angle, small numbered runes become visible on each formation:

1. Formation closest to the entrance (lowest tone)
8. Formation to the right of #1 (second lowest tone)
7. Formation opposite #2 (third lowest tone)
6. Formation opposite #3 (middle-low tone)
5. Formation opposite #4 (middle-high tone)
4. Formation opposite #5 (high-middle tone)
3. Formation opposite #6 (second highest tone)
2. Formation opposite #7 (highest tone)

### Ice Mirror Reflections
The ice mirrors scattered throughout the galleries show reflections that change depending on the viewer. When a player with musical training or proficiency in Performance views a reflection, they briefly see the fountain with glowing formations indicating the correct sequence.

## Correct Sequence
The tones must be played in the following order: 1-8-7-6-5-4-3-2

When played correctly, the timing between notes should follow a simple rhythm: quarter notes with a slight pause after the fourth tone.

## Effects of Success and Failure

### Success
When the correct sequence is played with proper timing:
- The frozen fountain begins to glow with blue-white light
- Ice crystals within the fountain shift and rearrange
- The water begins to flow upward rather than downward
- A hidden doorway in the far wall slides open, revealing a passage to the Frozen Lake
- The fountain continues to flow for 1 hour, during which whispers from throughout the caves can be clearly heard

### Failed Attempts
Each incorrect attempt triggers different responses depending on how close the players came to the correct sequence:

- Completely wrong sequence: The crystalline formations vibrate discordantly, creating uncomfortable sensations but no harmful effects
- Partially correct sequence (3-4 correct tones): The formations glow briefly before fading, indicating partial success
- Nearly correct (5-7 correct tones): The fountain begins to activate but then freezes again, and a shower of ice crystals falls harmlessly around the chamber
- Correct tones but wrong timing: The formations all glow but the fountain remains frozen; a whisper can be heard saying, *"Find the rhythm of the binding..."*

### Harmful Failure
If players deliberately try to damage the fountain or repeatedly play discordant sequences (more than 5 failed attempts), the chamber's defenses activate:
- Echo Mimics hiding within the walls emerge to protect the fountain
- The acoustic properties of the room intensify, causing any loud sounds to deal 1d4 thunder damage to all creatures in the chamber
- The exit may temporarily seal until the room returns to a state of harmony

## Alternative Solutions
Players who think creatively can find other ways to solve the puzzle:

- A bard or character with musical training can make a DC 15 Performance check to intuit the correct sequence
- Characters with Stone Cunning or similar abilities might notice patterns in the chamber's construction that hint at the sequence
- Magic like *Detect Magic* reveals the flow of magical energy through the formations, suggesting their proper activation order
- Clever use of *Minor Illusion* or similar spells to create tones can work just as well as physically striking the formations

## DM Notes
This puzzle is designed to engage players who enjoy both logical reasoning and creative problem-solving. The key is to provide multiple ways to discover the solution rather than forcing a single approach.

If players struggle, the Echo Mimics might actually provide additional clues before becoming hostile—mimicking the correct tones in sequence as a form of taunt, unwittingly helping the players solve the puzzle.

For groups less interested in puzzles, simplify by making the whispered clues more direct or having an NPC like Major recognize the pattern from similar puzzles he's encountered before.
