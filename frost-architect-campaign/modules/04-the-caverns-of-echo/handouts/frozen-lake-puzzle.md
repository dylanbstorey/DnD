# The Column Activation Puzzle
*Awakening the Ancient Runes*

## Overview
The Frozen Lake contains eight ancient columns rising from the ice, each inscribed with runes that glow with a soft blue light. These columns are arranged in a specific pattern around the lake and represent significant events from the original binding of the Frost Architect. To unlock the sealed door leading to the Chamber of Resonance, the columns must be activated in the correct sequence.

## Physical Description
Each column is a ten-foot-tall pillar of blue-white ice that seems to glow from within. The columns are numbered 1 through 8, with the numbering visible on their bases. Runes spiral around each column from base to top, telling fragments of the binding ritual story.

The ice of the lake is perfectly clear, allowing glimpses of strange shapes moving in the depths below. The surface is slippery but stable enough to walk on (DC 12 Acrobatics check to run or fight effectively on the ice).

## The Puzzle Challenge
To open the sealed door on the far side of the lake, players must:

1. Discover the correct sequence for activating the columns
2. Touch each column in the proper order
3. Speak the word revealed in the vision when touching each column

## Column Positions and Words
Each column, when touched, reveals a momentary vision of the past and a word that must be spoken aloud:

| Column | Position | Vision | Word |
|--------|----------|--------|------|
| 1 | Northwest | Dwarves and Yeti forming an alliance | "Alliance" |
| 2 | Northeast | Seven heroes sacrificing themselves | "Sacrifice" |
| 3 | East | The ritual circle being formed | "Circle" |
| 4 | Southeast | The Frost Architect being captured | "Capture" |
| 5 | South | The binding taking effect | "Binding" |
| 6 | Southwest | The Stones being separated | "Separation" |
| 7 | West | The Stones being hidden | "Concealment" |
| 8 | North | The return of warmth to the land | "Renewal" |

## Clues for Solving the Puzzle

### Vision Spots
Four locations on the lake (marked on the map) provide important visions when touched:

- **Northwest Vision Spot:** Shows Yeti and Dwarves joining hands, whispering "Alliance forms the foundation."
- **Northeast Vision Spot:** Shows a ritual with seven participants, whispering "Binding requires harmony of purpose."
- **Southeast Vision Spot:** Shows seven stones arranged in a circle, whispering "Seven become one in proper sequence."
- **Southwest Vision Spot:** Shows a crystal glowing with inner light, whispering "Clarity shows the way from beginning to end."

### Rune Patterns
The runes on each column feature subtly different patterns. When viewed with magical detection or by someone proficient in Arcana:

- The runes flow in different directions on each column
- Column 1's runes flow outward toward Column 8
- Column 8's runes flow toward Column 7
- This pattern continues in sequence: 7→6→5→4→3→2

### Ice Reflections
The perfectly clear ice reflects the columns in meaningful ways. On a successful Intelligence (Investigation) check (DC 15):

- The reflection of Column 1 appears strongest/clearest
- The reflection of Column 2 appears faintest/most distorted
- The reflections form a gradient from clearest to faintest that suggests the activation order

## Correct Activation Sequence
The columns must be activated in the following order: 1-8-7-6-5-4-3-2

This sequence represents the order of events from the binding ritual, starting with the alliance (1) and ending with the final sacrifice (2).

## Effects of Success and Failure

### Success
When the columns are activated in the correct sequence and each word is properly spoken:

- All eight columns illuminate simultaneously with bright blue light
- Light beams connect between columns in the pattern of the binding ritual
- The sealed door on the eastern shore slides open with a deep rumbling sound
- The ice becomes more stable (no more Acrobatics checks needed to move normally)
- The Depth Hunter retreats deeper into the lake, avoiding the column light

### Failed Attempts
Each incorrect attempt has consequences:

- **Wrong Sequence:** The columns briefly flash red, then return to their dormant state
- **1-3 Mistakes:** The ice beneath the players cracks slightly (cosmetic effect only)
- **4-5 Mistakes:** The ice cracks more severely (DC 13 Dexterity save or fall prone)
- **6+ Mistakes:** Large ice fissures form (DC 15 Dexterity save or take 1d6 cold damage and fall prone)

### Critical Failure
If the players make three complete failed activation attempts, the defense mechanism activates:

- All columns flash red simultaneously
- The Depth Hunter rises through the ice at the center of the lake
- The ice becomes more unstable (DC 15 Acrobatics to move at normal speed)
- The Hunter attacks until the party leaves the lake or defeats it

## Depth Hunter's Behavior
The Depth Hunter patrols beneath the ice, visible as a shadowy presence:

- Initially, it observes the party from a distance
- It becomes more aggressive as the party makes mistakes
- It may attack through the ice if the party spends too long in one spot
- If the party stands on cracked ice, it is more likely to attack from below
- It retreats temporarily if damaged for more than 1/4 of its hit points at once

## Alternative Solutions
Creative players may find other ways to overcome the challenge:

- Using divination magic like *Augury* or *Commune* to learn the correct sequence
- Casting *Control Water* or similar spells to manipulate the ice and reveal hidden clues
- Using *Stone Shape* on the columns themselves to examine their inner structure
- Characters with relevant background knowledge (Arcana or History proficiency) might recognize the ritual pattern and deduce the sequence

## DM Notes
This puzzle is designed to incorporate exploration, history, and problem-solving:

- Allow players to gain insight through skill checks appropriate to their characters
- The puzzle isn't designed to be "solved" immediately - encourage exploration of the lake
- If players struggle, the Depth Hunter might inadvertently reveal clues during combat
- For parties without magical abilities, emphasize the visual clues and vision spots
- Consider the party's composition - if they lack certain skills, make alternative clues more prominent

The key insight players need to make is that the puzzle represents the historical events of the binding ritual, from alliance to sacrifice, and must be activated in that narrative order.
