# Session 7: The Final Binding

## Session Overview
The climactic finale of the Frost Architect campaign — or so the party believes. They descend into the Forbidden Peaks, battle through the Frost General, and reach the Chamber of Binding to perform the ritual that will imprison the ancient evil forever. But nothing is as it seems. The truth waiting in that chamber will reframe everything they thought they knew.

**DM CRITICAL NOTE:** This session contains the campaign's major twist. The Frost Architect does not exist — or rather, the entity they've been told about was never real. The binding was never broken. The entire quest was orchestrated by an unknown party to gather all four Stones of Binding in one place. When the party attempts the ritual, they discover an empty prison, and the stones are stolen. This sets up the next campaign arc.

---

## Background for DM

### The Truth
The "Frost Architect" the party has been fighting against is a fabrication — a projection created by the sphere network, given substance by cold magic, but ultimately a puppet with no puppeteer. The real Frost Architect either:
- Never existed (the legend was always a myth used to explain natural magical phenomena)
- Died centuries ago (the original binding worked permanently)
- Was something else entirely (misidentified by ancient peoples)

What the party has actually been fighting:
- **The Spheres:** Real magical constructs that gather cold energy — but their purpose was to power the projection and the constructs, not to "free" anything
- **The Constructs:** Real creatures of ice magic, created by whoever built the spheres
- **The Frost Colossus:** A real threat, but just a very large construct
- **The Frost General:** Real and dangerous, the most sophisticated construct in the network
- **The "Frost Architect" Projection:** A hologram given enough magical substance to seem threatening, but with nothing behind it

### Who Did This?
This is deliberately left vague for the DM to develop. The unknown orchestrator:
- Wanted all four Stones of Binding gathered in one place
- Had the magical knowledge to create the sphere network and constructs
- Knew the legend of the Frost Architect well enough to exploit it
- Has plans for the Stones that are far worse than anything the "Frost Architect" represented

Possible clues to leave (choose one or more):
- A symbol burned into the altar — unknown origin, for future identification
- A voice that speaks as the stones vanish — but whose?
- Lady Rime recognizing something and being terrified (if she's present)
- The spheres all simultaneously shutting down — their purpose complete
- A message left in ancient script: "The collection is complete"

### The Emotional Arc
This session should move through distinct emotional beats:
1. **Determination** — The final push, the end in sight
2. **Triumph** — Defeating the Frost General, reaching the Chamber
3. **Anticipation** — Beginning the ritual, stones in place
4. **Confusion** — The ritual isn't working; something's wrong
5. **Horror** — The prison is empty; the binding was never broken
6. **Loss** — The stones are taken; they've been used
7. **Resolve** — A new enemy, a new quest; this isn't over

## Part 1: Journey to the Forbidden Peaks

### Setting Out
With three stones in hand (Clarity, Resilience, Power), the combined resonance reveals the path to the Chamber of Binding — and the final stone that rests there.

**Read aloud:**
*The three Stones pulse with unified light as you hold them together. The resonance builds until images flood your mind: a path descending into darkness, ancient doors sealed with ice, a chamber where cold light pulses like a heartbeat. You see the final Stone — the Stone of Binding itself — resting on an altar at the heart of the chamber, waiting for its siblings to complete the circle.*

*The path is clear now. The Forbidden Peaks. The prison of the Frost Architect. The place where this all began, and where it must end.*

**Major:** "The way is revealed. But the Architect knows we're coming now. Every servant he has left will be between us and that chamber."

**Dreamspeaker** (if present, or via sending stone): "The ritual requires all four stones placed at the cardinal points of the binding circle. Speak the words of sealing — I will teach them to you — and pour your will into the stones. The prison will be renewed, and the Frost Architect will sleep for another age."

*She pauses, her ancient eyes troubled.* "But the Architect will not submit quietly. Be prepared for his final desperate assault."

### The Approach
The journey to the Forbidden Peaks takes one day of hard travel through increasingly hostile terrain.

**Environmental Description:**
*As you ascend into the Forbidden Peaks, the world transforms around you. The sky darkens to a perpetual twilight, heavy clouds blocking the sun. The temperature drops beyond natural cold — your breath freezes before it leaves your lips, falling as tiny crystals. The wind carries whispers that almost sound like words.*

*Ice formations grow in impossible geometries: spirals that defy gravity, crystalline trees with razor-sharp branches, frozen waterfalls suspended in mid-flow. The mountains themselves seem to lean inward, as if watching your progress.*

*Major's hackles rise. "We're being observed. The Architect knows we're here."*

### Journey Encounters (Choose 1-2)

**1. The Gauntlet of Ice**
A narrow pass between two cliff faces, lined with ice construct sentries.

- 4-6 Ice Scouts positioned on ledges
- Attempting to pass quietly: Group Stealth DC 16
- If detected: Scouts don't attack directly but begin collapsing ice formations
- Each round in the pass: DC 14 DEX save or 2d6 bludgeoning from falling ice
- Destroying 3+ scouts stops the bombardment

**2. The Frozen Army**
The party crests a ridge and sees what awaits.

*Below you, arranged in silent ranks, stands an army of ice. Hundreds of constructs — Winter Wights, Ice Scouts, hulking Frost Sentinels — all motionless, all facing the path to the Chamber. They don't move, don't react to your presence. They simply... wait.*

*Major studies them carefully. "They're not activated. Reserve forces, perhaps. Or..."*

*He doesn't finish the sentence. The implication is clear: if the ritual fails, this army will awaken.*

**DM Note:** This army never activates. It's a red herring — impressive but ultimately revealed to be inert constructs with no one to command them. After the twist, their presence becomes eerie rather than threatening.

**3. Lady Rime's Warning (if allied)**
If the party has the Rime Pendant or Lady Rime is traveling with them:

*Lady Rime stops suddenly, her pale features even more drawn than usual. "Something is wrong," she whispers. "I've served... entities of cold for centuries. The presence we're approaching... it feels hollow. Like an echo with no voice."*

*She shakes her head. "Perhaps the Architect is weaker than we thought. Or perhaps..." She trails off, unwilling to voice the alternative.*

**Insight DC 15:** She's genuinely disturbed, not performing. She doesn't know what's waiting, but her instincts are screaming that something is fundamentally wrong.

## Part 2: The Gate of Winter

### The Entrance
The path descends to a massive gate carved into the mountainside — the entrance to the Chamber of Binding.

**Read aloud:**
*The Gate of Winter rises before you, a hundred feet of carved stone and magical ice. Ancient dwarven craftsmanship frames a door of solid frost that glows with inner light. Runes of binding cover every surface, their meanings lost to time but their power still palpable.*

*The carvings tell a story: tall figures alongside shorter, broader ones — Yeti and Dwarf working together. A great battle against a crowned figure of ice. The ritual of binding, seven stones arranged in a circle. And finally, peace — snow falling gently on a sleeping mountain.*

*But standing before the gate, barring your passage, is the figure you've been dreading: the Frost General.*

### The Frost General

**Read aloud:**
*He stands nine feet tall, a warrior sculpted from living ice and armored in frost that shifts and reforms with every movement. A crown of icicles adorns his head, and his eyes burn with cold fire. In his hands, he holds a greatsword of crystalline ice that hums with contained power.*

*"You have come far," he says, his voice like wind through a frozen canyon. "Farther than any have come since the binding was first wrought. But your journey ends here."*

*He raises his blade.* "The Architect commands it. And I obey."*

### Pre-Battle Dialogue
The Frost General will exchange a few words before combat:

**If asked about the Frost Architect:**
"The Architect is eternal. The Architect is winter itself. You cannot comprehend what you seek to imprison."

**If asked why he serves:**
"I was made to serve. It is my purpose, my existence. When the Architect speaks, I act. This is the nature of creation."

**Insight DC 18:** There's something mechanical about his devotion — not passionate loyalty, but programmed response. He speaks of the Architect in rehearsed phrases, as if reciting rather than feeling.

**If Lady Rime is present:**
The Frost General's eyes linger on her. "Traitor. The Architect will deal with you when this is finished."

Lady Rime laughs, but it's hollow. "Will he? I wonder if he'll deal with anyone, ever again."

### Frost General Battle

This is the climactic boss fight of the campaign. The Frost General is the most dangerous enemy the party has faced.

**Frost General**
*Large construct, lawful evil*

- **Armor Class:** 19 (magical ice armor)
- **Hit Points:** 200
- **Speed:** 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 24 (+7) | 14 (+2) | 22 (+6) | 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 18 (+4) |

- **Saving Throws:** STR +12, CON +11, WIS +8, CHA +9
- **Damage Immunities:** Cold, poison
- **Damage Resistances:** Bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Condition Immunities:** Charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, poisoned
- **Damage Vulnerabilities:** Fire
- **Senses:** Darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 18
- **Languages:** Common, Primordial
- **Challenge Rating:** 13

#### Abilities

**Frost Aura:** Any creature that starts its turn within 10 feet of the Frost General takes 2d6 cold damage and must succeed on a DC 17 CON save or have its speed reduced by 10 feet until the start of its next turn.

**Ice Regeneration:** The Frost General regains 15 hit points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 hit point and is in contact with ice or snow.

**Magic Resistance:** The Frost General has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Glacial Presence:** The ground within 30 feet of the Frost General is difficult terrain for creatures other than constructs.

#### Actions

**Multiattack:** The Frost General makes three attacks with Glacier Blade.

**Glacier Blade:** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +12 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 2d10+7 slashing damage plus 2d8 cold damage.

**Winter's Grasp (Recharge 5-6):** Ranged spell attack, +9 to hit, range 60 ft. On hit, target takes 4d8 cold damage and is restrained by ice (DC 17 STR check to break free, or AC 14/20 HP to destroy the ice).

**Blizzard Form (Recharge 6):** The Frost General transforms into a swirling blizzard for up to 1 minute. While in this form:
- Resistance to all damage except fire
- Can move through spaces as narrow as 1 inch
- Can move through other creatures' spaces
- Any creature whose space it moves through takes 4d6 cold damage (DC 17 DEX save for half)
- Cannot make weapon attacks, but can use Winter's Grasp

#### Legendary Actions (3/round)

**Detect:** Makes a Perception check.

**Ice Shard:** Ranged attack, +9 to hit, 60 ft., 2d6+4 piercing plus 1d6 cold.

**Frost Command (2 actions):** All ice constructs within 60 feet can immediately move up to half their speed and make one attack.

**Glacial Slam (3 actions):** The Frost General slams the ground, creating a 20-foot radius shockwave. All creatures in the area must make a DC 17 DEX save or take 4d8 bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone. On a success, half damage and not prone.

#### Legendary Resistances (3/day)
If the Frost General fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

### Battle Environment

**The Gate Courtyard:**
- 60 feet wide, 80 feet deep
- Ice-covered flagstones (difficult terrain without Ice Walking)
- Four pillars of carved ice (provide cover, can be toppled with DC 18 Athletics — 3d6 damage in a line)
- A frozen fountain in the center (can be shattered for difficult terrain)
- The Gate itself at the far end (sealed until the General falls)

**Reinforcements:**
At the start of round 3 and round 6, 2 Ice Sentinels emerge from alcoves to assist the General.

**Ice Sentinel**
- AC 17, HP 65
- Multiattack: 2 ice blade attacks (+8, 2d6+5 slashing + 1d8 cold)
- Ice Spear: +6, range 60 ft., 1d10+3 piercing + 1d8 cold

### The General's Fall

**Read aloud:**
*The Frost General staggers as your final blow lands true. Cracks spread across his icy form, fractures spiderwebbing through the crystalline armor that has made him so formidable. He drops to one knee, his great sword shattering against the ground.*

*"Impossible," he breathes, his voice weakening. "The Architect... promised... eternal..."*

*His eyes flicker, the cold fire dimming. "I hear... nothing. The voice... is silent. It was always... silent..."*

*With a sound like a glacier calving, the Frost General collapses, his body breaking into a thousand glittering shards that scatter across the frozen courtyard. The pieces don't reform. They simply... melt, as if whatever magic animated him has finally, completely, run out.*

*Behind you, the Gate of Winter groans. Ancient mechanisms, freed from the General's presence, begin to turn. The door of solid frost cracks, shifts, and slowly swings inward.*

*The path to the Chamber of Binding is open.*

**DM Note:** The General's last words are the first real hint. "The voice is silent. It was always silent." He's realizing, in his final moments, that the presence he served never actually existed.

## Part 3: The Chamber of Binding

### Descent

**Read aloud:**
*Beyond the gate, stairs descend into darkness. The walls are carved with more scenes of the ancient binding — the battle, the ritual, the imprisonment. But as you descend, you notice something strange: in these deeper carvings, the "Frost Architect" figure seems less defined, more abstract. Almost as if the artists weren't sure what they were depicting.*

*The cold intensifies with each step, but it feels different now. Not the aggressive, hunting cold of the spheres and constructs. This cold is ancient, patient, and utterly still. Nothing has disturbed these depths in centuries.*

*Major's ears flatten against his head. "It's too quiet," he murmurs. "If the Architect truly dwells here, where is his presence? Where is the malevolence we've felt from every sphere and construct?"*

*The stairs end at another door — smaller than the gate above, but covered in the most intricate binding runes you've yet seen. The three Stones in your possession pulse urgently, straining toward whatever lies beyond.*

### The Chamber

**Read aloud:**
*The door opens at your touch, responding to the Stones you carry. Beyond lies a circular chamber, perhaps fifty feet across, with a domed ceiling lost in shadow. The walls are smooth ice, carved with a continuous spiral of binding runes that wind from floor to ceiling. At the center of the room stands a raised dais with four pedestals arranged at cardinal points around a central altar.*

*On the altar rests the fourth Stone — the Stone of Binding. It glows with soft light, waiting.*

*But at the heart of the chamber, between the altar and the far wall, there is... nothing.*

*No prison of magical ice. No slumbering figure of terrible power. No ancient evil straining against its bonds. Just empty air, empty stone, empty cold.*

*Major's voice is barely a whisper: "Where is he?"*

### Investigating the Chamber

Let the players investigate. Key discoveries:

**Perception DC 14:**
"The dust on the floor hasn't been disturbed in... decades? Centuries? Whatever walked here last, it was a very long time ago."

**Arcana DC 16:**
"The binding runes are intact. Not weakened, not fading — fully powered and completely functional. Whatever they were meant to contain... they're still containing it. Or they never contained anything at all."

**Investigation DC 15:**
"There are pedestals for seven stones, not four. Three of them have been damaged — deliberately broken, not worn by time. Someone didn't want the original ritual to ever be performed again."

**History DC 17:**
"The carvings tell of a great evil imprisoned here. But the deeper you look, the more the 'Frost Architect' seems like a metaphor — 'the cold that hunts,' 'the winter that kills.' It reads more like a personification of harsh winters than a description of an actual entity."

**DC 20 Investigation (on the altar):**
Beneath a layer of frost, there are scratch marks. Recent — within the last few years. Someone has been here. Someone touched this altar.

### Major's Realization

**Read aloud:**
*Major paces the chamber, his nose working frantically. "This is wrong," he says, his voice rising. "This is all wrong. I've been sensing the Frost Architect's presence since the first sphere — that cold, malevolent awareness. But here, at the heart of his prison, there's nothing. NOTHING."*

*He stops, turns to face you.* "What if there never was anything? What if we've been chasing a ghost — no, worse than a ghost. A story. A lie."*

## Part 4: The Ritual (That Fails)

### Attempting the Binding

Despite their doubts, the party may still attempt the ritual. Dreamspeaker taught them the words. They have the Stones. What else can they do?

**Read aloud (when they place the Stones):**
*You place the four Stones on their pedestals — Clarity to the east, Resilience to the north, Power to the west, Binding at the center. As the last Stone settles into place, light erupts from each, beams connecting them in a web of brilliant energy.*

*The binding runes on the walls flare to life, pulsing in sequence like a heartbeat quickening. The air hums with power. For a moment, it seems to be working.*

*You speak the words Dreamspeaker taught you. Ancient syllables of sealing, of imprisonment, of eternal sleep. The words feel right, resonating with the Stones, with the chamber, with magic older than memory.*

*The light intensifies. The hum becomes a roar. The Stones strain toward the center of the chamber, toward the space where the Frost Architect should be imprisoned...*

*And pass through empty air.*

*The beams of light meet, intertwine, and find nothing. No target. No entity. No prisoner to bind. The energy surges, seeking something to latch onto, and finds only void.*

### The Failure

**Read aloud:**
*The light flickers. Wavers. The hum becomes discordant, the runes pulsing erratically. Something is wrong — not with the ritual, but with its target.*

*"There's nothing there," you realize. "There's nothing to bind."*

*The energy builds to a crescendo with nowhere to go. The chamber shakes. The Stones pulse desperately, reaching for a presence that doesn't exist.*

*And then, from somewhere outside the chamber — outside the mountain — a voice speaks. Not the voice of the Frost Architect. Something else. Something that has been watching, and waiting, for this exact moment.*

### The Theft

**Read aloud:**
*"Thank you."*

*The voice is calm, almost warm. It echoes through the chamber without source or direction — as if the stones themselves are speaking.*

*"I could never have gathered them myself. The wards, the guardians, the distances involved... but you. You brave, determined heroes. You did what I could not."*

*The Stones begin to shake on their pedestals.*

*"The 'Frost Architect' was a useful fiction. A threat terrible enough to unite enemies, to justify any sacrifice, to drive heroes to the ends of the earth. And you performed admirably."*

*One by one, the Stones rise from their pedestals, floating toward the center of the chamber.*

*"Now the Stones of Binding are together for the first time in five centuries. And they are exactly where I need them to be."*

### The Vanishing

**Read aloud:**
*You lunge for the Stones, but your hands pass through them — they've become intangible, already more there than here.*

*"Don't mourn your failure. You succeeded beyond all expectation. You simply didn't know what game you were playing."*

*The four Stones spiral together, their light merging into a single brilliant point. Runes appear in the air around them — not the binding runes of this chamber, but something else. Something you don't recognize.*

*"We'll meet again. You've proven yourselves... useful."*

*The light flashes, blinding-bright, and when your vision clears, the Stones are gone. The pedestals are empty. The chamber is silent.*

*On the altar where the Stone of Binding rested, something has been burned into the stone — a symbol you've never seen before. [Describe the symbol — DM's choice for future campaign hook.]*

*The ritual is over. The prison is empty. The Stones are gone.*

*And somewhere, someone is very, very pleased.*

## Part 5: Aftermath

### Immediate Reactions

Give the players time to process. They've just had the rug pulled out from under them. Let them express their characters' emotions.

**Possible NPC Reactions:**

**Major:**
*Major sits heavily, his ears flat, his tail still.* "I led us here. I believed... we all believed..." *He shakes his head.* "Everything we fought. Everything we sacrificed. For a lie."

**Lady Rime (if present):**
*Lady Rime stares at the symbol burned into the altar, her face pale.* "I don't recognize it. I've served powers of cold and winter for centuries, and I don't know that symbol." *Her voice shakes — the first time you've heard fear from her.* "What have we unleashed?"

### The Symbol

The symbol burned into the altar is the primary clue for the next campaign. Describe it vividly but leave its meaning ambiguous.

**Suggested Symbol Options:**

1. **A spiral within a circle, with seven points around the edge** — suggests the Stones will be used in a different ritual

2. **An eye with a snowflake for a pupil** — someone was watching the entire time

3. **Three interlocking triangles** — an organization's mark, suggesting conspiracy

4. **A crown above an empty throne** — someone seeks to rule, not destroy

5. **A door, slightly ajar** — the Stones will open something

**Investigation DC 18 on the symbol:**
"It was burned here by the same energy that took the Stones. Whoever did this wanted us to see it. Wanted us to know there's more to come."

### What They Know Now

Help the players piece together what they've learned:

1. **The Frost Architect never existed** — or died so long ago that the "prison" has been empty for centuries
2. **The sphere network was real** — but its purpose was to create the illusion of a threat, not to free an entity
3. **The constructs were real** — created by whoever orchestrated this, not by any "Frost Architect"
4. **The legend was exploited** — someone knew the old stories and used them as cover
5. **The Stones were the goal** — everything else was misdirection
6. **Someone is still out there** — and now they have all four Stones of Binding

### What They Don't Know

1. Who spoke to them
2. What the Stones will be used for
3. What the symbol means
4. How long this plan has been in motion
5. Whether there are other "heroes" being manipulated elsewhere

## Part 6: Epilogue

### Leaving the Chamber

**Read aloud:**
*You climb the stairs in silence. The Gate of Winter stands open, the Frost General's shards already melting in the thin mountain air. Beyond, the frozen army you passed still stands motionless — but as you watch, the constructs begin to crumble. Without the sphere network to power them, without a will to animate them, they're just ice. They collapse one by one, hundreds of warriors returning to the snow from which they were made.*

*The threat of the Frost Architect is over. It was never real to begin with.*

*But something else has begun.*

### Return to the Council

**Dreamspeaker's Reaction:**
*The ancient Yeti listens to your account in silence. When you finish, she is still for a long moment.*

*"The Frost Architect was real, once. Of that I am certain — my ancestors' memories do not lie. But it seems he has been gone far longer than any of us knew. The binding worked. Perhaps worked too well."*

*She looks at the sketch you made of the symbol.* "This, I do not know. But I will search the oldest memories, the deepest dreams. Someone has played a very long game, and we are all pieces on their board."*

**Elder Frostmantle:**
*"The spheres are failing. The constructs are melting. The immediate threat to our people is ended."* He pauses. *"But at what cost? What will these Stones be used for? What horror have we unknowingly enabled?"*

### Hooks for the Future

Plant seeds for the next campaign:

1. **The Symbol:** Someone, somewhere, knows what it means. Finding them is the first step.

2. **Lady Rime's Fear:** If she's present, her genuine terror suggests the true enemy is something even a centuries-old fey finds frightening.

3. **Other Heroes:** The voice said "you've proven yourselves useful." Have there been others? Are there other "quests" being manipulated right now?

4. **The Stones' Purpose:** The Stones of Binding were made to imprison. If someone wanted them badly enough to orchestrate all of this... what do they plan to bind? Or unbind?

5. **Cheese's Council:** The Yeti Council has connections throughout the mountains. They may have heard rumors, stories, other strange events that now take on new meaning.

### Final Scene

**Read aloud:**
*Days pass. The unnatural cold recedes from the mountains. The spheres go dark, then crumble. The displaced Yeti families begin the long journey home. Life, it seems, will return to normal.*

*But you know better.*

*You've seen the symbol. Heard the voice. Felt the Stones slip through your fingers into the hands of something you don't understand. The immediate threat is over, but a larger shadow has fallen across your path.*

*Major finds you as you prepare to depart the settlement.* "The Elders have asked me to remain with you. Whatever comes next, you'll need a guide. And frankly—" *his tail wags once, briefly* "—I want to know who made a fool of all of us."*

*The mountains stretch before you, beautiful and treacherous as ever. Somewhere out there, someone holds four stones of immense power and a plan centuries in the making.*

*The Frost Architect's tale is over.*

*Yours is just beginning.*

## DM Notes

### Managing the Twist

**Foreshadowing:** Throughout the campaign, subtle hints were present:
- The "Frost Architect" was only ever a projection
- Constructs spoke in programmed phrases about the Architect
- The Frost General's dying realization that "the voice was always silent"
- Lady Rime's instinct that something was "hollow"

**Player Reactions:** Some players may feel cheated. Acknowledge their characters' frustration in-game while emphasizing:
- The threats they faced were real
- The people they saved are genuinely saved
- They now have knowledge no one else has
- They're positioned to be the ones who uncover the truth

**The Unknown Villain:** Resist the urge to reveal too much. The voice should be memorable but not identifiable. The symbol should be striking but not explicable. Mystery drives future engagement.

### Tone Guidance

The session should feel like the climax of a thriller that pivots into a conspiracy revelation:
- **Early session:** Epic, triumphant — the final battle, the culmination of their journey
- **Mid session:** Confused, unsettling — something's wrong, where's the Architect?
- **Late session:** Horrified, determined — they've been used, but they won't be used again

### Alternative Endings

If the twist doesn't land well, or if you want a more conclusive ending:

**Alternative A: Partial Truth**
The Frost Architect existed but died during the original binding. Someone has been using his legend and leftover power. The party's ritual actually cleanses the last remnants of his influence — a genuine victory, but the Stones are still stolen.

**Alternative B: Lady Rime's Sacrifice**
If Lady Rime is present and allied, she can choose to pursue the Stones as they vanish, following them to wherever they're being taken. She disappears with them, but establishes a connection the party can later use to track her — and the Stones.

**Alternative C: One Stone Remains**
The theft is interrupted — perhaps by Matrim's Frost Rage flaring, or Madrigal's Wild Magic surging. One Stone (probably Clarity, thematically) remains with the party, giving them a tool and a connection to the others.

1. Research the symbol
2. Contact other settlements/organizations about similar strange events
3. Find Lady Rime (if she vanished) or consult her (if she remained)
4. Investigate who knew enough about the Binding Stones to orchestrate this
5. Prepare for whatever comes next

### Campaign Transition
This ending transforms the Frost Architect campaign from a complete story into the prologue of something larger. The tone shifts from "heroes vs. ancient evil" to "investigators uncovering conspiracy." The next arc should feel more uncertain, more paranoid, and more personal — they were used, and they want to know by whom.
