# Session 5: The Dwarven Fortress

## Session Overview
This session takes the players to an abandoned dwarven fortress in search of the Stone of Resilience, the second of the Four Stones of Binding. They will discover the dwarves' connection to the original binding ritual and face more powerful servants of the Frost Architect.

## Part 1: Preparations and Journey

### Planning the Expedition
- The Stone of Clarity has revealed the location of the second stone: Frosthold, an ancient dwarven fortress
- Major identifies the location on their maps: "Three days northwest, in the Iron Peaks. The fortress was abandoned centuries ago."
- Opportunity for players to resupply and prepare for the new environment

### Journey Overview
The three-day journey takes the party through:
1. **Day 1:** Alpine valleys gradually ascending into higher mountains
2. **Day 2:** Treacherous mountain passes with evidence of old dwarven roads
3. **Day 3:** High rocky terrain with abandoned mining operations

> **DM Note:** Choose ONE journey encounter that best suits your party's strengths or challenges.

### Journey Encounter Table (d4)
1. **Frost Archer Patrol** (3-4 Frost Archers)
   - Humanoid constructs of ice with crystalline bows
   - Taking position on high ground to ambush travelers
   - AC 15, HP 45 each
   - **Ice Bow:** +7 to hit, range 150/600 ft., 1d8+4 piercing plus 1d6 cold damage
   - **Freezing Shot (Recharge 5-6):** Target must make DC 14 Constitution save or be restrained by ice until end of its next turn
   - **Tactical Retreat:** Can disengage as a bonus action

2. **Avalanche Trap** (Environmental challenge)
   - Magically triggered avalanche set by the Frost Architect's servants
   - DC 15 Perception to notice disturbed snow and ice before triggering
   - If triggered: DC 16 Dexterity save or take 4d6 bludgeoning damage and be buried
   - Buried characters must be dug out (Athletics checks) or suffocate

3. **Dwarven Ghosts** (Social/Exploration encounter)
   - Spectral remnants of dwarven miners who died during the Long Winter
   - Not hostile but disoriented, believing they're still alive
   - Can provide historical information about Frosthold if approached carefully
   - Charisma (Persuasion) DC 14 to calm them and gain information
   - Reveal that Frosthold has a secret vault "beneath the king's hammer"

4. **Sphere Deployment** (Story encounter)
   - Party witnesses a small sphere (like those seen inside the first sphere) descending from the sky
   - It embeds itself in the mountainside and begins emitting cold energy
   - No immediate guardians, but the deployment site grows colder by the minute
   - Players can choose to investigate or continue to Frosthold
   - If investigated: Contains tracking runes linked to the main sphere network

## Part 2: Frosthold Approach

### First Sight
"As you round the mountain path, you see Frosthold carved into the mountainside—massive stone doors flanked by weathered dwarven statues stand at the entrance to what was once a mighty fortress. The statues hold hammers aloft, though one has crumbled, its hammer lying broken at its feet. Blue-white light pulses from within the partially open doors, casting eerie shadows across the ancient stonework. Snow has drifted halfway up the entrance, but a path has been cleared recently—something is using this fortress."

### Outer Defenses
The approach to Frosthold is guarded by:
- 3-4 Winter Wight Guards (enhanced)
  - AC 14, HP 45 each
  - **Enhanced Cold Aura:** 1d8 cold damage to all creatures within 5 feet
  - **Frozen Claw:** +5 to hit, 1d8+3 slashing plus 1d8 cold damage
  - **Ice Mail:** Resistance to non-magical piercing damage
  - **Tactical Coordination:** These wights fight with surprising intelligence, using flanking tactics

- Environmental hazards:
  - Patches of supercooled ice (DC 13 Perception to spot, DC 15 Dexterity to avoid slipping)
  - Unstable snow cornices that can collapse (2d6 bludgeoning damage)
  - Ancient dwarven traps, some still functional after centuries

### The Entrance Hall
After defeating or bypassing the guards:
- Massive hall with 30-foot ceilings supported by stone pillars carved to resemble mighty dwarven warriors
- Floor covered with a thin layer of ice, cracked dwarven skeletons visible beneath
- Walls show signs of a desperate last stand—weapon marks, scorch patterns
- Far end features a throne on a raised dais, occupied by a frozen dwarven corpse wearing a crown

> **DM Guidance:** History check (DC 14) reveals these dwarves likely died during the Time of Long Winter when the Frost Architect first ruled. They appear to have been defending the fortress from within.

## Part 3: Fortress Exploration

### Major Areas
The fortress contains several key locations:
1. The Great Hall (entrance)
2. The Forges (east wing)
3. The Living Quarters (west wing)
4. The Throne Room (central chamber)
5. The Secret Vault (hidden beneath the throne room)

### Navigation Challenges
- Some passages have collapsed over centuries
- Magical cold has warped and frozen mechanisms
- Winter wights patrol throughout
- Ancient dwarven security systems still function

### The Forges

"This cavernous chamber once housed the legendary forges of Frosthold. Massive anvils and hammers lie frozen in place, and the great furnaces are cold and dark. Ice formations have grown from the ceiling to cover much of the equipment. In the center, a strange crystalline structure has been erected—a miniature version of the sphere you encountered earlier."

**Features:**
- Ancient forges that can still be lit with sufficient heat
- Scattered dwarven crafting tools, some still usable
- Frozen half-finished weapons and armor of exceptional quality
- Miniature cold sphere pulsing with energy

**Puzzle:** Players must reactivate one of the ancient forges to melt the ice around a sealed door. This requires finding fuel, repairing the bellows mechanism, and redirecting water flow.

**Encounter:** Forge Guardian (1)
- Partially completed dwarven construct now controlled by cold magic
- AC 17, HP 95
- **Multiattack:** Two slam attacks
- **Slam:** +8 to hit, 2d8+5 bludgeoning damage plus 1d6 fire damage
- **Molten Core:** When reduced to half health, its core becomes exposed, dealing 1d10 fire damage to all creatures within 5 feet at the start of its turn
- **Absorb Metal:** Can absorb metallic weapons that hit it (DC 15 Strength save to retain weapon)
- **Forge Tools:** Can use forge tools as improvised weapons

### The Living Quarters

"These once-comfortable chambers have been transformed into a nightmarish tableau. Frozen dwarven residents still sit at tables or lie in beds, perfectly preserved by the extreme cold. Personal belongings and treasures remain untouched. Winter wights move through these spaces, seemingly studying the frozen dwarves. In a central meeting hall, runes of cold magic have been carved into the floor, creating a ritual space."

**Features:**
- Frozen dwarven bodies containing valuable historical information
- Personal quarters with diaries and records about the original binding ritual
- Treasury with dwarvish valuables and magical items
- Ritual space being prepared for something ominous

**Puzzle:** Players must decipher the runes in the ritual space to understand the Frost Architect's plans. This requires finding a dwarvish translation key in the chief lorekeeper's quarters.

**Encounter:** Frost Ritualist and Winter Wight Acolytes (1 Ritualist, 3-4 Acolytes)
- Ritualist: Humanoid figure wrapped in flowing robes of shifting ice
  - AC 16, HP 70
  - **Spellcasting:** Frost Bolt, Cone of Cold, Hold Person
  - **Cold Manipulation:** Can shape and control ice within 30 feet
  - **Frost Shield:** Reaction to reduce damage from one attack by 2d8
  - **Ritual Casting:** If uninterrupted for 3 rounds, summons additional winter wights

- Acolytes: Enhanced winter wights with limited spellcasting
  - AC 14, HP 40 each
  - **Frost Touch:** +5 to hit, 2d6 cold damage
  - **Cantrips:** Ray of Frost, Chill Touch

### The Throne Room

"The throne room of Frosthold stands in silent testimony to the dwarves' last stand. A massive throne of stone and iron dominates the rear of the chamber, occupied by a perfectly preserved dwarven king. His frozen body still clutches a great warhammer, and his eyes stare defiantly forward. Above the throne, a mural depicts seven dwarven heroes alongside yeti allies, confronting a towering figure of ice. The floor bears scorch marks and weapon scratches—evidence of a desperate battle."

**Features:**
- Dwarven king's corpse holding the legendary Warhammer of Frosthold
- Mural depicting the original binding ritual with dwarves and yetis
- Hidden mechanism behind the throne
- Magical cold emanating from beneath the throne

**Puzzle:** The Stone of Clarity reveals that the king's warhammer must be placed on a specific point in the room to reveal the entrance to the vault. The correct position is shown in shadow only when the party creates enough light to illuminate the entire chamber.

**Encounter:** The Frozen King (1)
- The dwarven king's corpse animated by cold magic
- Initially appears dead until the players attempt to access the vault
- AC 18, HP 120
- **Multiattack:** Two attacks with Froststrike Warhammer
- **Froststrike Warhammer:** +9 to hit, 2d10+6 bludgeoning damage plus 2d6 cold damage
- **Commanding Presence:** Can summon 1d4 dwarven ghosts (use Winter Wight stats) as a bonus action
- **Royal Decree:** Targets one creature, who must make a DC 16 Wisdom save or be frightened for 1 minute
- **Frost Nova (Recharge 5-6):** 20-foot radius, DC 16 Constitution save, 6d6 cold damage and stunned until end of next turn on failed save, half damage on success

### The Secret Vault

"Beneath the throne room lies a perfectly circular chamber lined with dwarvish runes of protection. At its center stands a pedestal of blue stone, upon which rests a cube of what appears to be solid metal. As you enter, the Stone of Clarity in your possession begins to pulse with light, responding to its sibling. The metal cube slowly transforms, its surface flowing like liquid until it forms a perfect dodecahedron—the Stone of Resilience."

**Features:**
- Ancient dwarvish protections still active around the chamber
- Historical records carved into the walls explaining the dwarves' role in the binding
- Pedestal containing the Stone of Resilience
- Magical connection between the first and second stones

**Final Challenge:** As they approach the Stone, the chamber's final defense activates:

**Elemental Trials** (Environmental challenge)
- The chamber tests worthiness through elemental trials
- Four sequential challenges: earth, fire, water, air
- Each requires appropriate skill checks or creative problem-solving
- Earth: Chamber shakes violently (Strength/Dexterity checks to remain standing)
- Fire: Temperature rises to dangerous levels (Constitution saves or take fire damage)
- Water: Chamber begins to flood (Athletics checks to swim/stay afloat)
- Air: Air is sucked from the room (Constitution saves or begin suffocating)

## Part 4: The Stone of Resilience

### Recovering the Stone
After completing the trials:
- The Stone of Resilience floats from its pedestal
- "The dodecahedron-shaped Stone of Resilience hovers before you, its metallic surface constantly shifting between solid and liquid states. It feels surprisingly light when grasped, and warm to the touch despite the surrounding cold. When held near the Stone of Clarity, both stones pulse in a synchronized rhythm."

### Stone Powers
The Stone of Resilience grants:
- Increased resistance to damage (bearer gains resistance to one damage type of their choice, changeable after a long rest)
- Once per day: Cast Stoneskin spell
- When combined with the Stone of Clarity: Can create a protective barrier against cold energy
- Provides a vision of where the third Stone might be found

### Escape Challenge
As soon as the Stone is taken:
- The fortress begins to shake violently
- The Frost Architect becomes aware of the theft
- A powerful servant is dispatched to the fortress
- Players must escape before being trapped or confronted

## Part 5: The Frost General

### Final Confrontation
As the players attempt to leave the fortress:
- The entrance hall fills with swirling snow and bitter cold
- A figure forms from the blizzard—a towering warrior made of ice and adorned with armor of frost
- "Before you stands a figure of terrible majesty—the Frost General, one of the Frost Architect's most powerful lieutenants. Standing nine feet tall, its body is composed of dense ice with patterns of frozen armor fused to its form. A crown of icicles adorns its head, and its eyes burn with blue-white fire. It raises a massive sword of crystalline ice and speaks in a voice like an avalanche: 'The Architect has awakened. The stones are his by right. Surrender them, and your deaths will be swift.'"

### The Frost General
- AC 19, HP 180
- **Multiattack:** Three attacks with Glacier Blade
- **Glacier Blade:** +10 to hit, 2d10+7 slashing damage plus 2d8 cold damage
- **Frost Command:** Bonus action to direct all winter wights within 60 feet to attack a single target
- **Winter's Grasp:** Ranged spell attack, +8 to hit, range 60 ft., target is restrained by ice (DC 16 Strength check to break free)
- **Blizzard Form (Recharge 5-6):** Transforms into a swirling blizzard, gaining resistance to all damage, moving through other creatures' spaces, and dealing 3d6 cold damage to any creature it moves through
- **Legendary Resistances (2/Day):** Can choose to succeed on a failed saving throw

### Tactical Retreat
This enemy is likely too powerful for a direct confrontation at this stage:
- Major warns the party that this is a fight they cannot win yet
- The combined power of the two stones can create a temporary barrier
- Players must find creative ways to escape while the General pursues
- Collapsing sections of the fortress can slow the General's pursuit

## Part 6: Escape and Revelations

### Desperate Flight
- Chase sequence through the crumbling fortress
- Environmental obstacles and pursuing winter wights
- Strategic use of the Stones to block passages with barriers

### Vision from the Stones
Once safely away from the fortress, the two Stones activate together:
- Shows a vision of a glittering city nestled in a mountain valley, steam rising from hot springs
- Zooms to a grand manor filled with treasures — and there, in a glass case, the cubic Stone of Power
- The vision names the place: Crystalvale, and the collector: Lord Vesper Ashcroft
- Shows the Frost Architect growing stronger as more spheres activate
- Indicates that the Stones must be gathered quickly before the network is complete

### Major's Warning
"The Frost Architect knows we have two of the Stones now. His forces will hunt us relentlessly. We should return to the Council immediately—the Elders must know about the Frost General's appearance. It means the Architect is regaining his power more quickly than we feared."

### Session Conclusion
- The party has recovered the second of four Stones
- They've learned about the dwarves' role in the original binding
- They now face active opposition from the Frost Architect's lieutenant
- Time is running even shorter as more spheres are activated
