# Expedition Journal
*Weathered page torn from a leather-bound journal*

### Day 12
We've established camp half a mile from the sphere. The cold is unlike anything I've experienced—it doesn't simply chill, it *drains* warmth as if it were a living thing consuming it. Our magical protections barely help. Jormund believes the sphere is drawing heat from its surroundings.

### Day 13
Closer observations reveal the sphere is approximately 30 feet in diameter, made of some silvery metal I cannot identify. The entire surface is covered in runes that glow with a pale blue light. There's what appears to be an entrance at the base—a hexagonal opening surrounded by more elaborate runes.

Humanoid figures made of ice patrol the perimeter. Harrik calls them "constructs" and says they're animated by cold magic.

### Day 14
The metal sphere grows colder each day. Jormund insists we keep studying it, but three men have already lost fingers to frostbite. The runes pulsate in patterns—definitely not random. Something inside is awake and watching.

We identified six distinct rune patterns repeating across the surface. Each seems to pulse in a specific sequence. Harrik sketched some of them before his hands grew too numb to hold a pencil.

In the moments when the runes pulse most brightly, the air temperature drops precipitously. It's as if the sphere is *feeding*.

### Day 15
Ulric and Tanner never returned from their scouting mission. When we went to search for them, we found only strange angular tracks in the snow leading back toward the sphere.

We're abandoning camp at first light. I've seen other lights in the distant peaks—I fear this sphere is not the only one. Something terrible is happening in these mountains.
