# Session 1: The Frost Architect's Return

## Session Overview
This session introduces the ancient threat of the Frost Architect, sends the players on their quest to investigate the crashed sphere, and covers their journey, discovery, and initial exploration of the sphere.

## Background for DM
The Frost Architect is an ancient entity of primordial cold who once ruled the mountain region during an era known as the Time of Long Winter. This powerful being commanded winter wights, ice constructs, and all manner of frost creatures, bringing misery to all living things. The ancestors of the current Yeti Council, along with dwarven allies, performed a ritual using seven magical stones to imprison the Frost Architect in the Forbidden Peaks.

The recent appearance of metal spheres is connected to the Frost Architect's awakening. These devices are designed to:
1. Absorb ambient heat from the surrounding area
2. Channel cold energy to strengthen the Frost Architect
3. Form a network that, when complete, will allow him to fully escape his prison
4. Transform the region into a domain of endless winter

The players have already rescued Mistwalker from Snowhaven and defeated some winter wights. Now they're returning to the Yeti Council with this rescued healer, unaware of the greater threat they're about to discover.

## Part 1: Council Meeting (10 minutes)

### Setting
The massive ice chamber of the Council, now crowded with Yeti families who have fled other settlements. The mood is tense and worried.

### Narrative Description
*The Council Chamber is a spacious cavern of polished ice, illuminated by dozens of softly glowing blue lanterns. Since your last visit, the chamber has become crowded with Yeti families who've fled their homes—mothers comforting frightened younglings, elders speaking in hushed tones, warriors standing guard with troubled expressions. The air is thick with tension and whispered fears.*

*At the center sits the familiar semicircular table of transparent ice, behind which wait the three imposing figures of the Council—Elder Frostmantle with his silvered fur, Elder Snowheart with her crystal-adorned braids, and Elder Iceclaw with his distinctive ice-like right hand. Their expressions brighten slightly as you enter with Mistwalker.*

### Key Dialogue
**Elder Frostmantle:** "You return with Mistwalker. Our hopes were well-placed in you, small friends." *His voice carries both gratitude and lingering concern.* "Tell us what you discovered at Snowhaven."

*After the players recount their experiences...*

**Mistwalker:** *Bowing deeply before the Council* "Honored Elders, these brave souls found me trapped in ice. But there is grave news. Something fell from the sky—a sphere of metal that brings an unnatural cold. Our settlement became uninhabitable, and the winter wights were drawn to its emptiness."

**Elder Snowheart:** *Her expression darkening* "A sphere of metal bringing deadly cold... There are stories, ancient legends passed through generations." *She exchanges a troubled glance with the other elders.* 

**Elder Frostmantle:** "The strange metal sphere is connected to an ancient evil called the Frost Architect, who once ruled these mountains in an age of endless winter. Our ancestors imprisoned him long ago, but now something has weakened his prison."

**Elder Snowheart:** "These spheres are gathering cold energy and will form a network if left unchecked. The sphere that fell near Snowhaven must be investigated immediately."

**Elder Iceclaw:** "Three other settlements have been abandoned due to similar cold phenomena. This is spreading."

### Items Provided
- **Frost Resistance Elixir** (3 vials): Grants protection against extreme cold for 24 hours
- **Coldseeker Compass**: Points toward sources of unnatural cold
- **Dreamspeaker's Runestone**: Ancient artifact that may reveal secrets when placed against the sphere

**Elder Snowheart:** *Presenting a crystal vial of shimmering silver liquid* "Frost Resistance Elixir. When consumed, it grants protection against extreme cold for a full day. We have only three such vials, so use them wisely."

**Elder Iceclaw:** *Offering a small crystalline device resembling a compass* "The Coldseeker. It points toward sources of unnatural cold. It should lead you directly to the sphere."

**Dreamspeaker:** *An elderly Yeti approaching the party* "I am Dreamspeaker, keeper of the oldest memories. There is something about these spheres that troubles my dreams. If the Frost Architect has returned, he would be weakened from his long imprisonment. These spheres may be gathering cold energy to restore his strength."

*Pressing a small runestone into the hands of the party's apparent leader* "This stone was carved during the original binding. It may reveal secrets about the spheres that would otherwise remain hidden. Place it against the sphere's surface, and ancient magics will respond."

### DM Notes
- Keep all explanations brief and direct
- If players ask questions, have the elders say, "There will be time for more answers upon your return. For now, you must act quickly."
- Major will accompany them as a guide

### Skill Check Opportunities
- **History/Arcana (DC 15):** To recall legends about primordial winter beings
- **Religion/Arcana (DC 14):** To recognize elements of binding rituals; success reveals that powerful binding rituals require anchors (like the stones) and that breaking these anchors would weaken the imprisonment
- **Nature/Survival (DC 12):** To recall information about the Forbidden Peaks; success reveals these peaks are known for bizarre weather patterns, dangerous ice formations, and strange creatures

## Part 2: Journey to the Sphere (15 minutes)

### Narrative Description
"As you journey higher into the mountains beyond Snowhaven, the environment becomes increasingly alien. Ice forms in impossible geometric patterns. Frost crystals grow upward like strange plants. The natural wildlife has completely disappeared, replaced by eerie silence broken only by the occasional cracking of ice formations. Major leads you along narrow mountain paths that gradually become more treacherous, the cold intensifying with each mile."

**Major:** *Stopping to test the wind* "The cold grows more intense with every step. This isn't natural, even for these mountains."

*Noticing strange angular tracks in the snow* "Ice constructs. Created by powerful cold magic. We should proceed with caution."

### Group Check
- One Survival check (DC 14) to navigate to the sphere
- Failure means the party suffers from intense cold (non-mechanical consequence) and encounters a Wilderness Complication

### Wilderness Complications (d4)
1. **Extreme Cold Snap** - Temperature drops dangerously. Characters without cold protection must make a Constitution save (DC 15) or gain one level of exhaustion.
2. **Disorientation** - Strange magnetic effects from the sphere interfere with navigation. The party travels in the wrong direction for 1d4 hours unless someone succeeds on a Survival check (DC 16).
3. **Ice Crack** - The ground splits open beneath one random party member. Dexterity save (DC 14) to avoid falling 2d6×5 feet into a crevasse.
4. **Mind-Numbing Cold** - The cold affects mental faculties. All Intelligence and Wisdom checks are made with disadvantage for the next 2d4 hours unless characters drink something hot.

### Single Encounter
Choose ONE from the following options (or roll 1d4):

1. **Ice Scout Patrol** (2 Ice Scouts)
   - AC 14, HP 22 each
   - **Attack:** Ice Blade (+5 to hit, 1d8+2 slashing plus 1d4 cold damage)
   - **Detect Life:** Can sense warm creatures within 60 feet
   - **Tactics:** Attempt to alert the sphere if they spot the party
   - **Appearance:** Humanoid figures made of translucent ice with angular, faceted features and glowing blue eyes

2. **Winter Wights** (2 Enhanced Winter Wights)
   - AC 12, HP 32 each
   - **Enhanced Cold Aura:** 1d6 cold damage to all creatures within 5 feet at start of turn
   - **Frozen Claw:** +4 to hit, 1d6+2 slashing plus 1d6 cold damage
   - **Ice Armor:** First hit against each wight deals half damage
   - **Appearance:** More substantial than normal winter wights, with armor-like formations of ice covering their skeletal frames

3. **Unstable Ice Bridge**
   - A narrow bridge of ice spanning a deep crevasse
   - Each character must make an Acrobatics or Athletics check (DC 14)
   - Failure means the character falls but can catch the edge (Strength save DC 12)
   - Bridge collapses after half the party crosses (those remaining need to find another way)

4. **Abandoned Expedition Camp**
   - Tents and equipment for 3-4 people, partially buried in snow
   - Evidence of attack by ice creatures
   - Supplies: 1 day of rations, climbing gear, 1 healing potion
   - Journal page with partial information about the sphere
   - **Journal Excerpt:** *"Day 14: The metal sphere grows colder each day. Jormund insists we keep studying it, but three men have already lost fingers to frostbite. The runes pulsate in patterns—definitely not random. Something inside is awake and watching..."*

### DM Notes
- The journey should feel increasingly strange and threatening as they approach the sphere
- Weather conditions worsen, with temperature dropping noticeably
- Emphasize the unnatural behavior of ice in the area
- Major can provide exposition and warnings based on what he observes

## Part 3: The Sphere (Main Session Content)

### Discovery
As the party crests a ridge, they see a bowl-shaped valley with the sphere at its center:

**Narrative Description:**
*You crest a snow-covered ridge and look down into a bowl-shaped valley below. The sight that greets you is both awe-inspiring and deeply unsettling. At the center of the valley sits a massive metallic sphere, approximately thirty feet in diameter, half-buried in snow and ice as if it crashed there with tremendous force.*

*Its surface is a dull silver color, etched with intricate patterns and runes that pulse with blue light. The patterns shift subtly, creating the impression that the sphere is somehow alive. The air around it is visibly distorted, wavering like a heat mirage but emanating cold instead.*

*Occasional pulses of energy ripple outward from the sphere, freezing everything they touch into exotic crystalline forms. At the base of the sphere is what appears to be an entrance—a hexagonal opening surrounded by more elaborate runes.*

*Patrolling around the perimeter are several winter wights similar to those you encountered at Snowhaven, but they move with more purpose. Two larger figures—humanoid constructs made entirely of jagged ice—stand guard at the entrance. These ice guardians stand at least eight feet tall, with massive ice blades for arms.*

- 30-foot diameter metallic sphere, half-buried in snow and ice
- Surface covered in strange runes that pulse with blue light
- Air visibly distorted around it from extreme cold
- Entrance visible: a hexagonal opening at the base
- Guards: 2 Ice Guardians and 3-4 Winter Wights patrolling

### Approach Options
1. **Stealth Approach:** Circle around to a blind spot (Stealth checks DC 16)
2. **Distraction:** Create a diversion to draw guards away
3. **Direct Assault:** Fight through the guards
4. **Magical Approach:** Use the Frost Resistance Elixir to move through the cold barrier

> **DM Note:** Players without cold protection take 1d6 cold damage per minute within 100 feet of the sphere, and 2d6 cold damage per minute within 50 feet. The Frost Resistance Elixir negates this damage entirely.

### External Investigation
If players examine the sphere's exterior:
- **Perception (DC 14):** Notice that runes pulse in a pattern, possibly a sequence or code
- **Arcana (DC 16):** Identify the runes as designed to channel and focus cold energy
- **Using the Runestone:** When placed against the sphere, a section becomes transparent, revealing the interior mechanism

**Runestone Reaction Description:**
*As you press the runestone against the sphere's surface, the stone grows warm in your hand—a stark contrast to the bitter cold of the sphere itself. The runes closest to the stone begin to glow more intensely, their light shifting from blue to a bright white.*

*Suddenly, the section of the sphere beneath the stone becomes transparent, as if the metal has turned to glass. Through this window, you can see inside the sphere—a complex mechanism of metal and ice surrounds a pulsing core of what appears to be pure elemental ice. Energy flows from this core through conduits that lead to various points on the sphere's surface.*

*The vision lasts for about a minute before the metal gradually becomes opaque again, but the knowledge of what lies within remains clear in your mind.*

### Entering the Sphere
Guard stats if combat occurs:
- **Ice Guardians (2):**
  - AC 16, HP 45 each
  - **Ice Blade:** +7 to hit, 2d6+4 slashing plus 1d6 cold damage
  - **Ice Javelin:** +5 to hit, range 30/120 ft., 1d8+3 piercing plus 1d6 cold damage
  - **Frost Shield:** Reaction to reduce damage from one attack by 1d10+2
  - **Appearance:** Eight-foot-tall humanoid figures composed entirely of jagged ice with no discernible facial features except for glowing blue eyes

- **Winter Wights (3-4):**
  - Stats as previously noted
  - More organized than those at Snowhaven, they coordinate attacks and alert others if they spot intruders

### Inside the Sphere
The interior contains:

**Narrative Description:**
*The interior of the sphere is a maze of conduits, chambers, and crystalline growth. The walls are a strange metal that feels neither hot nor cold to the touch, despite the freezing temperature inside. Everything is illuminated by the soft blue glow emanating from various runes and the core itself.*

*At the heart of the sphere is a central chamber containing a swirling vortex of elemental ice—pure cold energy in physical form. It pulses rhythmically, sending waves of energy through conduits that branch throughout the sphere.*

*A control panel of sorts sits nearby, covered in more runic inscriptions that shift and change as you watch. Above this panel floats a three-dimensional map display showing the mountain region, with multiple points marked by glowing blue dots.*

*In a smaller chamber, you find several smaller spheres, about the size of a human head, apparently ready to be deployed. They're miniature versions of the main sphere, complete with similar runes on their surfaces.*

1. Central ice core - swirling vortex of elemental cold energy
2. Control panel with runic inscriptions
3. Map display showing other sphere locations
4. Several smaller spheres ready for deployment

Investigation opportunities:
- **Arcana (DC 15):** The core draws cold energy from an extraplanar source
- **Investigation (DC 16):** The control panel appears to regulate energy flow
- **Intelligence (DC 13):** The map shows planned locations for a network of spheres

### Session Climax
As players investigate, they trigger a security measure:

**Narrative Description:**
*As you continue exploring the sphere's interior, your movements or perhaps your warm presence triggers something. A high-pitched tone emanates from the control panel, and the blue light pulsing through the sphere suddenly intensifies.*

*The control panel flares to life, and above it appears a spectral image—a tall, gaunt figure composed of ice and shadow. Though the image flickers and distorts, you can make out a crowned head and long, skeletal fingers that end in ice-like claws.*

*The figure speaks in a language of ice and wind, its words cracking like breaking glaciers. Though the language is alien, something about it resonates with primordial forces of winter and darkness.*

*As the spectral image fades, the core pulses more rapidly, and throughout the sphere, ice begins forming into humanoid shapes—more ice constructs being created to defend the sphere. The message is clear: you have been detected, and the sphere's defenses are activating.*

- Spectral image appears - tall, gaunt figure of ice and shadow
- Image speaks in a language of ice and wind
- If players understand primordial languages: "...network disrupted... intruders detected... deploying guardians..."
- The core pulses rapidly, and ice begins forming humanoid shapes throughout the sphere

### Session Conclusion
Players must decide:
1. Attempt to disable/destroy the sphere
2. Retreat with the information they've gathered

Disabling methods:
- **Direct Damage to Core:** Break through barriers (AC 18, 50 HP)
- **Control Panel Override:** Three Arcana checks (DC 17)
- **Power Conduit Disruption:** Perception (DC 15) to locate key conduits

**Concluding Narration:**
*Time is running short as the ice constructs continue to form. You must decide quickly—attempt to disable the sphere and potentially stop its cold-gathering function, or retreat with the valuable information you've gathered about its purpose and the network it's part of.*

*Either way, it's clear that the threat is greater than just this one sphere. The spectral figure, the map showing multiple locations, the smaller deployment spheres—all point to a coordinated effort to spread extreme cold throughout the mountain region.*

*The knowledge of what you've discovered weighs heavily as you make your decision, aware that whatever path you choose, the consequences will echo far beyond this single valley.*
