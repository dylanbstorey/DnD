# Session 6: The Ashcroft Heist

## Session Overview
This session breaks from the dungeon-crawl pattern with a heist-style adventure. The Stone of Power has ended up in the private collection of an eccentric noble, and the party must infiltrate a high-society gala to steal it. Comedy, disguises, and social chaos ensue — but the arrival of a rival "collector" raises the stakes considerably.

## Background for DM

### The Stone's Journey
The Stone of Power was unearthed decades ago by dwarven miners who didn't recognize its significance. It passed through several hands — traders, scholars, a brief stint in a museum — before Lord Vesper Ashcroft acquired it at auction fifteen years ago. To him, it's simply a beautiful magical curiosity that impresses guests and completes his "Artifacts of the First Age" collection.

### Lord Vesper Ashcroft
A human noble in his late sixties, Lord Ashcroft is obsessively devoted to collecting rare magical items. He's not evil — just profoundly self-absorbed and disconnected from the world's problems. The spreading cold? Terrible for shipping costs. Refugees? Cluttering up the trade roads. The potential end of the world? Well, at least his collection will be preserved in ice for future generations to admire.

He lives in Ashcroft Manor, a sprawling estate above the mountain trading city of Crystalvale. He rarely leaves his galleries and is attended by a small army of servants, guards, and specialists who maintain his collection.

### Lady Rime
A winter fey who ostensibly serves the Frost Architect — but her loyalty is to herself. She's been sent to acquire the Stone of Power, but she has no intention of delivering it to her master. Lady Rime has survived for centuries by playing all sides, and she recognizes that the Frost Architect's victory would make the world boring and cold. She prefers a world with warmth, passion, art, and things worth stealing.

She's attending the gala as "Countess Isadora of the Northern Reaches," a mysterious collector with deep pockets and an interest in cold-themed artifacts.

**Key Insight:** A DC 18 Insight check during conversation with Lady Rime reveals micro-expressions of contempt when the Frost Architect is mentioned, and genuine appreciation for warmth, beauty, and chaos — things her master would destroy.

## Part 1: The Briefing

### Return to the Council
The party returns to the Yeti settlement with two Stones. The combined resonance reveals the location of the third — but not what they expected.

**Dreamspeaker's Vision:**
*Dreamspeaker places her massive hands on the two Stones, and her eyes cloud with milky white light. When she speaks, her voice carries an echo of ages past.*

"The Stone of Power... it no longer rests in the ancient shrine. It was taken long ago by dwarf-folk who did not understand what they held. It has passed through many hands since." *Her brow furrows.* "I see... a great house. Treasures beyond counting. A man who hoards beauty like a dragon hoards gold. The Stone sits in a glass case, admired but not understood."

*She opens her eyes.* "The Stone is in the collection of a human lord. One called... Ashcroft."

### Major's Intelligence
Major's ears perk up. "Ashcroft. I know that name. Lord Vesper Ashcroft — he's famous among the mountain traders. Eccentric collector, never sells anything, throws elaborate parties to show off his acquisitions."

*He tilts his head thoughtfully.* "There's a gala coming up. The 'Winter Solstice Exhibition.' Ashcroft invites wealthy collectors from across the region to view his latest acquisitions. Very exclusive."

**Elder Snowheart:** "Can we not simply explain the situation? Surely this lord would surrender the Stone to prevent catastrophe."

**Major:** *A very dog-like snort.* "Lord Ashcroft once refused to lend a healing artifact to save a dying princess because it would 'disrupt the aesthetic balance of the east gallery.' We're not getting that Stone by asking nicely."

### The Plan Takes Shape
The party needs to infiltrate the gala and steal the Stone. But how?

**Major:** "I've been thinking. The gala attracts wealthy eccentrics from across the region. Ashcroft has never met most of them in person — he corresponds by letter. A dignitary from a distant land, accompanied by... exotic companions... might attract curiosity rather than suspicion."

*He sits up straighter, assuming an almost regal bearing.* "I shall attend as **Lord Majorus of the Eastern Reaches**, a mysterious collector known for my unusual household. You two—" *he looks at Matrim and Madrigal* "—will be my... pets."

**Matrim:** "Your WHAT."

**Major:** "It's fashionable among eccentric collectors to keep unusual companions. A dwarf and an elf of striking appearance would be quite the conversation piece. You'll wear collars. Decorative ones."

*Before Matrim can object further, Major turns to Cheese.* "And you'll need to get in separately. Ashcroft always hires entertainment for these events — acrobats, musicians, performers. A Tabaxi acrobat would be exactly the sort of exotic act he'd want. You can move freely through the servant areas, case the security, and be in position when we make our move."

### Cheese's Angle
**Cheese:** *Already grinning.* "Cheese has performed in worse venues for worse pay. What's the security like?"

**Major:** "Ashcroft employs private guards — well-trained but not magical. The real protection is the wards on his galleries. Magical alarms, locks that can't be picked by normal means, display cases that trigger if tampered with."

*He pauses.* "But during the gala, some of those wards are lowered so guests can admire the collection up close. That's our window."

### The Competition
**Dreamspeaker:** *Her expression darkens.* "There is... another complication. In my vision, I saw a second figure approaching the Stone. A woman of winter. She serves the Frost Architect, but..." *She shakes her head.* "Her heart is clouded. I cannot read her true purpose."

**Major:** "A servant of the Architect, at the gala? That changes things. We may not be the only ones trying to steal that Stone."

## Part 2: Arrival in Crystalvale

### The City
Crystalvale is a prosperous trading city nestled in a mountain valley, built around natural hot springs that keep the climate mild even as winter grips the surrounding peaks. It's become a haven for wealthy refugees fleeing the spreading cold — and a playground for those rich enough to ignore the crisis.

**Read aloud:**
*Crystalvale sprawls across the valley floor in layers of white stone and red tile roofs. Steam rises from the famous hot springs, giving the city a perpetual misty glow. The streets are crowded with merchants, traders, and refugees — but the upper terraces, where the noble estates perch above the common bustle, are immaculate and serene.*

*Ashcroft Manor dominates the highest terrace, a sprawling complex of towers, galleries, and glass-domed conservatories. Even from the city below, you can see guards patrolling the perimeter walls.*

### Preparations
The party has one day before the gala to prepare.

**Available Activities:**

| Activity | Character | Skill Check | Success Result |
|----------|-----------|-------------|----------------|
| Get hired as entertainment | Cheese | Performance DC 12 | Gains staff access, learns servant passages |
| Forge invitation for "Lord Majorus" | Any | Forgery kit or Deception DC 15 | Convincing invitation; failure means extra scrutiny at door |
| Gather intel on security | Cheese or Madrigal | Investigation DC 14 | Learn guard rotation, ward locations |
| Find servant who can be bribed | Cheese | Persuasion DC 13 | Inside contact who can "lose" a key |
| Acquire fancy collars (decorative) | Major | Persuasion DC 10 | Embarrassing but necessary |
| Research Lord Ashcroft's interests | Madrigal | History or Investigation DC 12 | Conversation topics to keep him distracted |
| Scout escape routes | Any | Perception DC 14 | Identify best exit points |

**Cheese's Audition:**
If Cheese attempts to get hired, describe his audition for Ashcroft's event coordinator — a fussy halfling named **Pip Whistledown** who is simultaneously impressed by Cheese's acrobatics and stressed about the catering.

*"Yes, yes, very good, the flip was excellent — can you do it while holding a tray of champagne? No? Well, practice. And for the love of all that's holy, no coughing up hairballs during the string quartet."*

## Part 3: The Gala

### Arrival
The gala begins at sunset. Guests arrive in carriages, palanquins, and in one case, a floating disk of solid gold.

**Major's Entrance:**
*You approach the manor gates. Major walks with regal bearing, wearing a tiny but elegant waistcoat and a small top hat perched between his ears. Beside him, Matrim and Madrigal wear ornate collars connected to silken leashes that Major holds in his mouth.*

*The doorman — a tall, thin human with a practiced expression of polite disdain — examines your invitation.*

"Lord... Majorus? Of the Eastern Reaches?" *He peers at Major.* "I don't believe I've heard of—"

*Major clears his throat and speaks in a refined accent.* "I prefer to keep a low profile. My collection is... specialized. I've corresponded with Lord Ashcroft about his remarkable preservation techniques."

**Doorman Check:**
- If the invitation is well-forged (DC 15 success): The doorman nods and steps aside.
- If the invitation is suspicious (DC 15 failure): The doorman hesitates. Major must make a DC 14 Persuasion or Deception check.
- If Major fails: He's still admitted, but a guard is assigned to "attend" to Lord Majorus throughout the evening (complication).

**The Doorman's Reaction to the "Pets":**
*The doorman's eyes travel to Matrim and Madrigal. His eyebrow rises approximately one millimeter — the maximum expression of surprise permitted by his profession.*

"And these are...?"

**Major:** "My companions. The dwarf is remarkably well-trained — watch this. Matrim, sit."

*Matrim's eye twitches. Through gritted teeth:* "...woof."

*The doorman nods slowly.* "How... delightful. Do ensure they don't mark the furniture."

### The Gala Floor
The party enters a grand ballroom filled with the wealthy, the eccentric, and the insufferable.

**Read aloud:**
*The ballroom glitters with magical lights that drift near the ceiling like captive stars. Guests in elaborate finery cluster around display cases containing bizarre treasures — a skull that whispers secrets, a painting that moves, a sword that hums with barely contained violence.*

*Servants weave through the crowd carrying trays of delicacies. In one corner, a string quartet plays. In another, a Tabaxi acrobat is doing backflips between two posts while guests applaud politely.*

**Key NPCs Present:**

| NPC | Description | Useful For |
|-----|-------------|------------|
| **Lord Vesper Ashcroft** | Elderly human, white beard, talks exclusively about his collection | Distraction, information about the Stone's location |
| **Lady Rime / "Countess Isadora"** | Pale woman with silver-white hair, ice-blue eyes, elegant winter gown | Competition, potential ally (see Insight check) |
| **Pip Whistledown** | Stressed halfling event coordinator | Cheese's handler, can be manipulated |
| **Captain Vorn** | Scarred half-orc head of security | Obstacle, knows all guard positions |
| **Lady Pemberton** | Gossipy human noblewoman | Source of rumors about other guests |
| **The Masked Collector** | Figure in an ornate mask who speaks to no one | Red herring, actually just shy |

### Comedy Beats

**Matrim's Humiliation:**
Throughout the evening, Major occasionally commands Matrim to perform tricks for amused guests.

*"Shake." "Roll over." "Speak."*

Each command requires Matrim to succeed on a DC 10 Charisma (Performance) check to maintain his cover. Failure means he responds too naturally (standing up, speaking in full sentences) and draws suspicion.

**Major's Moments:**
Major must navigate conversations with other collectors who assume he's human and keep asking about his "unique perspective" and "how he maintains such lustrous hair."

Whenever food is served, Major must make a DC 12 Wisdom save to avoid being distracted by the passing canapés.

**Madrigal's Wild Magic:**
The stress of pretending to be a pet, combined with the ambient magic of so many artifacts, means Madrigal's wild magic is especially volatile. 

Any time she casts a spell (even a cantrip) or experiences strong emotion (embarrassment, anger, amusement), roll a d20. On a 1-3, roll on the Wild Magic Surge table. Describe the results as subtle effects she tries to hide — flowers blooming in her hair, her eyes flashing purple, nearby drinks freezing or boiling.

**Cheese's Performance:**
Cheese must perform three times during the evening. Each performance is a DC 13 Performance or Acrobatics check.
- **Success:** Applause, freedom to move around, tips (1d6 gold)
- **Failure:** Pip Whistledown hovers anxiously, restricting movement
- **Natural 1:** Cheese lands in a guest's lap, champagne everywhere, immediate chaos

Between performances, Cheese can case the manor, pick locks, and scout the gallery where the Stone is kept.

## Part 4: The Gallery

### Location of the Stone
The Stone of Power is displayed in the **Vault of Wonders** — Ashcroft's most secure gallery, accessible through a door behind the main ballroom. During the gala, guests are permitted to view the Vault in small groups, escorted by guards.

**The Vault:**
*The Vault of Wonders is a circular chamber with a domed glass ceiling showing the stars above. Display cases line the walls, each containing a treasure: a crown, a grimoire, a crystalline orb. At the center, on a pedestal of black marble, sits a cube of deep blue crystal with glowing runes orbiting it slowly — the Stone of Power.*

*Two guards stand at attention near the door. A third patrols the room's perimeter. Faint magical wards shimmer around each case.*

**Security Details:**
- **Guards:** 3 in the Vault, 2 outside the door, rotating every 30 minutes
- **Wards:** Alarm spell on each case; touching without disabling triggers a silent alert to Captain Vorn
- **The Stone's Case:** Additional ward — opening the case causes the Stone to teleport back inside and the case to lock
- **Weakness:** The wards are keyed to Lord Ashcroft's signet ring. Anyone wearing the ring can open cases freely.

### Getting the Ring
Ashcroft wears the signet ring at all times. Options to acquire it:

| Method | Skill Check | Consequence of Failure |
|--------|-------------|----------------------|
| Pickpocket during conversation | Sleight of Hand DC 18 | Ashcroft notices, calls guards |
| Convince him to remove it (admiring the craftsmanship) | Persuasion DC 16 | He becomes suspicious |
| Spill wine on his hand, "help" clean it | Sleight of Hand DC 14 with setup | Ring falls, everyone notices |
| Madrigal uses Subtle Spell + suggestion | Spell DC 13 WIS save | If he saves, he's confused but not hostile |
| Create a distraction, Cheese grabs it in chaos | Performance DC 15 + Sleight of Hand DC 15 | Partial success possible |

**Alternative:** The party can attempt to disable the wards directly (Arcana DC 18) or brute-force the heist (grab and run), but these approaches trigger alarms and guards.

## Part 5: Lady Rime

### The Encounter
At some point during the evening — ideally when the party is close to making their move — Lady Rime approaches.

**Read aloud:**
*A woman glides toward your group, her silver-white hair cascading over a gown of pale blue that seems to shimmer like frost. Her skin is porcelain-pale, and her eyes are the color of glacier ice. She moves with inhuman grace, and the air around her carries a faint chill.*

"Lord Majorus," *she says, her voice melodic and cold.* "How fascinating to meet a collector of such... unusual tastes." *Her gaze lingers on Matrim and Madrigal with knowing amusement.*

"I am Countess Isadora of the Northern Reaches. I believe we share an interest in a certain... cubic artifact."

### The Conversation
Lady Rime knows they're not what they seem. She's been watching them all evening. She also knows they have two of the Stones — she can sense the resonance.

**Her Goals:**
- Acquire the Stone of Power for herself
- Prevent the party from interfering with her plans
- Avoid direct conflict if possible (she prefers elegant solutions)

**Her Offer:**
"We could fight over the Stone. You might even win — you've proven resourceful. But consider: we both want it taken from this place tonight. Why not... cooperate? You handle the security. I handle the distraction. We meet outside and discuss ownership like civilized beings."

*She smiles, and it doesn't quite reach her eyes.* "The alternative is I alert Lord Ashcroft's guards that thieves have infiltrated his gala. Messy for everyone."

### The Insight Check
During conversation with Lady Rime, any character can attempt an Insight check.

**DC 12:** She's definitely not human. The cold around her is supernatural.

**DC 15:** She's not being entirely honest about her intentions. There's something she's not saying about why she wants the Stone.

**DC 18 (The Key Revelation):**
*When she mentions "her master" or the Frost Architect, you notice the briefest flicker of expression — not loyalty, but contempt. Disgust, even. And when her gaze passes over the warm lights, the laughter, the music... there's something almost like longing.*

*This woman serves the Frost Architect, but she doesn't want him to win.*

**If Confronted:**
If a character calls out her true feelings (requires passing the DC 18 check), Lady Rime's demeanor shifts.

*Her smile fades, replaced by something more genuine — and more dangerous.* "Perceptive. Yes, I serve the Architect. I was *made* to serve him, long ago. But eternity in a world of ice and silence?" *She shakes her head.* "I prefer a world with warmth. With beauty. With things worth taking."

"The Architect wants this Stone to complete his awakening. I want it to ensure he never fully rises — or if he does, that I have leverage to survive what comes after."

*She meets your eyes.* "We have more in common than you might think."

### Options
The party must decide how to handle Lady Rime:

1. **Accept her help:** She creates a distraction (a "fainting spell" that draws guards away), giving them a clean window. They'll have to deal with her afterward.

2. **Refuse and compete:** She becomes an active complication, potentially alerting guards or making her own grab for the Stone.

3. **Attack her:** Combat in the middle of the gala — chaotic, destructive, and likely to end the heist badly.

4. **Negotiate a longer alliance:** If they passed the DC 18 check and appeal to her self-interest, she might agree to help them against the Frost Architect — for a price to be determined later.

## Part 6: The Heist

### The Distraction
Whether Lady Rime helps or not, the party needs a distraction to make their move.

**Possible Distractions:**

| Distraction | Who | Check | Effect |
|-------------|-----|-------|--------|
| Lady Rime's fainting spell | Lady Rime | Automatic if allied | Guards rush to help the "Countess," 3-round window |
| Cheese's "accident" during performance | Cheese | Performance DC 14 | Creates chaos, 2-round window |
| Madrigal triggers Wild Magic intentionally | Madrigal | Roll on table | Unpredictable but attention-grabbing |
| Major loudly accuses another guest of theft | Major | Deception DC 15 | Argument draws guards, 2-round window |
| Matrim "escapes his leash" and causes havoc | Matrim | Athletics DC 12 | Funny chaos, 1-round window |
| Set off a minor alarm elsewhere | Any | Thieves' Tools DC 14 | Guards investigate, 2-round window |

### The Grab
With the distraction active, the designated thief (probably Cheese) must:

1. **Enter the Vault** — Stealth DC 13 if guards are distracted
2. **Approach the Stone** — Stealth DC 14 (one guard remains)
3. **Disable or bypass the ward:**
   - With ring: Automatic success
   - Without ring: Arcana DC 18, or Thieves' Tools DC 20, or take the alarm
4. **Grab the Stone** — No check required
5. **Exit** — Stealth DC 14, or Acrobatics DC 15 to escape through window/vent

### When Things Go Wrong
Because they will. Roll or choose:

| d6 | Complication |
|----|--------------|
| 1 | A guard wasn't as distracted as expected — combat with 1 guard |
| 2 | The ward triggers a secondary effect — the Stone is HOT (1d6 fire damage per round held bare-handed) |
| 3 | Lord Ashcroft wanders into the Vault looking for a guest to bore |
| 4 | Cheese's escape route is blocked by servants carrying a giant ice sculpture |
| 5 | Madrigal's Wild Magic triggers at the worst moment |
| 6 | Lady Rime double-crosses them (if allied) OR makes her own grab (if competing) |

## Part 7: The Escape

### Getting Out
Once the Stone is grabbed, the party has limited time before full lockdown.

**Round 1-2:** Confusion — guards investigating the distraction
**Round 3-4:** Alert spreading — "The Stone is missing!"
**Round 5+:** Full lockdown — all exits guarded, Captain Vorn hunting

**Escape Options:**

| Route | Check | Risk |
|-------|-------|------|
| Front door (brazen) | Deception DC 16 ("What chaos! We're leaving!") | Guards may not buy it |
| Servant passages | Stealth DC 14 (Cheese knows the route) | May encounter staff |
| Balcony and climb down | Acrobatics DC 13, Athletics DC 12 | Spotted by perimeter guards |
| Through the conservatory (glass dome) | Break glass + Acrobatics DC 14 | Very loud |
| Lady Rime's carriage (if allied) | Persuasion already handled | She's coming with you |

### The Chase
If the alarm is raised, a chase sequence ensues:

**Chase Mechanics (simplified):**
- Party has a 2-round head start
- Each round, each character makes a check (Athletics, Acrobatics, Stealth, or creative alternative)
- DC 13 to maintain lead, DC 16 to gain ground
- 3 successes = clean escape
- 3 failures = cornered (but not caught — transition to Part 8)

**Chase Complications (roll each round):**

| d4 | Event |
|----|-------|
| 1 | Narrow alley — squeeze through (Athletics DC 12) or find another route |
| 2 | Crowded market — easy to hide (Stealth advantage) but slow going |
| 3 | Guard patrol ahead — Stealth DC 14 or fight 2 guards |
| 4 | Dead end — climb (Athletics DC 14) or break through door (Strength DC 15) |

## Part 8: Lady Rime's Resolution

### If She Helped
Lady Rime expects the party to honor their implicit agreement — a conversation about the Stone's fate.

**At a safe location:**
*Lady Rime examines the Stone of Power with undisguised appreciation.* "Beautiful. I can feel the Architect's prison weakening with each sphere that activates. This Stone could sustain him for decades — or, in the right hands, help ensure he never fully wakes."

**Her Proposal:**
"Keep your Stone. Use it for your ritual. But when you face the Architect, remember: I am not your enemy. I am simply... self-interested."

*She produces a small crystal snowflake pendant.* "Break this if you need me. I make no promises, but I do so enjoy being on the winning side."

**The Gift:** The **Rime Pendant** allows the party to summon Lady Rime once. She'll help for exactly one combat or one skill challenge before departing. (Useful in Module 7.)

### If She Competed
Lady Rime intercepts the party outside the manor.

**Combat Option:** If the party wants to fight, she's a challenging encounter:

**Lady Rime (Winter Fey)**
- AC 16, HP 90
- **Multiattack:** Two frost claw attacks
- **Frost Claw:** +7 to hit, 1d8+4 cold damage
- **Ice Shard:** Ranged spell attack, +6 to hit, 2d6 cold damage
- **Winter's Kiss (Recharge 5-6):** 30-foot cone, DC 15 CON save, 4d8 cold damage and speed halved
- **Misty Escape:** When reduced to half HP, can teleport 30 feet and turn invisible until end of next turn

**Negotiation Option:** Even as a competitor, she'd rather talk than fight. The DC 18 Insight revelation can still happen here, leading to a tense truce.

### If She Was Ignored
Lady Rime acquired the Stone by other means while the party was distracted. The party must track her down (skill challenge) and either fight her or negotiate. This extends the session but provides a satisfying conclusion.

## Part 9: Conclusion

### The Third Stone
However the heist resolves, the party should end the session with the Stone of Power in their possession.

**Stone Resonance:**
*As you bring the third Stone near the others, all three pulse with brilliant light. The resonance is almost overwhelming now — a harmonic vibration you feel in your bones. Images flash through your mind: a mountain path descending into darkness, ancient doors covered in ice, a chamber where cold light pulses like a heartbeat.*

*The path to the Chamber of Binding — and the final Stone — is now clear.*

### Return to the Council
The Yeti Council receives news of the successful heist with a mixture of relief and concern.

**Dreamspeaker:** "Three Stones gathered. One remains — the Stone of Binding itself, held within the Chamber where the original ritual was performed. The Frost Architect guards it directly now. His prison and his power are one and the same."

**Major:** "We have what we need. One more journey — into the Forbidden Peaks, into the heart of the cold. And then... we end this."

### Setting Up Module 7
**Dreamspeaker's Warning:**
"The Architect knows you come. He has called his servants home — the Frost General, the remaining constructs, everything that serves him. The path to the Chamber will not be unguarded."

*She pauses.* "And the ritual itself... I have told you it requires sacrifice. I cannot say what price will be demanded. Only that the binding cannot be renewed without cost."

## DM Notes

### Tone Guidance
This session should be significantly lighter than the others until the Lady Rime confrontation. Lean into the comedy of the disguises, the absurdity of high society, and the indignity of the "pet" situation. The heist itself should feel tense but fun — closer to a caper than a dungeon crawl.

The Lady Rime revelation adds depth and sets up a potential ally/wildcard for the finale. Don't rush it — let the players engage with her and make meaningful choices.

### Adjusting Difficulty
- **If the party struggles with social checks:** Provide more NPC assistance (a sympathetic servant, a distraction they didn't plan)
- **If the heist is too easy:** Add complications from the table; have Lady Rime be more aggressive
- **If Lady Rime's insight check fails:** She can still become an ambiguous figure; just don't reveal her full motivations yet

### Callbacks and Foreshadowing
- Cheese's prison break experience should be referenced — "This is easier than the last place"
- Any nobles at the party could mention the spreading cold, refugees, strange weather
- If Matrim talks to dwarven guests, they might mention Frosthold or recognize his clan
- The Stone of Power's resonance with Matrim's Frost Rage could be noted — it responds to him

### Alternative Paths
- **If the party refuses to do the heist:** They could try diplomacy (very hard), burglary without the gala cover (harder), or waiting for Lady Rime to steal it and ambushing her (morally complex)
- **If everything goes catastrophically wrong:** They flee Crystalvale as wanted criminals, but Lady Rime contacts them later with a deal
