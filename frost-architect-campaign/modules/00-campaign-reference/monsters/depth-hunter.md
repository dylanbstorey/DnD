# Depth Hunter
*Large monstrosity, neutral evil*

![Depth Hunter](A massive serpentine creature with the upper body of a predatory fish and the lower body of an eel, its scales a mix of translucent ice and midnight blue, with glowing blue eyes and rows of crystalline fangs)

**Armor Class:** 16 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 85 (10d10 + 30)  
**Speed:** 20 ft., swim 60 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 22 (+6) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 7 (-2) | 14 (+2) | 8 (-1) |

**Saving Throws:** Str +9, Con +6  
**Skills:** Perception +5, Stealth +5  
**Damage Immunities:** cold  
**Damage Resistances:** bludgeoning  
**Condition Immunities:** frightened, frozen  
**Senses:** darkvision 120 ft., tremorsense 60 ft. (ice only), passive Perception 15  
**Languages:** understands Primordial but cannot speak  
**Challenge:** 5 (1,800 XP)

***Ice Swim.*** The depth hunter can move through ice as if it were water, leaving no trace of its passage. It can breathe both water and air.

***Shadow in the Depths.*** While fully submerged in water or ice, the depth hunter has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks and can take the Hide action as a bonus action.

***Freezing Body.*** A creature that touches the depth hunter or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 5 (1d10) cold damage.

***Keen Hearing and Smell.*** The depth hunter has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.

#### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The depth hunter makes two claw attacks.

***Ice Breach.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 15 (2d10 + 4) slashing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be pulled into the water or ice. If pulled into ice, the target takes an additional 7 (2d6) cold damage and is grappled (escape DC 15).

***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 22 (3d10 + 6) piercing damage plus 5 (1d10) cold damage.

***Frost Breath (Recharge 5-6).*** The depth hunter exhales a 30-foot cone of freezing wind and water. Each creature in that area must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw, taking 21 (6d6) cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. A creature that fails its save is also restrained by ice until the end of its next turn.

#### Legendary Actions
The depth hunter can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The depth hunter regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

***Perceive.*** The depth hunter makes a Wisdom (Perception) check.

***Swift Swim.*** The depth hunter moves up to half its swimming speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

***Ice Crack (Costs 2 Actions).*** The depth hunter causes the ice in a 15-foot radius to crack and buckle. Each creature standing on the ice in that area must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or fall prone. Until the start of the depth hunter's next turn, that area is difficult terrain.

#### Tactics

- The depth hunter stalks prey from beneath the ice before striking
- It uses Ice Breach to isolate and drag vulnerable targets into the water
- It creates ice cracks to disable multiple opponents at once
- Against strong opponents, it retreats into the ice after each attack
- It targets creatures showing signs of cold vulnerability first

#### Description

The Depth Hunter is a terrifying predator that haunts the frozen lake within the Caverns of Echo. Neither fully fish nor serpent, this ancient creature has adapted to the magical cold of the caverns, evolving the ability to swim through solid ice as easily as water.

Its body is approximately twenty feet long, with a muscular upper torso featuring powerful clawed limbs, transitioning to a serpentine lower body ideal for powerful swimming. Its head combines fish-like and reptilian features, with multiple rows of crystalline teeth capable of puncturing armor. Its scales shimmer with an unnatural blue glow, allowing it to blend with the ice when still.

The Depth Hunter is territorial and cunning, understanding the concepts of ambush and retreat. It has learned that the ice surface sometimes supports prey it cannot reach, leading it to develop tactics to break through and drag victims into its watery domain.

This specimen has been altered by the Frost Architect's cold energy, granting it enhanced intelligence and making it a purposeful guardian rather than merely a territorial predator.
