# Frost Wolf
*Medium monstrosity, neutral evil*

![Character Portrait](Wolf with frost-rimed fur and glowing blue eyes)

**Armor Class:** 13 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 18 (4d8)  
**Speed:** 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 12 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 4 (-3) | 12 (+1) | 6 (-2) |

**Skills:** Perception +3, Stealth +4  
**Damage Resistances:** cold  
**Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13  
**Languages:** —  
**Challenge:** 1/2 (100 XP)

***Keen Hearing and Smell.*** The wolf has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing or smell.

***Pack Tactics.*** The wolf has advantage on attack rolls against a creature if at least one of the wolf's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.

***Cold Aura.*** At the start of each of the wolf's turns, each creature within 5 feet of it takes 2 (1d4) cold damage.

***Snow Stride.*** The wolf ignores difficult terrain caused by snow or ice.

#### Actions

***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (2d4 + 2) piercing damage plus 2 (1d4) cold damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 11 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

***Frost Howl (Recharge 6).*** The wolf lets out a bone-chilling howl. Each creature within 30 feet that can hear the wolf must succeed on a DC 11 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened until the end of its next turn.

#### Tactics
- Frost wolves always attack in packs, using Pack Tactics
- They try to surround prey, with some wolves knocking targets prone while others attack
- They are unnaturally coordinated and will protect the Frost-Infected Bear if present
- They use Frost Howl to frighten targets before closing to attack

#### Description
Frost wolves are normal wolves that have been corrupted by the Frost Architect's cold energy. Their fur is rimmed with frost that never melts, and their eyes glow with an eerie blue light. When they breathe, small ice crystals form in the air, and their howl carries an unnatural resonance that chills the soul as much as the body.
