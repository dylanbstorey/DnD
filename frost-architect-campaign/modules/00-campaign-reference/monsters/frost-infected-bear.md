# Frost-Infected Bear
*Large monstrosity, neutral evil*

![Character Portrait](Massive bear with fur crusted in ice crystals and glowing blue eyes)

**Armor Class:** 14 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 42 (5d10 + 15)  
**Speed:** 40 ft., climb 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 19 (+4) | 10 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 4 (-3) | 13 (+1) | 7 (-2) |

**Skills:** Perception +3  
**Damage Resistances:** cold  
**Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13  
**Languages:** —  
**Challenge:** 3 (700 XP)

***Keen Smell.*** The bear has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.

***Frost Infection.*** Any creature that touches the bear or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or take 2 (1d4) cold damage.

***Cold Fury.*** When the bear drops to half its hit points or fewer, it enters a state of cold fury. While in cold fury, the bear deals an extra 4 (1d8) cold damage with its attacks and has advantage on Constitution saving throws.

#### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The bear makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.

***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) cold damage.

***Claws.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage.

***Frost Breath (Recharge 5-6).*** The bear exhales a 15-foot cone of freezing wind. Each creature in that area must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

#### Tactics
- The frost-infected bear is unnaturally aggressive and territorial
- It uses Frost Breath as an opening attack when possible
- When injured, it becomes even more dangerous due to Cold Fury
- It coordinates with frost wolves if they're present

#### Description
Once a normal mountain bear, this creature has been transformed by the Frost Architect's cold energy. Its fur is crusted with ice crystals that seem to grow directly from its skin, and its eyes glow with an unnatural blue light. Frost forms with each breath it takes, and its roar carries the sound of cracking ice. The infection has made it larger, stronger, and far more aggressive than a normal bear.
