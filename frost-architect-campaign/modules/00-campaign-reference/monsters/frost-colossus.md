# Frost Colossus
## Elite Guardian of the Frost Architect

- **Damage Immunities**: Cold, poison

### Special Traits

#### Frost Aura
At the start of the Frost Colossus's turn, all creatures within 10 feet take 2d6 cold damage and must make a DC 14 Constitution save or have their speed reduced by half until the start of their next turn.

#### Siege Monster
The Colossus deals double damage to objects and structures.

#### Ice Walking
The Colossus can move across and climb icy surfaces without needing to make an ability check. Additionally, difficult terrain composed of ice or snow doesn't cost it extra movement.

#### Crystalline Nodes
The Colossus has four power nodes (shoulders, chest, and head) that can be targeted separately:
- Each node has AC 18 and 25 HP
- Destroying a node disables a specific ability
- Critical hits can target a node if declared before the attack
- Any attack dealing 20+ damage can target a node (DC 16 Dexterity save by the Colossus to avoid)

#### Shatter Form
When reduced to half HP, the Colossus shatters into 3 Medium-sized Ice Elementals, each with 40 HP.

### Actions

#### Multiattack
The Frost Colossus makes two slam attacks.

#### Slam
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.  
*Hit:* 3d8+6 (19-30) bludgeoning damage plus 1d8 (4-8) cold damage.

#### Ice Shards (Ranged Attack)
*Ranged Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, range 60/120 ft., one target.  
*Hit:* 2d8+4 (6-20) piercing damage plus 1d8 (1-8) cold damage.

#### Ground Freeze (Recharge 5-6)
As an action, the Colossus freezes the ground in a 20-foot radius. This area becomes difficult terrain, and creatures starting their turn there must make a DC 14 Dexterity save or fall prone.

#### Absorb Cold (Bonus Action)
The Colossus can absorb cold energy to heal 2d8+4 HP. If a cold spell targets the Colossus or includes it in its area of effect, the Colossus takes no damage and instead regains hit points equal to half the damage the spell would have dealt.

### Legendary Actions
The Frost Colossus can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The Frost Colossus regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

- **Detect**: The Frost Colossus makes a Wisdom (Perception) check.
- **Ice Shard**: The Frost Colossus makes an Ice Shard attack.
- **Frost Step (Costs 2 Actions)**: The Frost Colossus moves up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

### Reactions
- **Ice Shield**: When the Frost Colossus is hit by an attack, it can create a shield of ice, adding +5 to its AC against that attack. If the attack still hits, the Frost Colossus takes only half damage from it.

### Description
The Frost Colossus is an elite guardian created by the Frost Architect to protect important locations. Standing fifteen feet tall, this imposing figure is composed of magically reinforced ice with embedded crystalline nodes that pulse with cold energy.

Unlike mindless constructs, the Frost Colossus possesses a measure of intelligence and can speak, though its conversations are typically limited to threats and declarations of the Frost Architect's power. It can follow complex commands and make tactical decisions during combat.

The glowing nodes on its body are directly connected to the sphere network, allowing the Frost Architect to observe through its eyes and occasionally speak through it. These nodes are also its weak points - destroying them significantly reduces its combat effectiveness.

### Tactics
- Target spellcasters first with ranged attacks
- Use Ground Freeze when multiple enemies are grouped together
- Absorb Cold whenever below 75% health
- Use Frost Aura to weaken melee attackers
- When shattered into elementals, surround and focus on a single target
- Speak phrases in Common to intimidate or distract opponents

### Ice Elemental Stats (Shattered Form)
- **Armor Class**: 14
- **Hit Points**: 40 each
- **Speed**: 40 ft.
- **Special Abilities**: Ice Walk, Frost Form (1d6 cold damage on touch)
- **Actions**: Multiattack (two slams), Slam (+5, 2d6+3 bludgeoning plus 1d6 cold), Frost Breath (15-ft cone, DC 13 Con save, 3d6 cold damage)

### DM Notes
- The node weakness mechanic gives players a tactical advantage if they're observant
- If the party struggles, consider having Major point out the nodes
- For less experienced parties, the Colossus might not use all its legendary actions
- The shattered form provides a different tactical challenge - more targets but each is less powerful
- Fire damage is particularly effective and should be encouraged
