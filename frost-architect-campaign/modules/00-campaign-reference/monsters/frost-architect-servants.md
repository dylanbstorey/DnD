# Frost Architect's Servants
## Stat Blocks for Session 1

### Ice Scout
*Medium elemental construct, neutral evil*

**Armor Class:** 14 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 22 (4d6 + 4)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 13 (+1) | 8 (-1) | 12 (+1) | 6 (-2) |

**Skills:** Perception +3, Stealth +5  
**Damage Resistances:** cold, bludgeoning from nonmagical attacks  
**Damage Immunities:** poison  
**Condition Immunities:** exhaustion, poisoned  
**Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13  
**Languages:** understands Primordial but cannot speak  
**Challenge:** 1/2 (100 XP)

***Detect Life.*** The ice scout can sense the presence of warm-blooded creatures within 60 feet of it.

***Ice Camouflage.*** The ice scout has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide in snowy or icy terrain.

#### Actions

***Ice Blade.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 2) slashing damage plus 2 (1d4) cold damage.

***Ice Shard (1/day).*** The ice scout fires a shard of magical ice at a target it can see within 30 feet. The target must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

#### Description
Ice Scouts are sleek, humanoid constructs made of translucent ice with angular, faceted features and glowing blue eyes. They serve as the Frost Architect's reconnaissance units, patrolling the perimeter around frost spheres and other installations. They move swiftly and silently, their crystalline bodies blending perfectly with snowy environments.

When they detect intruders, Ice Scouts typically attempt to alert their allies rather than engaging directly. They prefer to attack from ambush, using their natural camouflage to gain advantage.

### Enhanced Winter Wight
*Medium undead, chaotic evil*

**Armor Class:** 12 (ice armor)  
**Hit Points:** 32 (5d8 + 10)  
**Speed:** 25 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 13 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 6 (-2) | 10 (+0) | 5 (-3) |

**Damage Immunities:** cold, poison  
**Damage Resistances:** necrotic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks  
**Condition Immunities:** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, poisoned  
**Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10  
**Languages:** understands the languages it knew in life but can't speak  
**Challenge:** 1 (200 XP)

***Enhanced Cold Aura.*** At the start of each of the winter wight's turns, each creature within 5 feet of it takes 3 (1d6) cold damage.

***Ice Armor.*** The first hit of each combat against the wight deals half damage (rounded down).

***Snow Walker.*** The winter wight ignores difficult terrain caused by ice or snow.

***Sunlight Sensitivity.*** While in sunlight, the winter wight has disadvantage on attack rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.

#### Actions

***Frozen Claw.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) cold damage.

***Chilling Groan (Recharge 5-6).*** The winter wight releases a terrible moan filled with the despair of frozen deaths. Each creature within 30 feet that can hear the wight must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened for 1 minute. A frightened creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

#### Description
Enhanced Winter Wights are more powerful variants of the standard winter wight, bolstered by the Frost Architect's cold energy. They appear as skeletal humanoid figures with armor-like formations of ice covering their frames, giving them a more substantial and menacing presence.

Their internal cold is so intense that it radiates outward, harming any creature that comes too close. Unlike normal winter wights that mindlessly seek warmth, these enhanced versions are more tactical and coordinated, capable of working together to trap and eliminate threats.

### Ice Guardian
*Large elemental construct, lawful evil*

**Armor Class:** 16 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 45 (6d10 + 12)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 18 (+4) | 13 (+1) | 15 (+2) | 8 (-1) | 10 (+0) | 7 (-2) |

**Damage Resistances:** bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks  
**Damage Immunities:** cold, poison  
**Condition Immunities:** exhaustion, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned  
**Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10  
**Languages:** understands Primordial but cannot speak  
**Challenge:** 2 (450 XP)

***Icy Body.*** A creature that touches the ice guardian or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 3 (1d6) cold damage.

***Immutable Form.*** The ice guardian is immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.

***Magic Resistance.*** The ice guardian has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

#### Actions

***Ice Blade.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (2d6 + 6) slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) cold damage.

***Ice Javelin.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) cold damage.

#### Reactions

***Frost Shield.*** When an attacker the ice guardian can see hits it with an attack, the guardian can reduce the damage by 1d10 + 2.

#### Description
Ice Guardians are imposing constructs of jagged ice that stand eight feet tall. They have no discernible facial features except for glowing blue eyes, and their arms end in massive ice blades capable of slicing through armor and flesh with ease.

These constructs serve as elite guards for the Frost Architect's most important installations, including the frost spheres. They are significantly more intelligent than other ice constructs, capable of basic tactics and coordinated defense. They typically work in pairs, with one engaging enemies in melee while the other attacks from range with ice javelins.

The magical cold that animates them is so intense that merely touching an Ice Guardian can cause frostbite. Their bodies are also capable of absorbing some of the force from attacks, represented by their Frost Shield reaction.
