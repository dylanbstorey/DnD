# Winter Wight
## Frozen Spirits of the Forgotten

![Winter Wight Description](Skeletal humanoid figure wrapped in tattered, frost-covered rags with glowing blue eyes)

### Basic Information
- **Type**: Undead
- **Challenge Rating**: 1 (appropriate for low-level adventurers)
- **Environment**: Arctic, frozen wastelands, abandoned settlements
- **Organization**: Solitary or small groups (1-4)
- **Alignment**: Chaotic Evil

### Stats
- **Armor Class**: 12 (ice-hardened rags)
- **Hit Points**: 22 (4d8 + 4)
- **Speed**: 25 ft.

### Immunities, Resistances, and Vulnerabilities
- **Damage Immunities**: Cold, poison
- **Damage Resistances**: Necrotic; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Damage Vulnerabilities**: Fire
- **Condition Immunities**: Charmed, exhaustion, frightened, poisoned
- **Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9
- **Languages**: Understands the languages it knew in life but can't speak

### Special Traits

#### Undead Nature
The winter wight doesn't require air, food, drink, or sleep.

#### Cold Aura
At the start of each of the winter wight's turns, each creature within 5 feet of it takes 2 (1d4) cold damage.

#### Snow Walker
The winter wight ignores difficult terrain caused by ice or snow.

#### Sunlight Sensitivity
While in sunlight, the winter wight has disadvantage on attack rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.

### Actions

#### Frozen Claw
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
*Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing damage plus 2 (1d4) cold damage.

#### Chilling Groan (Recharge 5-6)
The winter wight releases a terrible moan filled with the despair of frozen deaths. Each creature within 30 feet that can hear the wight must succeed on a DC 10 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened for 1 minute. A frightened creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Description

Winter wights are the restless spirits of those who died from exposure in the frozen wilderness. Drawn to places of abandonment and despair, they seek warmth and life to fill the emptiness that consumes them.

These spectral beings appear as humanoid figures with skeletal limbs wrapped in tattered, frost-covered rags. Glowing blue eyes burn in their skull-like faces, and frost forms in the air around them. Their bony fingers end in icy claws that can drain the warmth from the living.

### Tactics

Winter wights are not particularly intelligent but act with an instinctive cunning:

- They prefer to attack from hiding, emerging from snowdrifts or behind ice formations
- Multiple wights will spread out to maximize the effect of their cold auras
- They instinctively target the weakest-looking members of a group
- Wights prefer to attack at night when they don't suffer from sunlight sensitivity
- They retreat from open flames and areas of extreme heat

### Adventure Hooks

1. **The Abandoned Settlement**: Winter wights have been drawn to a recently abandoned settlement. Their presence prevents the rightful inhabitants from returning.

2. **Eternal Cold**: The mere presence of winter wights has caused an unnatural cold to spread throughout the region. Plants are dying and animals are fleeing. The wights must be dispatched to restore the natural balance.

3. **Lost Travelers**: A group of travelers went missing during a blizzard. Search parties fear they've become winter wights and must now be laid to rest rather than rescued.

### DM Notes

- For younger or more sensitive players, emphasize that these creatures can be put to rest rather than "killed"
- Winter wights make eerie crackling and groaning sounds as they move
- The blue glow from their eyes can be spotted from a distance in dark conditions
- Tracks left by winter wights appear as light frost patterns rather than footprints
- They leave behind small piles of glittering snow when defeated
