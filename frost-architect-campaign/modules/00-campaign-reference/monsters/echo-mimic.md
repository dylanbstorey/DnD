# Echo Mimic
*Medium monstrosity, neutral evil*

![Echo Mimic](A shimmering, translucent humanoid figure with a constantly shifting form that mimics those it encounters, its surface rippling like water when it moves, with faint blue crystalline patterns visible underneath its copied appearance)

**Armor Class:** 14 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 36 (8d8)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 12 (+1) | 18 (+4) | 10 (+0) | 13 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) |

**Skills:** Deception +6, Perception +5, Stealth +7  
**Damage Resistances:** cold, thunder  
**Condition Immunities:** charmed  
**Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 15  
**Languages:** understands Common and Primordial but cannot speak, mimics sounds instead  
**Challenge:** 2 (450 XP)

***Mimic Voice.*** The echo mimic can perfectly imitate any sound or voice it has heard, including complex speech patterns and vocalizations. Creatures hearing these sounds must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom (Insight) check to determine they are imitations.

***False Appearance.*** The echo mimic can use its action to polymorph into a Small or Medium humanoid it has seen, or back into its true form. Its statistics, other than its size, are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying transforms with it. It reverts to its true form if it dies.

***Acoustic Advantage.*** While in the Caverns of Echo, the echo mimic has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks and can use the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action as a bonus action on each of its turns.

***Amplified Presence.*** The echo mimic can create illusory duplicates of its mimicked form that appear up to 30 feet away from it. These duplicates make no sound and disappear when touched, but can be used to confuse enemies. Creating or maintaining these duplicates requires concentration.

#### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The echo mimic makes two touch of dissonance attacks.

***Touch of Dissonance.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) cold damage plus 4 (1d8) thunder damage.

***Disorienting Echo (Recharge 5-6).*** The echo mimic releases a disorienting wave of sound in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, a creature is confused until the end of its next turn. While confused, the creature has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks, and can't use reactions.

***Echoing Lure (3/day).*** The echo mimic creates an auditory illusion that sounds like a creature or sound familiar to the target. One creature the mimic can see within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or be compelled to move up to its speed in a direction of the mimic's choice. The target doesn't move into obviously dangerous ground, such as a fire or pit. A creature that successfully saves against this effect is immune to this mimic's Echoing Lure for 24 hours.

#### Tactics

- Echo mimics prefer to separate party members using their Echoing Lure ability
- They use their False Appearance to impersonate a party member, creating confusion
- In combat, they use Disorienting Echo to confuse multiple targets at once
- They leverage the cavern's acoustic properties to hide and reposition
- When severely wounded, they attempt to flee and ambush from a different direction

#### Description

Echo Mimics are mysterious entities that haunt the Caverns of Echo, created or warped by the combination of ancient magic and the Frost Architect's cold energy. They appear as shimmering, translucent humanoids with fluid forms that can perfectly mimic the appearance of any humanoid creature they encounter.

Unlike traditional mimics, Echo Mimics don't disguise themselves as objects—instead, they copy people, using the caverns' unusual acoustics to perfectly imitate voices and sounds. This allows them to separate groups by luring individuals with familiar voices, or to infiltrate parties by posing as separated members.

Their true form resembles a crystalline humanoid figure with a rippling, water-like surface. When they attack, their limbs temporarily solidify into resonating crystalline structures that deliver painful cold and sound-based damage.

Echo Mimics work instinctively to protect the Caverns of Echo from intruders, but some exhibit greater intelligence and may attempt to communicate using a patchwork of mimicked phrases from previous victims.
