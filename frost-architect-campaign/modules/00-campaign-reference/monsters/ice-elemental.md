# Ice Elemental
## Shattered Form of the Frost Colossus

![Ice Elemental Description](A humanoid figure about eight feet tall composed of jagged ice with a vaguely defined head and limbs. Blue energy flows through cracks in its crystalline body)

### Basic Information
- **Type**: Elemental
- **Challenge Rating**: 2 (450 XP)
- **Environment**: Arctic, sphere locations
- **Organization**: Groups (1-3)
- **Alignment**: Neutral Evil

### Stats
- **Armor Class**: 14
- **Hit Points**: 40 (9d8 + 9)
- **Speed**: 40 ft.

### Immunities, Resistances, and Vulnerabilities
- **Damage Immunities**: Cold, poison
- **Condition Immunities**: Poisoned
- **Damage Vulnerabilities**: Fire
- **Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Languages**: Primordial

### Special Traits

#### Ice Walk
The elemental can move across and climb icy surfaces without needing to make an ability check. Additionally, difficult terrain composed of ice or snow doesn't cost it extra movement.

#### Frost Form
A creature that touches the elemental or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 1d6 cold damage.

### Actions

#### Multiattack
The elemental makes two slam attacks.

#### Slam
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
*Hit:* 2d6+3 (5-15) bludgeoning damage plus 1d6 (1-6) cold damage.

#### Frost Breath (Recharge 5-6)
The elemental exhales a 15-foot cone of freezing wind. Each creature in that area must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw, taking 3d6 (3-18) cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### Description
When the Frost Colossus is severely damaged, it can shatter into multiple smaller entities known as Ice Elementals. These more agile constructs retain a portion of the Colossus's power but are individually less formidable.

Ice Elementals appear as roughly humanoid figures composed of jagged, translucent ice. Blue energy flows through cracks in their crystalline bodies, and they move with surprising speed and grace despite their seemingly solid forms.

Though smaller than their original form, Ice Elementals are still dangerous opponents. Their bodies emanate intense cold, harming anyone who comes into direct contact with them, and they can exhale freezing breath that can rapidly induce frostbite.

### Tactics
- Surround and focus on a single target
- Use speed advantage to flank enemies
- Coordinate attacks with other elementals
- Use Frost Breath when multiple opponents are clustered together
- Exploit their immunity to cold by attacking through freezing terrain

### DM Notes
- Ice Elementals are meant to be fought as a group rather than individually
- They work well tactically and focus fire on vulnerable targets
- Their vulnerability to fire continues from the Frost Colossus form
- They're faster than the Colossus, making them harder to escape from
- Consider having them pursue fleeing characters more aggressively
