# Frost Sentinel
*Large elemental construct, lawful evil*

![Frost Sentinel](A towering humanoid figure made of jagged ice with enhanced features, more angular and crystalline than standard Ice Guardians, with intense blue energy flowing through complex patterns across its surface)

**Armor Class:** 17 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 65 (10d10 + 10)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 19 (+4) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 8 (-1) |

**Saving Throws:** Str +7, Con +6  
**Damage Resistances:** bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks  
**Damage Immunities:** cold, poison  
**Condition Immunities:** exhaustion, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned  
**Senses:** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11  
**Languages:** understands Primordial but cannot speak  
**Challenge:** 4 (1,100 XP)

***Icy Body.*** A creature that touches the frost sentinel or hits it with a melee attack while within 5 feet of it takes 3 (1d6) cold damage.

***Immutable Form.*** The frost sentinel is immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.

***Magic Resistance.*** The frost sentinel has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Echo Sensitivity.*** The frost sentinel has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks based on sound within the Caverns of Echo.

#### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The frost sentinel makes two ice blade attacks.

***Ice Blade.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) cold damage.

***Ice Spear.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, range 60/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d10 + 3) piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) cold damage.

***Frost Barrier (1/day).*** The frost sentinel creates a wall of ice on a solid surface within 60 feet. The wall is up to 15 feet long, 10 feet high, and 1 foot thick. Each 10-foot section of the wall has AC 12, 30 hit points, vulnerability to fire damage, and immunity to cold, poison, and psychic damage. When the wall appears, each creature within its area must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 10 (3d6) cold damage and is pushed 5 feet out of the wall's space. The wall lasts for 1 minute or until the frost sentinel creates another frost barrier.

#### Reactions

***Frost Shield.*** When an attacker the frost sentinel can see hits it with an attack, the sentinel can reduce the damage by 1d10 + 3.

#### Tactics

- Frost sentinels are more intelligent than standard ice guardians and work in coordinated pairs
- They prefer to engage spellcasters first, recognizing them as greater threats
- When guarding a location, they position themselves to funnel attackers into choke points
- They use Frost Barrier to divide enemy groups or block escape routes
- If one sentinel is heavily damaged, its partner will try to protect it while it retreats to a defensive position

#### Description

Frost Sentinels are advanced ice guardians created specifically to protect important locations in the Frost Architect's domain. Standing over nine feet tall, these constructs feature more complex ice formations than standard ice guardians, with intricate crystalline patterns that channel and amplify cold energy.

Unlike regular ice guardians, Frost Sentinels possess a rudimentary intelligence that allows them to make tactical decisions and communicate silently with each other. Their bodies are reinforced with denser magical ice, making them more resistant to damage and allowing them to create barriers of magical frost.

Within the Caverns of Echo, Frost Sentinels are specially attuned to the unusual acoustic properties of the environment, allowing them to pinpoint intruders by sound alone even when visibility is poor.
