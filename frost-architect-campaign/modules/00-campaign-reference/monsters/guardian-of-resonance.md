# Guardian of Resonance
*Large elemental, neutral*

![Guardian of Resonance](A towering humanoid figure composed entirely of vibrating crystalline formations that pulse with inner light in response to sound, with a featureless face except for numerous small crystalline shards that act as sensory organs)

**Armor Class:** 18 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 110 (13d10 + 39)  
**Speed:** 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 20 (+5) | 16 (+3) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 18 (+4) | 18 (+4) |

**Saving Throws:** Con +7, Wis +8, Cha +8  
**Skills:** Perception +8, Insight +8  
**Damage Immunities:** cold, poison, psychic  
**Damage Resistances:** bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks  
**Damage Vulnerabilities:** None  
**Condition Immunities:** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned  
**Senses:** blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius), passive Perception 18  
**Languages:** understands Common and Primordial but communicates only through harmonic resonance  
**Challenge:** 7 (2,900 XP)

***Sound Perception.*** The guardian cannot see. Instead, it has blindsight that relies on detecting sound and vibrations. It is blind beyond the range of its blindsight.

***Immutable Form.*** The guardian is immune to any spell or effect that would alter its form.

***Acoustic Resonance.*** The guardian is perfectly attuned to the Chamber of Resonance. If it makes a Wisdom (Perception) check to locate a creature in the chamber, it automatically knows the exact location of that creature if it can hear the creature.

***Harmonic Absorption.*** When the guardian takes thunder or force damage, it heals for half the damage it would have taken instead of taking that damage.

***Frequency Shift.*** As a bonus action, the guardian can change its resonant frequency. The next creature to hit it with a melee attack before the end of the guardian's next turn must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or take 10 (3d6) thunder damage.

***Magic Resistance.*** The guardian has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

#### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The guardian makes two resonant strike attacks.

***Resonant Strike.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 14 (2d8 + 5) force damage plus 4 (1d8) thunder damage.

***Sonic Pulse (Recharge 5-6).*** The guardian releases a pulse of concentrated sound in a 20-foot radius. Each creature in that area must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw, taking 18 (4d8) thunder damage and being pushed 10 feet away from the guardian on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

***Harmonic Test.*** The guardian can sense a creature's worthiness through sound. One creature the guardian can sense within 30 feet must make a DC 15 Charisma saving throw. On a successful save, the guardian ceases hostilities if the creature demonstrates musical or harmonic ability (such as playing an instrument, singing, or creating a harmonious pattern of sounds). On a failed save, the guardian continues to regard the creature as unworthy and hostile.

#### Reactions

***Sonic Shield.*** When the guardian takes damage, it can create a sound barrier that grants it +5 AC against that attack. If the attack still hits, the guardian takes only half damage from it.

***Harmonic Counter.*** When a creature within 60 feet of the guardian casts a spell with a verbal component, the guardian can disrupt the spellcasting. The spellcaster must succeed on a DC 15 Charisma saving throw or the spell fails and the spell slot is wasted.

#### Tactics

- The guardian tests intruders before attacking, responding positively to harmonious music or sounds
- It uses Sonic Pulse to push enemies away from the central dais
- It targets spellcasters with Harmonic Counter to disrupt their casting
- It positions itself to defend the Stone of Clarity, never leaving the chamber
- If multiple intruders demonstrate harmony, it may cease hostilities entirely

#### Description

The Guardian of Resonance is an ancient elemental entity created to protect the Stone of Clarity. Unlike most guardians who rely on brute force, this being embodies the principle of clarity through perfect harmony. Its body is composed entirely of crystalline structures that constantly vibrate at various frequencies, creating a subtle harmonic background whenever it moves.

The guardian has no traditional sensory organs; instead, it perceives the world through sound and vibration. This makes it perfectly suited to the Chamber of Resonance, where it can detect the slightest disturbance or disharmony. Its form pulsates with inner light that changes color and intensity based on the sounds around it—blue for harmony, red for discord.

Though initially hostile to intruders, the Guardian of Resonance doesn't necessarily seek to destroy them. Instead, it tests their worthiness through their ability to understand and create harmony. Those who demonstrate harmonic talent or insight may find the guardian becomes an ally rather than an adversary.

The guardian was not created by the Frost Architect but is an ancient protector from the original binding ritual. Its connection to the Stone of Clarity makes it impervious to the Frost Architect's corruption, though it remains vigilant against any who would disturb the Stone.
