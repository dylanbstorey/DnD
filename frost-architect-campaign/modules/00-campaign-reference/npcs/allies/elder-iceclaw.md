# Elder Iceclaw
## Defender of the Yeti Council

![Elder Iceclaw](A powerfully-built Yeti with a distinctive right hand that forms natural ice-like claws, battle scars visible through his white fur, and vigilant eyes that constantly scan for threats)

### Basic Information
- **Type**: Yeti Elder
- **Role**: Chief Defender and Military Strategist
- **Alignment**: Neutral Good
- **Location**: Yeti Settlement Central Caverns

### Description
Elder Iceclaw stands as the martial guardian of the Yeti Council. His most distinctive feature is his right hand, which naturally forms into ice-like claws—an unusual mutation considered a blessing among the Yeti clans. Standing eleven feet tall with a broader, more muscular build than most Yetis, his white fur is marked with subtle battle scars from his younger days as a defender of Yeti territories.

As the chief defender of the Yeti people, he oversees the settlement's defenses, trains younger warriors, and plans for threats like the Frost Architect's return. Unlike the other elders, he often wears minimal ceremonial adornments, preferring functionality over symbolism, though he does carry a massive ice-crystalline axe that serves as both weapon and badge of office.

### Personality
- **Demeanor**: Vigilant, direct, and pragmatic
- **Strengths**: Tactical thinking, physical prowess, decisive action
- **Flaws**: Sometimes too quick to suggest martial solutions, impatient with lengthy deliberation
- **Motivation**: Protecting the Yeti people from all threats, preserving their way of life through strength

### Relationships
- **Elder Frostmantle**: Respects his leadership but sometimes chafes at his caution
- **Elder Snowheart**: Values her wisdom while occasionally finding her methods too indirect
- **Dreamspeaker**: Appreciates her visions as tactical intelligence
- **The Party**: Evaluates them primarily on their combat prowess and usefulness in the conflict ahead

### Voice and Mannerisms
- Speaks in a gruff, direct manner with a voice like grinding ice
- Often flexes his ice-claw hand when considering threats or strategies
- Uses metaphors related to hunting, weather, and natural predators
- Stands with a watchful posture, rarely sitting fully at ease

### Roleplaying Notes
- Elder Iceclaw represents the protective strength of the Yeti people
- He respects displays of courage and practical skill
- Though intimidating at first, he shows loyalty and fairness to proven allies
- In moments of danger, he becomes focused and surprisingly swift

### Abilities

#### Keen Smell
Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.

#### Cold Adaptation
Immunity to cold damage and difficult terrain caused by ice or snow.

#### Multiattack
Elder Iceclaw can make three attacks: one with his ice axe and two with his ice claws.

#### Ice Axe
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.  
*Hit:* 4d6 + 6 slashing damage plus 2d6 cold damage.

#### Ice Claws
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
*Hit:* 2d6 + 6 slashing damage plus 1d6 cold damage.

#### Frost Breath (Recharge 5-6)
Elder Iceclaw exhales a 30-foot cone of freezing wind. Each creature in that area must make a DC 18 Constitution saving throw, taking 12d6 cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

#### Battle Command
As a bonus action, Elder Iceclaw can direct an ally he can see within 30 feet of him. That creature gains advantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn.

### History and Background
Elder Iceclaw was born with his distinctive ice-claw mutation, which was seen as a sign he was destined for the warrior path. From youth, he excelled in hunting and combat, quickly becoming one of the settlement's most formidable defenders. His reputation grew after he single-handedly drove off a particularly aggressive frost giant that had been threatening Yeti hunting grounds.

He rose to the Council decades ago after leading a successful defense against a massive winter wight incursion during an unusually harsh winter. Though initially reluctant to take a leadership role, preferring direct action to council meetings, he has grown into his position as the settlement's chief defender.

Elder Iceclaw was among the first to recognize the tactical threat posed by the Frost Architect's return, immediately beginning preparations for defending the settlement and organizing reconnaissance missions. He maintains a network of scouts throughout the mountains who report directly to him about unusual activities.

### Plot Hooks
- Elder Iceclaw might ask the party to train a group of young Yeti defenders in tactics against the Frost Architect's servants
- He could provide the party with specialized weapons or tactical advice for upcoming encounters
- In recognition of proven combat prowess, he might share a secret Yeti fighting technique
- He may reveal the existence of hidden Yeti outposts that could serve as safe havens during the party's journeys
