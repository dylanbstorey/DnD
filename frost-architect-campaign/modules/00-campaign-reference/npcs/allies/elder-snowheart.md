# Elder Snowheart
## Spiritual Guide of the Yeti Council

![Elder Snowheart](A female Yeti with intricate crystal beads woven into her fur, wise eyes that seem to peer beyond the physical realm, and gentle paws that move with practiced grace)

### Basic Information
- **Type**: Yeti Elder
- **Role**: Spiritual Leader and Healer
- **Alignment**: Neutral Good
- **Location**: Yeti Settlement Central Caverns

### Description
Elder Snowheart is the spiritual heart of the Yeti Council, distinguished by her intricate crystal beads woven into her thick white fur. Slightly smaller than Elder Frostmantle at about ten feet tall, she moves with a graceful, flowing quality unusual for her size. Her eyes hold a penetrating quality, as if seeing beyond the physical world into the realm of spirits and possibilities.

As the keeper of Yeti spiritual traditions, she oversees rituals, healing practices, and maintains the connection between the physical world and the ancestral wisdom of the Yeti clans. She carries a staff topped with a large crystal that glows with gentle blue light, which serves as both a focus for her magic and a symbol of her position.

### Personality
- **Demeanor**: Serene, insightful, and compassionate
- **Strengths**: Intuitive understanding, spiritual wisdom, healing knowledge
- **Flaws**: Sometimes cryptic in her guidance, can be overly focused on omens and signs
- **Motivation**: Preserving the spiritual well-being of the Yeti people and maintaining harmony with the mountain's natural rhythms

### Relationships
- **Elder Frostmantle**: Respects his leadership and provides spiritual counsel
- **Elder Iceclaw**: Balances his martial focus with reminders of compassion
- **Dreamspeaker**: Works closely with her, interpreting visions and omens
- **The Party**: Sees them as instruments of fate in the struggle against the Frost Architect

### Voice and Mannerisms
- Speaks in a melodic, gentle voice that carries surprising authority
- Often touches her crystal beads when thinking or speaking of important matters
- Uses metaphors related to light, crystals, echoes, and spiritual harmony
- Frequently pauses to consider the deeper implications of information

### Roleplaying Notes
- Elder Snowheart represents the connection between Yetis and the spiritual world
- She offers guidance that works on multiple levels—practical advice with spiritual undertones
- While gentle by nature, she can be fiercely protective of those in her care
- She recognizes signs and patterns others might miss

### Abilities

#### Keen Smell
Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.

#### Cold Adaptation
Immunity to cold damage and difficult terrain caused by ice or snow.

#### Spiritual Magic
Elder Snowheart can cast the following spells without components:
- *Mass Cure Wounds* (3/day)
- *Divination* (2/day)
- *Commune with Nature* (1/day)
- *Protection from Energy* (2/day)

#### Crystal Attunement
Elder Snowheart can attune to crystals found in the mountains, using them to store magical energy or create protective wards. She can identify magical properties of crystals with a touch.

### History and Background
Elder Snowheart was born during a rare celestial alignment that the Yetis consider a powerful omen. As a young Yeti, she demonstrated unusual sensitivity to the spiritual energies of the mountains, often predicting avalanches and storms before any physical signs appeared. 

She was mentored by the previous spiritual leader, Elder Crystalmind, who recognized her extraordinary gifts. Under this tutelage, Snowheart learned the ancient healing arts of the Yeti people and how to interpret dreams and omens. She joined the Council relatively young after her mentor passed unexpectedly during a journey to a sacred site.

Elder Snowheart was among the first to sense the stirring of the Frost Architect, experiencing troubling dreams and noticing subtle changes in the mountain's spiritual energy long before physical signs appeared. She has been preparing for this conflict by strengthening the spiritual defenses of the settlement and researching ancient texts about the previous binding.

### Plot Hooks
- Elder Snowheart could request the party retrieve a specific crystal from a dangerous location to create a protective ward
- She might share a prophetic vision that provides cryptic clues about upcoming challenges
- In moments of great healing need, she could teach a party member a Yeti healing technique
- She could reveal the spiritual significance of locations the party must visit in their quest
