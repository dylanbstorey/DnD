# Frost-Touched Merchant - Landin
*Medium humanoid (human), neutral*

![Character Portrait](Young man wrapped in extra furs with geometric blue patterns visible on his exposed skin)

**Armor Class:** 10  
**Hit Points:** 8 (2d8 - 1)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 11 (+0) | 10 (+0) | 9 (-1) | 12 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) |

**Skills:** Deception +4, Persuasion +4  
**Senses:** passive Perception 11  
**Languages:** Common  
**Challenge:** 0 (10 XP)

***Frost Infection (Stage 1).*** Landin has been infected with the Frost Architect's cold energy. He feels constantly cold, and geometric blue patterns have formed on his skin where he touched a strange ice formation. Each day, he must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or advance to stage 2 of the infection (reduced Constitution, vulnerability to fire, resistance to cold).

***Merchant Knowledge.*** Landin has advantage on Intelligence checks related to trade goods, market values, and business practices.

#### Actions

***Dagger.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 2 (1d4) piercing damage.

#### Equipment
- Dagger
- Merchant's clothes
- Extra furs and blankets
- Small book of trade records
- Pouch with 12 gold pieces
- Small silver pendant of a mountain goat (family heirloom)

#### Personality
Landin is young and ambitious, on his first major trading expedition when disaster struck. Now he's frightened of the strange patterns spreading on his skin but tries to hide his condition from others. He's becoming increasingly cold-natured and finds himself drawn to colder areas without realizing why. He's desperate for help but afraid of being abandoned if the full extent of his condition is known.

#### Information He Knows
- He touched a strange ice formation with perfect geometric patterns
- Since then, he's felt constantly cold and the patterns have been spreading
- He's started having dreams of "a tall figure with a crown of ice" that calls to him
- He can sometimes sense directions to sources of intense cold, like a pull in his mind
- He knows the sphere crashed "on the north side of Crystal Ridge, in a bowl-shaped valley"
