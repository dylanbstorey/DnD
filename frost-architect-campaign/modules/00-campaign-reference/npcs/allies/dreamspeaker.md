# Dreamspeaker
## Ancient Yeti Seer

![Dreamspeaker](An elderly Yeti with fur so white it seems almost translucent, eyes clouded with age yet glowing with inner knowing, hunched slightly with a gnarled staff of ancient wood)

### Basic Information
- **Type**: Yeti Elder Seer
- **Role**: Keeper of Ancient Memories and Visions
- **Alignment**: Neutral Good
- **Location**: Yeti Settlement Central Caverns

### Description
Dreamspeaker is the oldest living Yeti, her exact age unknown but rumored to extend well beyond the normal Yeti lifespan. Smaller than other elders due to her hunched posture and advanced age, standing about nine feet tall, her fur has turned so white it appears almost translucent in certain lights. Her eyes, though clouded with age, seem to glow with an inner light when she speaks of visions or ancient memories.

As the keeper of the oldest memories and receiver of prophetic dreams, Dreamspeaker serves as an advisor to the Council rather than a formal member. She carries a gnarled staff of ancient wood—unusual in Yeti culture where ice and stone are more common materials—said to have come from a sacred tree that grew during the first binding of the Frost Architect.

### Personality
- **Demeanor**: Mysterious, ancient, and occasionally cryptic
- **Strengths**: Visionary insight, vast historical knowledge, connection to ancient magic
- **Flaws**: Often speaks in riddles, can become lost in memories or visions
- **Motivation**: Ensuring the accumulated wisdom of generations is not lost, guiding the present with knowledge from the past

### Relationships
- **Elder Frostmantle**: Serves as his most trusted advisor on matters of ancient lore
- **Elder Snowheart**: Shares visions and spiritual insights, mentor-like relationship
- **Elder Iceclaw**: Provides historical context for his defensive strategies
- **The Party**: Sees them as fulfilling an ancient prophecy, pieces moving in a pattern set long ago

### Voice and Mannerisms
- Speaks in a wispy, ethereal voice that sometimes shifts between present awareness and ancient memory
- Often closes her eyes when recalling important information, as if viewing it internally
- Uses archaic turns of phrase and references events from centuries past as if they happened recently
- Occasionally pauses mid-sentence as new visions or memories surface

### Roleplaying Notes
- Dreamspeaker embodies the living memory of the Yeti people
- She experiences time differently than others, sometimes conflating past, present, and possible futures
- Though physically frail, her knowledge makes her one of the most powerful Yetis
- In rare moments of complete clarity, her insights are startlingly precise and practical

### Abilities

#### Keen Smell
Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.

#### Cold Adaptation
Immunity to cold damage and difficult terrain caused by ice or snow.

#### Ancient Magic
Dreamspeaker can cast the following spells without components:
- *Legend Lore* (3/day)
- *Foresight* (1/day)
- *Dream* (at will)
- *Scrying* (3/day)
- *Contact Other Plane* (1/day)

#### Memory Keeper
Dreamspeaker has perfect recall of any historical information she has ever learned. She has advantage on all Intelligence checks related to history, especially regarding the Frost Architect and the original binding ritual.

#### Prophetic Visions
Once per day, Dreamspeaker can enter a trance and receive visions relevant to current events. These visions are not always literal and may require interpretation, but always contain valuable truth.

### History and Background
Dreamspeaker's early life is largely unknown, as it occurred so long ago that few records remain. What is known is that she was born with the rare gift of prophetic dreams and perfect memory, marking her for the path of a seer from childhood. She has served as advisor to at least five generations of Yeti Councils.

Most significantly, Dreamspeaker is the last living Yeti with any direct connection to the original binding of the Frost Architect. While too young to have participated directly, she was apprenticed to a seer who did, and through a special ritual, received many of that elder's memories before their passing. This makes her knowledge of the Seven Stones of Binding and the original ritual uniquely valuable.

Throughout her long life, she has predicted numerous significant events—from natural disasters to conflicts with other beings—allowing the Yeti settlements to prepare accordingly. Her dreams of the Frost Architect's stirring began decades before any physical signs appeared, giving the Yeti people precious time to gather lore and prepare.

### Plot Hooks
- Dreamspeaker might call upon the party after receiving a particularly urgent vision about their quest
- She could reveal critical details about the original binding ritual as they become relevant
- In moments of need, she might perform a ritual allowing a party member to temporarily access ancestral Yeti memories
- She could provide cryptic but ultimately vital warnings about unexpected dangers in specific locations
