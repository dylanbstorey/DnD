# Mistwalker
## Healer of Snowhaven

![Mistwalker](A female Yeti with slightly silver-tinged fur and gentle eyes, hands that glow with healing energy, wearing simple adornments of medicinal herbs and healing crystals)

### Basic Information
- **Type**: Yeti Healer
- **Role**: Settlement Healer and Plant Knowledge Keeper
- **Alignment**: Neutral Good
- **Location**: Originally Snowhaven, now refugee at Yeti Council Settlement

### Description
Mistwalker is a middle-aged female Yeti healer who served as the primary caretaker for the small settlement of Snowhaven. Slightly smaller than the average Yeti at about nine feet tall, her fur has a distinctive silver tinge particularly around her healing hands. Her eyes reflect gentleness and patience cultivated through years of healing practice.

Unlike most Yetis who focus on ice and cold magic, Mistwalker specializes in the healing properties of mountain plants and minerals. She wears simple adornments—dried herbs and small crystals woven into her fur—that serve both decorative and practical purposes in her healing work. She carries a satchel of healing supplies that she managed to save when fleeing Snowhaven.

### Personality
- **Demeanor**: Gentle, patient, and observant
- **Strengths**: Healing knowledge, selfless dedication, calm under pressure
- **Flaws**: Sometimes places others' needs above her own safety, stubborn about her duties
- **Motivation**: Preserving life and alleviating suffering, maintaining the healing traditions of her people

### Relationships
- **The Yeti Council**: Respects their leadership but focused primarily on caring for Snowhaven refugees
- **Elder Snowheart**: Special connection due to shared healing knowledge
- **The Party**: Deep gratitude for their rescue, willing to share healing knowledge
- **Snowhaven Refugees**: Feels responsible for their wellbeing

### Voice and Mannerisms
- Speaks softly but clearly, her voice carrying a soothing quality
- Often gestures with her hands when explaining medicinal properties
- Tends to lean slightly forward when listening to symptoms or concerns
- Hums quietly while preparing remedies or tending to patients

### Roleplaying Notes
- Mistwalker embodies the healing traditions of the Yeti people
- She prioritizes care for the vulnerable over politics or grand strategies
- Though gentle by nature, she becomes firmly resolute when a patient's wellbeing is threatened
- Her knowledge of mountain flora offers unique insights beyond typical Yeti ice magic

### Abilities

#### Keen Smell
Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.

#### Cold Adaptation
Immunity to cold damage and difficult terrain caused by ice or snow.

#### Master Healer
Mistwalker can use her action to touch a creature and restore 4d8 + 4 hit points to it. Additionally, the touch cures one disease or neutralizes one poison affecting the creature. She can use this ability three times, regaining all expended uses after a long rest.

#### Herbal Remedies
Mistwalker carries various herbal preparations that she can apply as a bonus action:
- *Pain-Relief Salve*: Target gains 2d4 temporary hit points and advantage on Constitution saving throws for 1 hour
- *Warming Poultice*: Target gains resistance to cold damage for 1 hour
- *Clarity Tincture*: Target can end one effect causing the charmed or frightened condition on itself
- *Revitalizing Tea*: Target reduces its exhaustion level by one

#### Winter Bloom
Once per day, Mistwalker can cause plants to rapidly grow in a 20-foot radius around her, even in frozen or inhospitable environments. These plants last for 8 hours and can provide food, basic medicinal components, or difficult terrain for enemies.

### History and Background
Mistwalker was born in Snowhaven during a rare summer snowstorm that the elders interpreted as a sign she would walk between the seasons—hence her name. From childhood, she showed an unusual affinity for plants, somehow coaxing them to grow even in the harshest winter conditions.

She trained under the previous healer of Snowhaven, Elder Frostbloom, who taught her both traditional Yeti healing magic and the more specialized knowledge of medicinal plants that grow in the mountains. When Frostbloom passed, Mistwalker took on the role of settlement healer despite being relatively young for the position.

When the strange sphere fell near Snowhaven and began emanating unnatural cold, Mistwalker recognized the danger immediately. She helped organize the evacuation of most residents but chose to remain behind to maintain the healing shrine, believing its magic might slow the sphere's influence. This decision led to her becoming trapped by winter wights until the party rescued her.

Now safe at the main Yeti settlement, Mistwalker divides her time between caring for the refugees from Snowhaven and assisting Elder Snowheart with preparations for the conflicts to come. She continues to study the effects of the unnatural cold on mountain flora, seeking new remedies that might counteract its influence.
