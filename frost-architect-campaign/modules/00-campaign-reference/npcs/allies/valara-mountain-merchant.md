# Mountain Merchant Leader - Valara
*Medium humanoid (human), lawful neutral*

![Character Portrait](Weather-beaten woman with a thick fur hat and determined expression)

**Armor Class:** 12 (leather armor)  
**Hit Points:** 16 (3d8 + 3)  
**Speed:** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 13 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 15 (+2) | 14 (+2) |

**Skills:** Animal Handling +4, Insight +4, Perception +4, Persuasion +4, Survival +6  
**Tools:** Navigator's tools +4, Vehicles (land) +3  
**Senses:** passive Perception 14  
**Languages:** Common, Dwarvish, Giant (trading dialect)  
**Challenge:** 1/4 (50 XP)

***Mountain Guide.*** Valara has advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks made in mountain terrain.

***Merchant Networks.*** Valara has contacts in most major settlements in the region and can provide information about local events, routes, and dangers.

#### Actions

***Shortsword.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) piercing damage.

***Light Crossbow.*** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d8 + 1) piercing damage.

#### Equipment
- Leather armor
- Shortsword
- Light crossbow with 20 bolts
- Merchant's pack (includes ledger, ink, pen, 10 sheets of parchment, sealing wax, string, scales)
- Winter clothing
- Pouch with 25 gold pieces
- Small map of mountain trading routes

#### Personality
Valara is a no-nonsense leader who has been guiding merchant caravans through the mountains for over fifteen years. She's seen her share of unusual things, but the recent cold phenomena have genuinely frightened her. She's practical, direct, and suspicious of strangers, but she'll warm up to anyone who shows their worth or helps her group.

#### Information She Knows
- Trade routes to the north have become impassable due to sudden, intense cold
- Several settlements have been abandoned in the past month
- She's seen strange metal objects falling from the sky "like shooting stars that land instead of burning up"
- Her group encountered ice creatures unlike any she's seen before three days ago
- One of her merchants, Landin, touched a strange ice formation and now has unusual frostbite patterns on his skin
