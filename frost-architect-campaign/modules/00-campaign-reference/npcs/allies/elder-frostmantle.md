# Elder Frostmantle
## Leader of the Yeti Council

![Elder Frostmantle](A large, imposing Yeti with silvered fur and wise, ancient eyes that reflect years of leadership)

### Basic Information
- **Type**: Yeti Elder
- **Role**: Leader of the Yeti Council
- **Alignment**: Lawful Good
- **Location**: Yeti Settlement Central Caverns

### Description
Elder Frostmantle is the central figure on the Yeti Council, distinguished by his silvered fur that denotes his advanced age and wisdom. He stands nearly twelve feet tall, his imposing stature balanced by the gentle, thoughtful expression he typically wears. His eyes hold the wisdom of many seasons, and he speaks with measured calm even in dire situations.

As the leader of the Council, he is respected for his balanced judgment and ability to consider multiple perspectives before making decisions. He wears simple but elegant crystal beads that denote his status, and carries a staff of carved ice that serves as both a walking aid and a symbol of authority.

### Personality
- **Demeanor**: Calm, measured, and thoughtful
- **Strengths**: Diplomatic leadership, strategic thinking, balancing tradition with practicality
- **Flaws**: Sometimes hesitant when quick action is needed, overly concerned with consensus
- **Motivation**: Ensuring the survival and prosperity of all Yeti clans

### Relationships
- **Elder Snowheart**: Respects her insight and spiritual wisdom
- **Elder Iceclaw**: Values his martial perspective but sometimes finds him too aggressive
- **Dreamspeaker**: Deeply respects her connection to ancestral wisdom
- **The Party**: Sees them as valuable allies who have proven their worth

### Voice and Mannerisms
- Speaks in a deep, resonant voice that carries authority without intimidation
- Often places his large paws together in thought before speaking
- Uses metaphors related to balance, mountains, and the changing seasons
- Addresses younger Yetis and outsiders with respectful patience

### Roleplaying Notes
- Elder Frostmantle embodies calm leadership during crisis
- He weighs all options carefully before committing to action
- He represents the diplomatic face of Yeti society to outsiders
- Though peaceful by nature, he becomes formidable when his people are threatened

### Abilities

#### Keen Smell
Advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.

#### Cold Adaptation
Immunity to cold damage and difficult terrain caused by ice or snow.

#### Frost Magic
Elder Frostmantle can cast the following spells without components:
- *Cone of Cold* (3/day)
- *Wall of Ice* (2/day)
- *Control Weather* (1/day, only to affect snow and cold conditions)
- *Mass Healing Word* (2/day)

#### Mountain Wisdom
Elder Frostmantle has advantage on Wisdom and Intelligence checks related to the mountains, winter survival, and Yeti history.

### History and Background
Elder Frostmantle has led the Yeti Council for over fifty winters, one of the longest leadership tenures in recent memory. Born during a particularly harsh winter, he showed wisdom beyond his years from an early age. He was mentored by the previous council leader, Elder Whiteridge, who recognized his balanced temperament and foresight.

During his leadership, Frostmantle has navigated several crises, including territorial disputes with other mountain creatures, uncommonly harsh winters, and occasional human encroachment. His greatest achievement was negotiating peace with the neighboring dwarven clans after decades of mutual suspicion, establishing trade routes that benefited both peoples.

The current crisis with the Frost Architect weighs heavily on him, as he recognizes it as potentially the gravest threat of his lifetime. He carries the stories of the previous binding ritual in his memory, passed down through generations of elders, and understands better than most what failure might mean for all who dwell in the mountains.

### Plot Hooks
- Elder Frostmantle might ask the party to serve as diplomats to other settlements that haven't yet heard about the Frost Architect's return
- He could reveal additional information about the Stones of Binding as the party earns his deeper trust
- In times of great need, he might grant the party access to ancient Yeti artifacts kept in the settlement's vault
