# The Frost Architect
*Huge elemental, chaotic evil*

![Character Portrait](Towering entity composed of living ice and shadow with a crown of crystalline spikes and glowing blue eyes)

**Armor Class:** 19 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points:** 225 (18d12 + 108)  
**Speed:** 40 ft., burrow through ice 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|

**Saving Throws:** Str +13, Con +12, Wis +10, Cha +12  
**Skills:** Arcana +11, History +11, Insight +10, Intimidation +12, Perception +10  
**Damage Resistances:** bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks  
**Damage Immunities:** cold, poison

**Languages:** Common, Dwarvish, Giant, Primordial, telepathy 120 ft.  
**Challenge Rating:** 23 (50,000 XP)

### Special Traits

***Legendary Resistance (3/Day).*** If the Frost Architect fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

***Magic Resistance.*** The Frost Architect has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Regeneration.*** The Frost Architect regains 20 hit points at the start of its turn if it has at least 1 hit point and is in contact with ice or snow. If the Frost Architect takes fire damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of its next turn.

***Siege Monster.*** The Frost Architect deals double damage to objects and structures.

***Primordial Cold.*** The Frost Architect radiates intense cold. At the start of each of the Frost Architect's turns, each creature within 20 feet of it takes 10 (3d6) cold damage. Creatures that touch the Frost Architect or hit it with melee attacks take 14 (4d6) cold damage.

***Frozen Domain.*** The ground in a 60-foot radius around the Frost Architect is treated as difficult terrain for creatures without ice walk. Water in this area freezes instantly, and fires are extinguished unless they are magical in nature.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The Frost Architect makes three attacks: two with its Glacial Claws and one with its Winter's Breath.

***Glacial Claws.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +13 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. *Hit:* 19 (2d10 + 8) slashing damage plus 10 (3d6) cold damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution saving throw or have its speed reduced by 10 feet for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

***Winter's Breath (Recharge 5-6).*** The Frost Architect exhales a blast of freezing wind in a 60-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 20 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 49 (14d6) cold damage and is restrained by ice until the end of its next turn. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn't restrained.

***Blizzard (1/Day).*** The Frost Architect magically creates a blizzard in a 100-foot radius centered on itself. The blizzard spreads around corners. Each creature in the area when the blizzard appears must make a DC 20 Constitution saving throw or take 35 (10d6) cold damage and become blinded for 1 minute. The area becomes heavily obscured, and exposed flames in the area are extinguished. The blizzard lasts for 10 minutes, although the Frost Architect can use a bonus action to end it early.

***Summon Ice Servants (1/Day).*** The Frost Architect magically summons 2d4 Ice Scouts, 1d4 Winter Wights, or 1 Frost Colossus. The summoned creatures appear in unoccupied spaces within 60 feet of the Frost Architect, act as its allies, and can't summon other creatures. They remain for 1 hour, until the Frost Architect dies, or until it dismisses them as an action.

***Teleport (1/Day).*** The Frost Architect magically teleports itself and up to five willing creatures it can see within 30 feet of it, along with any equipment they are wearing or carrying, to a location the Frost Architect is familiar with, up to 1 mile away.

### Legendary Actions

The Frost Architect can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The Frost Architect regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

***Glacial Spike.*** The Frost Architect creates a spike of ice that erupts from the ground at a point it can see within 60 feet. Any creature in that space must make a DC 20 Dexterity saving throw, taking 15 (3d10) piercing damage and 10 (3d6) cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

***Frost Step.*** The Frost Architect moves up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

***Cold Snap (Costs 2 Actions).*** The Frost Architect intensifies the cold in a 30-foot radius. Each creature in that area must make a DC 20 Constitution saving throw, taking 21 (6d6) cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. Until the end of the Frost Architect's next turn, creatures that start their turn in the affected area have their speed reduced by half.

***Winter's Dominion (Costs 3 Actions).*** The Frost Architect exerts its control over cold and ice. It can target one of the following: a creature made of ice or cold energy, a creature affected by a cold-based spell or effect, or an object made of ice or affected by a cold-based spell or effect. If the target is a creature, it must make a DC 20 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the Frost Architect controls the creature until the end of its next turn, forcing it to take an action of the Frost Architect's choice on its next turn. On a successful save, the target is immune to Winter's Dominion for 24 hours. If the target is an object, the Frost Architect can reshape it, animate it, or destroy it.

### Description

The Frost Architect is an ancient entity of primordial cold who once ruled the mountain region during an era known as the Time of Long Winter. Standing nearly 20 feet tall, its body is composed of living ice and shadow with a crown of crystalline spikes adorning its head. Its eyes glow with an inner blue light that seems to pierce through any defense or deception.

This powerful being commands winter wights, ice constructs, and all manner of frost creatures, having the ability to corrupt normal creatures and even humanoids with its cold energy. The Frost Architect seeks to transform the world into a domain of endless winter, where it would reign supreme as the only true power.

Currently weakened from its long imprisonment, the Frost Architect is regaining its strength through a network of magical spheres that gather cold energy. As more spheres activate, its powers grow stronger, and its influence spreads further across the mountains.

The Frost Architect is not merely destructive—it is calculating and patient, willing to spend centuries executing its plans. It views warmth and life as aberrations in what should be a perfect, crystalline world of ice and silence.

### Tactics

- The Frost Architect prefers to fight in areas already affected by extreme cold or covered in ice and snow
- It uses Winter's Breath to damage multiple opponents and potentially restrain them
- Against powerful opponents, it will summon servants to distract or flank them
- When damaged, it will retreat to heal using its Regeneration ability
- It uses its Legendary Actions strategically, especially Winter's Dominion to turn enemies' allies against them
- If significantly injured, it will use Teleport to escape and recover
