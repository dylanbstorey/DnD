# Frost Sphere Cold Damage Mechanics

## Overview
The Frost Sphere emits magical cold in concentric zones of increasing intensity. Unlike natural cold that dissipates with distance, the Frost Sphere's unnatural cold works differently, becoming more dangerous as you get closer to its core.

## Damage Zones

### Outer Zone (100ft radius)
- **Effects:** 1d6 cold damage per minute
- **Appearance:** Air visibly distorts like a heat mirage, but emanating cold instead
- **Natural Environment:** Small ice formations begin to appear, vegetation becomes brittle and frost-covered
- **Protection Required:** Winter clothing provides no protection; magical protection like the Frost Resistance Elixir is necessary

### Inner Zone (50ft radius)
- **Effects:** 2d6 cold damage per minute 
- **Appearance:** Breath freezes instantly, creating small ice crystals that fall to the ground
- **Natural Environment:** Advanced ice formations grow rapidly, taking on unnatural geometric patterns
- **Protection Required:** The Frost Resistance Elixir provides complete protection; without it, characters must make Constitution saving throws (DC 13) each minute or gain a level of exhaustion

## Why Cold Intensifies Closer to the Sphere

Unlike normal cold that would radiate outward and gradually lessen with distance, the Frost Sphere actively draws heat energy inward, creating a reverse gradient where:

1. **Heat Absorption:** The sphere absorbs ambient heat energy from the surroundings
2. **Energy Conversion:** This stolen heat is converted into magical cold energy
3. **Dimensional Manipulation:** The sphere creates a thermodynamic anomaly where heat flows toward the sphere (the opposite of normal physics)

This creates the counter-intuitive effect where the closer you get to the sphere, the more intense the cold becomes, as the heat extraction process is more efficient at close range.

## Protection Methods

### Frost Resistance Elixir
- **Effect:** Complete immunity to both damage zones
- **Duration:** 24 hours after consumption
- **Appearance:** When consumed, the user's skin briefly glows with a faint blue light before returning to normal
- **Limitations:** Only three vials provided by the Yeti Council

### Alternative Protection
- **Magical Items:** Items with resistance to cold damage reduce damage by half
- **Spells:** *Protection from Energy (Cold)* provides resistance
- **Rotation Strategy:** Characters with no protection can move in and out of the damage zones, limiting exposure

## Strategic Considerations

1. **Conservation of Resources:** Save the elixirs for those who need to operate near the sphere for extended periods
2. **Time Management:** Plan activities within the damage zones carefully to minimize exposure
3. **Buddy System:** Never explore alone in case someone succumbs to the cold effects
4. **Warning Signs:** Watch for frost forming on equipment and exposed skin, indicating worsening conditions

## Important Notes

The Frost Sphere's cold energy is not just physically damaging but has a supernatural component—prolonged exposure risks more than just health effects. Characters may experience hallucinations, memory lapses, or in extreme cases, begin developing frost patterns on their skin similar to those seen on Winter Wights.

This is not normal cold—it is a manifestation of the Frost Architect's power, designed to transform living beings and prepare the environment for his return.
